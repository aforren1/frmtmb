into frmtmb 0.69.0. This is the record of what was done, how it was
verified and what is left. Each lane's `dev/<lane>-findings.md` has its
validation, numbers and scripts, and `dev/reviews/2026-10-07-<lane>.md`
has its adversarial review.

(`dev/reviews/2026-10-07-release.md`, B1 and m1 to m7). Nothing is
committed.

## How the release tree was built

The four lanes were combined in `../frmtmb-wt-release` (branch
`wt-release` at `4f5ea39f`), in the order ciharden, surface, setier,
optima. Nothing was committed there, and no git write was made: the
base blobs are read with `git show`, and `git merge-file` works on
files, not on the index.

- `dev/rel069-merge.sh` merges every lane in one rerunnable pass, in
  the manner of `dev/rel068-nanse-merge.sh`: it restores each of the
  97 tracked files any lane changed to its `4f5ea39f` blob, merges the
  lanes in order with `git merge-file` against that blob (line endings
  normalized to LF first), copies the lanes' untracked files without
  overwriting, then runs the consolidation's edits
  (`dev/rel069-resolve.R`) and the NEWS merge
  (`dev/rel069-news-merge.R`), and stops if a conflict marker is left.
  `man/*.Rd`, `NAMESPACE` and frmtmb.sample's generated
  `helper-brms-suite.R` are left out; roxygen writes them. Log
  `dev/rel069-log/merge-1.txt`. Rerunning it after the ledger
  regeneration (below) would restore the lanes' generated
  `test-brms-suite-*.R` files, so the ledger steps follow it.
- Untracked lane files copied, 0 clashes: 43 from ciharden, 72 from
  surface (7 generated Rd files skipped), 108 from setier, 90 from
  optima. Stan caches and logs are gitignored and were not copied.
- `dev/stan-cache` was seeded by the coordinator with 228 programs and
  `dev/brms-suite/` with brms 2.23.0's tarball and tree. The OpenBLAS
  0.3.32 build of R was made with `dev/ciharden-openblas.sh 0.3.32
  lapack` from lane ciharden's downloaded release (log
  `dev/rel069-log/openblas-build.txt`: Rblas 76 symbols to OpenBLAS,
  Rlapack 617).
- `roxygen2::roxygenise()` on core, install into
  `C:/Users/adf44/source/r/rellib-r7` (new; `rellib-r6` keeps the
  0.68.1 build), with drmTMB 0.7.0 copied from `rellib-r6`, then each
  extension roxygenised against it and installed in dependency order
  (`dev/rel069-install.sh`, log `dev/rel069-log/install-1.txt`). A
  second roxygen pass of all eight packages changed none of the 216
  generated files (md5, `dev/rel069-log/rox-md5-1.txt` and `-2.txt`).
  The generated files are the union of the lanes': each one a single
  lane changed equals that lane's, and `NAMESPACE` and
  `man/frmtmb_control.Rd`, which two lanes changed, equal the
  three-way merge of the lanes' generated copies.
  `extensions/frmtmb.sample/man/pp_mixture.Rd` is deleted (lane
  surface moved the topic to core).
- The release harness reads `rellib-r7`: `dev/release/run-tests.R`,
  `run-check.ps1`, `run-docs.ps1`, `dev/warnleak-scan.R`, the defaults
  of `dev/brmsport-run.R` and `dev/brmsport-guards.R`, and the
  `ROUND_BASE` default of `dev/ciharden-run1.R` (the runner of
  `dev/ciharden-scan.sh`).
- `pkgdown::check_pkgdown()` passes for all eight packages
  (`dev/rel069-pkgdown.R`, `dev/rel069-log/pkgdown.txt`) after the
  `_pkgdown.yml` repair below; before it, core's failed.

## 6. What was not done, and why

Rows left filed, all this lane's topic:

- `brmsfit-methods:314`: `fitted(sample_new_levels = "old_levels")`.
  `fitted()` is a Wald summary; it takes no draws of the group effects
  for a seen level to lend. `predict()` does, and takes it.
- `brmsfit-methods:391`, `:396`: `plot(hypothesis, plot = FALSE,
  ignore_prior = )`. brms returns ggplot objects; frmtmb draws with base
  graphics and does not depend on ggplot2. A decision for the user, not
  a spelling.
- `brmsfit-methods:595`: `nsamples(incl_warmup = TRUE)`; the warmup is
  discarded, a recorded gap.
- `brmsfit-methods:747`: `predict(newdata = )` with NA responses under
  `cov = FALSE`. brms fills them with predicted draws and runs the
  recursion over those; `simulate(newdata = )` does that here and
  `predict()` does not yet.
- `brmsfit-methods:995`: `parnames()` on a fit. frmtmb.sample defines
  the generic for draws; giving core a method moves the generic between
  the two packages' owner tables (`R/generic-owners.R`,
  `test-generic-collision.R` in both packages).

  by level order; doing it here needs the coding stored with the fit and
  applied on every refit (`influence()`, `frm_bootstrap()`) and every
  response read from newdata, or a subset holding one of the two values
  is coded wrong in silence.

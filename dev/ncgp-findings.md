## 1. Result

`frm()` holds an exact `gp()` as z. The block's `b` is a standard normal, brms's
`zgp`, and the field is `sdgp * L(lscale) * z`, with `L` the Cholesky factor of
the correlation plus `1e-12 I`. The centered route is gone, with no option.

- The floor moved three orders, to sigma near `1e-6 sdgp`. On the closed-form table
  of 24 rows (section 3), `frm()` reaches the marginal's maximum on all 24 to at most
  1.3e-3 nats (section 0). rellib-r16 is short by up to 82 nats and reads sigma 3
  orders low on the same table.
- `frm_sample()`'s columns on an exact field were wrong in this lane until the last
  fix round, and every suite passed (item 3 below). Now, with the factor farthest-first,
  its sigma at true 1e-2 reads brms's own program's within 1 percent at q05, q50 and
  q95, Rhat at most 1.002, against brms's 1.013 (section 4).
- brms's own program, unedited, reads `frm()`'s estimate within 1e-6 `|ours|` on 18
  of 19 spellings at n = 60, and the worst row is 1.76 times it (section 4). On
  better-conditioned kernels that is the whole story; on the survey's five pairs and
  on row 10a of test-brms-likelihood.R, with longer length scales and 80 to 200
  rows, it is 1.1 to 170 times the tolerance, at most 0.016 nats (section 11). The
  rewritten program, an attribution only, reads 1e-14 to 3e-8 on every one, so

- The fit does not depend on y's units or x's (section 3, rows `y*1000`,
  `y/1000`, `x*1000`, and test-gp-noncentered.R).
- Cost: section 0, about 2, 1.8, 3 to 4 and 4 times.

The inner Hessian `I + sd^2 L' W L` is dense in z, and TMB tapes it one reverse sweep
per column, so n^3 work goes into nodes. The compressed form replays one sweep;
the atomic product takes one node per sweep. `sdreport()`'s own retape

lazily, so the scope forces it; before that, the setting did nothing on a real fit,
and the first perf run read it (H 48707 nodes against 5496).

What remains is the outer gradient: a reverse sweep per column through the atomic
product, 8 to 12 times the centered gradient at 500 positions, against 2.6 to 3 for
the value. I found no way through TMB's tape to take `tr(H^-1 dH)` as the one
matrix product it is.

**Recommendation on the closed form.** On a gaussian response with identity link
the field integrates to `y ~ N(X b, sd^2 Z K Z' + Sigma)`: no latent, no floor,
1.4 s against 84 s at 500 positions on hand tapes, and faster than the centered
route it replaces (6.3 s). Against the z route that is a factor of 60 at the cap.
It is a second route: `b` is absent from the tape, so `z` must be recovered after the
fit, and `sdreport()`'s joint precision and `spHess` do not exist on it, and every
reader in section 7 reads those. I did not build it. A later round could, as a
marginal tape for the optimizer with z and the joint computed once from the z tape's
gradient at the optimum, so that readers stay on one path. It would serve only
gaussian-identity fits with no other random effect on the row's Z and no mixture. It
is worth it if 500-position gaussian fits are common. The 6.6 to 8.8 times here are
not a failure at any size: memory is lower and the cap of 500 positions stands.
(Those two figures are the sorted factor's; the final build's are in section 1.)

The false convergence the review found at true sigma 3e-4 (ll 311.7289 against
311.7284): that row now reads conv 0 and no warning (section 11). At 1e-4 also conv 0.
**That row is not the rule.** On 15 other designs at the same noise (`dev/ncgp-fcwarn.R`,
n 40, 100, 200, seeds 1 to 5) `nlminb` stops at false convergence on 8, in either factor
order (8 sorted, 7 farthest-first, of 18 in `dev/ncgp-perm3.R`). The warning does not
mislead: each says a Newton step from the stop gains at most 2.06e-6 in log-likelihood,

**The user may want that warning silent when the headroom is within `grad_tol`.** I did
not change it: it is every model's message, not this lane's.

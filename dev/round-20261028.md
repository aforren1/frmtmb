## Release fixes

`dev/rel077-release-fix.R`, sourced by the resolver, and the edits of
fact in `dev/rel077-news-split.R`. Test blocks it appends are
`dev/rel077-fix-*-test.R`; each was seen to fail as section "The
semantic overlaps" lists.

- **S1 (sigmainn's review, short check), `insight::get_sigma()`.** The
  lane's `.get_sigma.frmtmb_fit` returned `sigma()`, so on a plain
  `bf(y ~ x, sigma ~ x2)` fit, where `sigma()` is `NA`, insight's
  fallback (1.7285282 on rellib-r14, seed 3) became `NA`. It is now
  `autocor_check_fit(x); NextMethod()`: a saved 0.76.0 fit is refused
  and every other fit gets insight's default. Test in
  `test-autocor-sigma.R` ("insight::get_sigma() keeps insight's
  default on a current fit", 3 assertions): `get_sigma()` equals
  `sqrt(|get_deviance()| / get_df(type = "residual"))` and is finite;
  on a fit without `sigma ~ x` it is `sigma()`. The NEWS line says so
  (edit of fact).
- **R1 (reslot's review, short check of fix round 2), a coerced
  fit-route table.** `as.data.frame()` drops a `default_prior()`
  table's route, so `frm_sample(prior = as.brmsprior(as.data.frame(pe)))`
  sampled `(flat)` on the intercept, sigma, sd and cor in silence.
  Keeping the mark through `as.data.frame()` is not clean: core's
  `test-get-prior-route.R` pins that the coerced table is identical to
  a hand-written data frame. So `as.brmsprior()` of a plain data frame
  that holds frmtmb's printed `"(flat)"` on a `source = "default"` row
  (brms's own tables store `""`) marks the priorlist `"table"`; the mark
  travels through `+`, `c()` and a column edit of a priorlist as
  `"fit"` does (`with_prior_route()`, `combined_prior_route()`), and
  frm_sample()
  refuses such a prior where its `(flat)` rows replace one of brms's
  proper defaults (`table_flat_lost()`), naming them. A coerced
  route = "sample" table never replaces one and resolves as the table
  does. The plain-data-frame refusal adds: "A default_prior() table is
  passed as it is, since as.data.frame() drops its route; for
  frmtmb.sample::frm_sample(), take it with route = "sample"". Test in
  frmtmb.sample's `test-flat-prior.R` ("as.data.frame() drops the
  route, and the (flat) rows are refused", 10 assertions), and reslot's
  "read as written" arm now builds its hand table without a `source`
  column. Cost, filed: a coerced sample-route table edited to `""` on a
  proper-default slot is refused too. CORRECTED after the release
  review (finding 1): R1 left a wider gap than this item claimed. A
  `frmtmb_prior_rows` table itself lost its route under `subset()` and
  a column selection (`[.data.frame` drops the attribute when the call
  gives `j`), and was then read as written, `(flat)` in place of brms's
  defaults; only a row selection, `head()`, `rbind()`, `unique()` and
  `$<-` kept it. Release fix F1 ("After the release review") closes it,
  widens the mark to any printed `(flat)`, and changes the hand-built
  arm to brms's `""` spelling.

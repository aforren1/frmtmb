# Lane fams: hurdle_negbinomial() and zero_one_inflated_beta()

Lane fams, 2026-09-25, base commit 91cbf355 (frmtmb 0.63.0). Both brms
families now exist, fit, and reach every post-fit method their
siblings reach (table below). They are built on `hurdle_poisson()`,
`zero_inflated_beta()` and `negbinomial()`, and registered wherever
those are.

## What changed

### The two families (`R/families.R`)

- `fam_hurdle_negbinomial()` and the exported `hurdle_negbinomial(link
  = "log", link_shape = "log", link_hu = "logit")`. dpars `mu`, `shape`,
  `hu`. `P(Y = 0) = hu`; for `y > 0`,
  `(1 - hu) NB(y | mu, shape) / (1 - NB(0 | mu, shape))`.
  - One density expression for every mean link:
    `RTMB::dnbinom_robust(y, log mu, 2 log mu - log shape)`, with
    `log mu` from `robust_logmu()` on the tape under the log link and
    `log(mu)` otherwise. The first draft kept the siblings' off-tape
    branch through `dnbinom2(y, mu, mu + mu^2 / shape)`; at
    `mu = exp(-25)` that forms `var - mu` by subtraction and lost the
    density to 5.9e-8 relative (measured, first run of
    `dev/fams-validate.R`, section 1), so it was replaced.
  - `log P(0) = -shape * logspace_add(0, log mu - log shape)`, and the
    truncation normalizer is `RTMB::logspace_sub(0, log P(0))`, which
    switches to `expm1()` as `P(0)` tends to one. brms's own
    `log1m((shape / (mu + shape))^shape)` has a relative error of
    1.53e-7 at `mu = exp(-25)`; the package form is within 5.7e-16 of
    `pnbinom(0, lower.tail = FALSE, log.p = TRUE)` there.
  - `hu` goes through `dpar_log_complement()`, `shape` through
    `dpar_log()`.
  - `post$mean_fn` is brms's `posterior_epred_hurdle_negbinomial()`,
    with `1 - P(0)` formed by `nb_mass_above_zero()` =
    `-expm1(-shape * log1p(mu / shape))`. `post$var_fn` is new relative
    to `hurdle_poisson()`, so pearson residuals work.
  - `sim` draws the positive part by inverse transform on the NB CDF
    above zero, as `hurdle_poisson()` does. It does NOT copy brms's
    `posterior_predict_hurdle_negbinomial()`, which is wrong (defects,
    below).
- `fam_zoi_beta()` and the exported `zero_one_inflated_beta(link =
  "logit", link_phi = "log", link_zoi = "logit", link_coi = "logit")`.
  dpars `mu`, `phi`, `zoi`, `coi`. `y = 0`: `zoi (1 - coi)`; `y = 1`:
  `zoi coi`; `0 < y < 1`: `(1 - zoi) Beta(y | mu phi, (1 - mu) phi)`.
  Response in `[0, 1]`, refused by name otherwise. Both gates through
  `dpar_log_complement()`, `mu` through `dpar_complement()`, as in
  `zero_inflated_beta()`. `mean_fn` is brms's
  `posterior_epred_zero_one_inflated_beta()`, `zoi coi + (1 - zoi) mu`;
  `var_fn` is the exact second moment.
- `post$fit_check` for the ZOIB (`zoi_beta_fit_check()`): `coi` is
  scored on the rows at exactly 0 or 1 and nothing else. Without this
  check, a response with neither gave `NaN` for EVERY standard error of
  the fit, `mu`'s included; `frm()` said nothing, and `vcov()` and
  `summary()` warned only later, naming `zoi` and `coi` as flat but not
  why. A response with one end only sent `coi` to that boundary (logit
  -22.6, SE 1.1e4) with no warning at all. The check warns at fit time
  in those cases and names the remedy (`Beta()`,
  `zero_inflated_beta()` on `y` or `1 - y`, or `bf(coi = 0.5)`). It is
  silent when `coi` is held at a constant. `dev/fams-coi-check.R`
  (output `.txt`, n = 300): it warns on 0s-only, 1s-only and neither,
  and stays silent on data with both ends and on every shape with
  `bf(coi = 0.5)`, whose standard errors are then finite (SE of the
  slope 0.0478 where the estimated-coi fit gave `NaN`). The test "a
  response that cannot identify coi warns, and only then" pins the
  same matrix.
- Registry entries, constructors, `@rdname frmtmb-families` docs, a new
  section "Hurdle and zero-one-inflated responses", and the
  `link_zoi`/`link_coi` parameter docs. The mixture refusal text now
  names the zero-one-inflated beta; brms bars it as a mixture component
  and allows `hurdle_negbinomial`, and so does frmtmb.

### Two sibling defects fixed on the way

1. **The registry's `no_simulator` group was stale.** It listed
   `tweedie`, `compois` and `hurdle_poisson`, all three of which
   simulate, so `frm_compat(f, "simulate")` said "refused" for them.
   `test-simulate-density.R` already reads the real gap off the family
   registry and finds `cox` alone, which has its own rule, so the group
   and its rule are removed. `dev/fams-pins.R`, output in
   `dev/fams-pins.txt`: base says `refused` with `sim_can TRUE` for all
   three; the lane says `works`.
2. **`residuals(type = "osa")` crashed with base R's message on every
   point-mass family.** All ten core families with a `zi`, `hu` or
   `zoi` dpar (the two new ones and eight siblings) failed with
   "comparison (==) is possible only for atomic and list types",
   because `oneStepPredict()` re-tapes the density with the response
   as an `osa` object and the density branches on `y == 0`. Measured
   one by one on fitted models (`dev/fams-osa-probe.R`, output
   `dev/fams-osa-probe.txt`: 8 of 8 on base, and on the lane 10 of 10
   refused by name). They are now refused by name before
   `oneStepPredict()` runs, keyed on a named list
   (`osa_point_mass_families` in `R/predict.R`) rather than on the dpar
   names, so a custom family that does unwrap the osa object is not
   refused with them. The refusal points to `type = "pearson"` and
   `dharma_residuals()`, which I checked runs on a hurdle NB fit. The
   registry now says `refused` for the `point_mass` group.

A third, smaller change: `frm_linpred(type = "zprob")` on a ZOIB fit is
refused with a message naming `dpar = "zoi"` and `dpar = "coi"`,
because glmmTMB's zprob is one gate and this family has two. Without it
the generic message said the family had no zero-inflation parameter.

How to read it:

- Sections 1, 3, 6 and 7 are MEASUREMENTS of the density at shared
  parameter points: `obj$fn` at the optimum and at two points perturbed
  by N(0, 0.3) on every parameter, the density alone on and off the
  tape at four mean predictors, and `log_lik()` at 50 posterior draws.
  All agree to a few ulps.
- Section 2 is agreement at the ML optimum with an independent fitter.
  `glmmTMB(ziformula = ~ z, family = truncated_nbinom2)` is exactly
  this model (its `zi` is the hurdle probability and `sigma()` the NB
  size). The logLik gap is 7e-11 without and 4.5e-9 with a random
  intercept; coefficients agree to 1e-6, and the random-effect SD to
  6e-6, both at optimizer precision.
- Section 4 is an IDENTITY, not a measurement. With separate
  predictors for `mu`/`phi`, `zoi` and `coi` the ZOIB log-likelihood
  is a bernoulli on `1{y in {0, 1}}` (all rows), a bernoulli on `y`
  (boundary rows) and a beta (interior rows), sharing no parameter, so
  the joint ML fit is the three separate fits (`glm` x 2 and glmmTMB's
  `beta_family()`; betareg is not installed) and its logLik is their
  sum. The residual -1.77e-10 and the coefficient gaps of 3e-7 to 7e-7
  are the numerical check of that algebra at optimizer precision.

Robustness (`test-numerical-robustness.R`): both new families keep a

Simulation (`test-simulate-density.R`): both families are in the

## Decided not to do

- **cens() and trunc().** brms supports both for `hurdle_negbinomial`
  (its Stan code has `hurdle_neg_binomial_lccdf`). frmtmb's
  `negbinomial()` has no CDF either, and the NB CDF for `trunc()` is
  its own open item in `dev/round-handoff.md`. Both new families refuse
  with the existing "need a family with a CDF" message, and the
  registry says refused. ZOIB takes neither in brms.
- **brms's default priors on the sample route.** brms 2.23.0 gives
  `shape` `inv_gamma(0.4, 0.3)`, `phi` `gamma(0.01, 0.01)` and `hu`,
  `zoi`, `coi` `beta(1, 1)` (read from `brms::default_prior()`). frm()
  is maximum likelihood and has no defaults for any family. frm_sample()
  leaves the siblings' `shape`, `phi`, `zi` and `hu` flat and says so
  for `shape` and `phi` in its announcement; the new families follow
  the same rule, and the announcement names `shape` and `phi` for them
  (seen on a ZOIB fit). Changing that is a frmtmb.sample policy change
  for every zero-inflated and hurdle family, not this gap. The prior
  CLASSES match brms: `set_prior(class = "coi")` and friends work.
- **The brms vignette port** (`dev/brms-vignettes/brms_families.R`)
  still records the two families as MISSING. It is an audit with
  recorded output; editing the rows without rerunning the harness would
  leave its record inconsistent. The next audit rerun flips them.
- **The simulate-density header count** ("251 of this file's
  assertions are statistical") was not re-derived. The two new rows add
  16: 4 chi-square, 4 mean, 4 variance, 2 P(Y = 0) and 2 P(Y = 1).

## Defects found and not fixed

1. **brms's `posterior_predict_hurdle_negbinomial()` does not draw the
   zero-truncated NB.** It draws `rnbinom(mu = mu - t, size) + 1` with
   `t` a truncated exponential, the exact trick for the Poisson carried
   over to the NB, where it is not exact. At `mu = 1.5, shape = 0.8`,
   2e6 draws: P(Y = 1 | Y > 0) is 0.567 against the true 0.393
   (z = 505), and the mean of the positive part 1.93 against 2.63
   (`dev/fams-brms-pp-check.R`, `.txt`). frmtmb's simulator draws the
   exact distribution, so `simulate()` here and brms's
   `posterior_predict()` differ on purpose. Worth reporting upstream.
2. **`RTMB::dnbinom_robust()`'s gradient in the size is noise at a very
   large shape.** At `shape = exp(30)`, `mu = 1`, the gradient in log
   shape is 1.3745e-3 where a central difference and the analysis give
   about 1e-13, identically (same digits) in `negbinomial()`,
   `zero_inflated_negbinomial()` and `hurdle_negbinomial()`; at
   `exp(25)` it is 1.67e-4, at `exp(20)` it is correct. It reaches a fit
   only when `shape` runs toward the Poisson limit. Not fixed: it is
   upstream of every NB family. The robustness test does not sweep
   `shape` for that reason and says so.
3. **The hurdle normalizer underflows below a mean predictor of about
   -744**, where `exp(eta)` is below the smallest subnormal and
   `log(1 - P(0))` becomes `-Inf`. `hurdle_poisson()` has the same
   limit. The truth there is `log(mu)`; nothing short of a series in
   `mu` reaches it, and no fit visits it.
4. The `brmsfit-methods:358`/`359` verdict inconsistency above.

  residuals, `conditional_effects()`, `emmeans()` and `frm_sample()`.
  The hurdle negative binomial agrees with glmmTMB's
  `truncated_nbinom2` hurdle to 7e-11 in the log-likelihood, and both
  log-likelihoods agree with brms's densities to a few ulps. A
  zero-one-inflated beta fit whose response cannot identify `coi` now
  warns and names the remedy.
* `residuals(type = "osa")` on a zero-inflated, hurdle or
  zero-one-inflated family is refused by name. It used to fail with
  base R's "comparison (==) is possible only for atomic and list
  types".
* `frm_compat()` no longer calls `simulate()` refused for
  `hurdle_poisson()`, `compois()` and `tweedie()`, whose simulators
  work.

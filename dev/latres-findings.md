# Lane latres: brms's latent residuals (D6)

Base d5d0056d (frmtmb 0.78.0). Library `C:/Users/adf44/source/r/wt-latres-lib`.
Logs: `dev/latres-out/` (local). The blocks marked "generated" come from
`dev/latres-report.R` and `dev/latres-grid-summary.R`; every number has its
script and seed beside it.

## 1. What changed

`ar()`, `ma()`, `arma()`, `cosy()` and `unstr()` on a family with no residual
`y - mu` were refused on 0.78.0 ("a residual correlation needs a family with
real residuals"). They now fit brms 2.23.0's latent residual model on the ML
route and the sampling route. `fcor()`, `sar()`, the multi-category families,
the mixtures and a nonlinear `mu` are refused in brms's words.

New file `R/autocor-latent.R`. The term is a field block of the covariance
registry (`covstruct_core[["latres"]]`, with no formula spelling), so the
delta method, the outer covariance and `frm_sample()` read it through the paths
every block has. `frame$autocor` keeps its meaning, the terms that replace the
response density; a latent term is not in it. `frame_ac_terms()` is the one
reader of both, for the prior classes. `expand_b()` and `rr_jacobians()` gained
`expand$uses_theta`, a map that reads the block's theta as `rr()`'s does.

## 2. brms's model, as read from its code and runs

`dev/latres-probe.R`, `dev/latres-probe2.R` (logs `dev/latres-probe.log`,
`dev/latres-out/probe2.log`) and `stan_ac()` in
`dev/brms-suite/brms/R/stan-predictor.R`:

- `zerr ~ std_normal()`, `sderr > 0`, `mu += ... + err`.
- `cov = TRUE`, `cosy()`, `unstr()`: `err = scale_time_err(zerr, sderr, Lcortime,
  ...)`. One Cholesky factor read by leading blocks, so the lag counts rows of a
  group. `unstr()` reads the time levels (`Jtime_tg`).
- `cov = FALSE`, AR only: `err = sderr * zerr`, and the model block adds
  `mu[n] += Err[n, 1:Kar] * ar`, with `ar` boxed on (-1, 1) entrywise.
- That sum sits after `mu = inv_link(mu)` when `stan_eta_transform()` is true: a
  non-identity link, no joint link, no built-in link-scale density
  (`has_built_in_fun()`, `dev/latres-sbi.R`). A `cens()` or `trunc()` response
  loses the built-in. So poisson log reads it on the log scale and Gamma log on
  the response scale. `latres_on_response()` is this rule, and
  `test-latres-brms.R` compares it with brms's own programs over 21 family x
  link arms.
- Priors (`prior_ac()`): `sderr` takes `def_scale_prior` with `lb = 0`; `ar`, `ma`
  on (-1, 1); `cosy` on (0, 1); `cortime` `lkj(1)`.

## 3. The design, and why the coordinates are not brms's

brms's `err = L z` makes the inner Hessian `I + L'WL`, dense within a group. The
block uses coordinates in which the same law is sparse (the table is in the head
comment of `R/autocor-latent.R`): an AR(1) chain in `s`, an extra state before a
group's first row for MA(1) and ARMA(1,1), a shared latent per group for cosy,
`d` latents per group for unstr. The Laplace value does not move. The likelihood
reads the latents only through `err = A s` with `s` gaussian, so
`log|Q + A'WA| - log|Q| = log|I + A Q^-1 A' W|` (Sylvester), which reads `A` only
through `Cov(err)`. Measured two ways below: `logLik(fit)` against the Laplace
value computed from brms's own Hessian in `zerr`, and, with no brms, against the
exact marginal on a lognormal family (`test-latres.R`).

Lags count rows of a group, as brms counts them, and not time levels as the
covariance form on gaussian does (nlme's reading, a recorded divergence). The
new model has no such history, so it follows brms; the L1 rows on ragged data
below hold because of it.

## 5. The identities (step 2)

`dev/latres-l1.R` (data seed 167, 30 groups of 5) and `dev/latres-l1x.R`, through
the survey's `eq_l1()`: the joint at the conditional modes plus the map's
log-Jacobian at the estimate and at the two points stepped by +0.05 and -0.05. A
stepped point on which brms's own program rejects (a rate below zero under a
non-canonical link) reads `NA`, and frm()'s density is `NaN` there too. The
Laplace column is a separate measure that reads no frmtmb chart: the joint on
brms's program plus the normal integral, from differences of Stan's gradient in
`zerr`.

The translator's new rules are in `tests/testthat/helper-brms.R`:
`brms_latres_of()`, `brms_latres_chol()`, `brms_latres_pars()`, reached from
`stan_pars_from_fit()`, and `zerr` in `brms_inner_pat`. The log-Jacobian is the
closed form `(m - n)/2 log(2 pi) - 1/2 log|Q|` per block, derived in the comment
there.

`me()` beside the term is `dev/latres-me.R`: constant -5.684e-14, gradient
6.65e-11.

Independent references that do not use brms (`test-latres.R`):

- Each chain's `A Q^-1 A'` equals `sderr^2 C` from brms's definitions, ragged
  groups included, to 1e-10 relative.
- On `lognormal()` the inner problem is gaussian, so the tape must equal the
  exact marginal `N(mu, sigma^2 I + sderr^2 C)`, within 1e-8 relative
  (`dev/latres-exact.R` measured about 1e-10 absolute).
- `dev/latres-prof.R`: `ar(t, g, cov = TRUE)` and frmtmb's own
  `ar1(ft + 0 | g)`, which is the same model, give one logLik to six decimals on
  all five shapes that ran both, -1819.803420, -1847.852269, -14681.543432,
  -14590.227694, -3703.387615.

11 of the 77 cells stop with convergence code 1. In those I read: a latent SD at
its boundary or `ar` running toward 1 with `sderr` toward 0, where `ar(cov =
TRUE)` becomes `cosy()`. The data decide it: bernoulli `ar1t` reads logLik
-101.2422671 and bernoulli `cosy` -101.2422651. The identity holds at those stops.
I did not change how the optimizer reports them. It warns, and a boundary message
names the parameter by `theta_k`.

## 7. Refusals (step 3)

In brms's words, each read from the grid (`dev/latres-grid.R`):

- "Please set cov = TRUE when modeling MA structures for this family": `ma()` and
  `arma()` under `cov = FALSE`.
- "Covariance formulation of ARMA structures is only possible for effects of
  maximal order one": order two under `cov = TRUE`, on these families only.
- "FCOR terms are not implemented for this family", and "SAR terms ..." for
  frmtmb.spatial's `sar()`.
- "Explicit covariance terms can only be specified on 'mu'": categorical,
  multinomial, dirichlet, mixtures. `pull_autocor()` said "can only be written on
  'mu'" before; two tests changed with it.
- "Latent residuals are not implemented for non-linear models".
  `check_autocor_latent()` holds it. Today `pull_autocor` or the parser's nl
  refusal fires first, so `test-latres.R` reads "non-linear|nonlinear"; lane nlfix
  opens `bf(nl = TRUE, autocor = )`, and then this line is what fires on these
  families.
- "Cannot predict new latent residuals when using cov = FALSE in autocor terms".

The guard's absent case is in `test-latres.R`: gaussian and student keep the joint
density, `frame$autocor` and `thetaac`, and order two under `cov = TRUE` stays
frmtmb's documented divergence there. The grid shows the same six cells fit.

`emmeans()` and `conditional_effects()` are 0 on all six: both drop the term. z's
are per-row means, 2000 draws a side; the Bonferroni point at 0.001 over 150 rows
is about 4.5.

What brms does: `predictor_ac()` adds `err` and nothing else, under any
`re_formula`, and on new rows draws a new `err` from the term's covariance, once
per posterior draw.

Decisions, each a place where this differs from brms:

1. **`cov = FALSE` methods read the model, not brms's `err`-only mean (brms-45,
   `dev/upstream-bugs.md`).** `dev/latres-brms45.R`, seed 167: `log_prob` -399.897154;
   brms's `sum(log_lik) + log N(zerr)` -400.185140, a gap of 0.288 nats; frmtmb's rows
   give -399.897154, gap -5.68e-14. The same sum under `cov = TRUE` is within 5.68e-14
   on both, the control. I call that clearly wrong under the project tiebreaker, since
   brms's methods do not describe the program they summarize. `test-latres-brms.R`
   pins both halves, so it fails if brms changes. This is why the two `cov = FALSE`
   rows above differ. **It is your decision to overrule: reading `err` alone would
   take four lines and give digit parity with brms.**
2. **Reversed in fix round 1 (M6).** `fitted()` on new rows is the mean over the
   latent. `frm_linpred()` stays at `err = 0`.
3. **A mean outside the family's support is NaN on the tape** (`latres_guard()`),
   where Stan rejects. Without it RTMB folds `0 * log(mu)` at a zero count
   (rtmb-pitfalls item 22): the first `poisson("identity")` fit ended at a rate of
   -6.6e22 at a row with `y = 0`. With the guard it fits and is an L1 identity.

The seam for lane predread: `lp_eta_design()`'s block list (`"latres"` joins
`smooth`, `gp`, `hsgp`), `coef_b()`/`expand_b()` for the values, and `eta_shift` for a
per-draw shift on new rows. No reader builds its own predictor.

`residuals(type = "osa")` answers on these fits, since the rows keep their own
density. The review measured its distribution: right on Gamma, poisson, negbinomial
and binomial, and wrong on bernoulli, as section 16 files.

## Findings

### 1. major: `conditional_effects(method = "posterior_predict")` bands changed by the factor `c`, unrecorded

Evidence: `dev/review-sigmainn-readers.R`, `readers-*.txt`. The
predictive band half-width over 1.96 is 0.469741 on the lane and
0.854937 on rellib-r14. These are the innovation sd and the marginal
sd. `ce_sim_one()` (R/conditional-effects.R) draws iid rows from
`sim_response()` at the dpar `sigma`. `autocor_cond_strip()` keeps the
`cov = TRUE` term, but the grid path never reads it. So the band
follows whatever the parameter means. frmtmb.sample's
`conditional_effects(method = "predict")` uses the same `ce_sim_one()`.

The new band is brms's. brms 2.23.0 passes `incl_autocor = FALSE` for
every autocor term (`conditional_effects.R:380`, `exclude_terms.R`
excludes class "ac"). So `posterior_predict_gaussian()` draws iid at
brms's sigma, which is the innovation sd. The change therefore
improves parity. But the lane claims to list every reader, and this
interval is now narrower than the marginal predictive spread by a
factor of 0.55 on this fit. The NEWS, the findings' reader table, the
compat notes and the tests do not mention it.

Required fix:
- (a) Decide explicitly: keep brms's band (recommended under the
  tiebreaker), or call brms clearly wrong and draw at the marginal sd.
- (b) Add a NEWS bullet and a row in the reader table.
- (c) Add a test that pins the band to `sigma(fit)` (seen to fail on
  rellib-r14), for core and for frmtmb.sample's `method = "predict"`.
- (d) Update the comment above `autocor_cond_strip()` in
  `conditional_effects()`, which says only `cov = FALSE` is dropped as
  brms drops it.

### Findings

S1. **minor:** `.get_sigma.frmtmb_fit` changes `insight::get_sigma()`
on fresh fits that have none of these terms, and the NEWS line
misstates it.

Evidence: `getsigma-r14.txt` against `getsigma-lane.txt` (seed 3).
- On a plain `bf(y ~ x, sigma ~ x2)` fit, rellib-r14 gives 1.7285282
  (insight's fallback `sqrt(deviance / df)`, after `sigma()` returned
  NA). The lane gives NA, with `sigma()`'s warning.
- gaussian, student, poisson, bernoulli, random effect and
  multivariate are unchanged.
- The NEWS says the fallback applied "where `sigma()` stopped". In
  fact `sigma()` returns NA under `sigma ~ x`, and that is the only
  fresh-fit case that changes.
- This contradicts the lane's claim that models without these terms
  are identical.

Fix: write the method as `autocor_check_fit(x); NextMethod()`.
`dev/review-sigmainn-nextmethod.R` shows that this restores 1.728528
under `sigma ~ x2` and 1.085854 for gaussian, and still refuses the
saved 0.76.0 fit. Reword the NEWS line to "refused for a saved fit",
and add a test: on a `sigma ~ x` fit, `get_sigma()` equals insight's
default.

S2. **nit:** `confint(parm = "x")` on a saved `ar(cov = TRUE)` fit
refuses, though a Wald interval for a mean coefficient does not read
sigma. It is consistent with the NEWS ("`confint()`"), so leave it or
check `parm`.

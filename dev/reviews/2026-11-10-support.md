# Review of lane support (round opened 2026-11-09, base e0cce906)

Reviewer: adversarial review. I formed this view from the diff, then read
`dev/support-findings.md`. Every number below comes from a log of a
reviewer script `dev/review-support-*.R` in this worktree. Each log has a
`lib:` line. "Base" is rellib-r19. "Lane" is wt-support-lib before
rellib-r19.

Verdict: **FIX**.

## Findings

### 1. BLOCKER: importance sampling and nested quadrature evaluate the GEV on doubles, and they now stop or give a different fit

The rule "doubles = a reader that reports" is false in this package. Two
fitting paths evaluate the objective and `row_lpdf()` on plain doubles
and feed the result back into the fit:

- `R/importance.R:1471-1473`, `freeze()`: `io$amat()` on doubles sets
  the tape's offset (`apply(amat, 1L, max)`). `imp_verify()`
  (`:1326-1380`) compares `sum(a[, j])` with `-nll(joint)` on doubles
  and refuses unless both are finite.
- `R/quadrature-nested.R:563-569`, `prime()`: the inner and outer
  offsets `off_i` and `off_o` are row maxima on doubles. The frozen tape
  then forms `v - off`, and with `off = -Inf` that is `+Inf` or NaN.
  `quad_nest_verify()` (`:600-625`) refuses unless both are finite. The
  `rule_err` pass (`:917-925`) does the same on doubles.

On the base, a draw or node that puts a GEV row outside its support gave
the finite barrier on both sides. On the lane it gives `-Inf`.

`dev/review-support-is.R`: GEV `y ~ 1 + (1 | g)` and `+ (1 | h)`, 15
groups of 8, mu 1 + u, sd(u) 0.7, sigma 1. Laplace, `importance = 500`
and one-block quadrature are identical on both builds. Nested quadrature
(`(1 | g) + (1 | h)`, `quadrature = TRUE`):

| seed, xi | base | lane |
|---|---|---|
| 1, 0.5 | -218.1409258 | -218.1409258 |
| 1, 0.8 | -241.0583474 | -241.0583474 |
| 2, 0.5 | -242.9981897, beta 1.1569858 | -242.9974927, beta 1.154474 |
| 2, 0.8 | -267.6683306 | ERROR: quadrature = TRUE could not reproduce ... (node pair 11, 1: -Inf against -Inf) |
| 3, 0.5 | -214.7792046 | ERROR, same |
| 3, 0.8 | -234.1633199 | ERROR, same |

At seed 2, xi 0.5 the lane fit is silently different. The cause is in
`dev/review-support-nq.R`, which traces `prime()`. The lane's first
candidate has `off_i non-finite 60 of 4950, off_o non-finite 44 of 165`.
That round is rejected ("rejected a step of 1, objective 243.03935"). The
base accepts the same step ("accepted a step of 1, objective 242.99823")
with every offset finite. The trust-region path then differs, and the
final rule has `off_o non-finite 294 of 315`. Final estimates:

    base logLik -242.998189713707 beta 1.156985773478 theta -0.543440065712 -3.749768780143
    lane logLik -242.9974927334698 beta 1.1544739786280 theta -0.5462561181370 -2.9999817642189

`dev/review-support-is2.R` (`importance = 500`, xi 1 and 1.5, seeds 1
to 4): 6 of 8 cases are identical on both builds. At seed 2 and seed 4
with xi 1.5, the lane stops with "`importance` could not reproduce this
model's joint log-density from its per-group pieces (draw 1: -Inf against
-Inf)". The base fits them (logLik -326.5836299 and -329.4890012).

This contradicts three claims. The NEWS bullet says "The fitted objective
keeps its barrier, so estimates and `logLik()` do not change". The
`?gen_extreme_value` text says "Only the objective the optimizer and the
sampler differentiate is different". The findings' "the fit is
unchanged" claim tested only seven fixed-effects ML fits. The findings
("Not done", third bullet) saw the importance numeric path and called it
harmless: "both weigh that draw 0". They did not read `imp_verify()` or
the offset.

Fix direction: switch on what the caller is, not on class. A reader
(`log_lik()`, `draws_row_loglik()`, the predictive densities) asks for
the exact value explicitly, for example with a flag or a dynamic option
around the reader. Every internal re-evaluation of the objective keeps
the tape's arithmetic. Otherwise every off-tape consumer must handle
`-Inf` (finding 2 lists them). Add a GEV test under `importance =` and
nested `quadrature = TRUE` where a draw or node leaves the support.

### 2. MAJOR: other off-tape evaluations of the objective and of densities that need the barrier's value

These sites evaluate the objective or `row_lpdf()` on doubles for a
fitting decision, not for a report. I did not construct a failure at
each one. Each one reads `-Inf` now where it read a finite number:

- `R/par-template.R:520-530`, `nl_check_body_read()`: `nll()` on
  doubles. A non-finite value passes, so this is benign.
- `R/par-template.R:597-640`, `nl_coef_curved()`: a finite-difference
  Hessian of `nll()` on doubles. A non-finite entry returns FALSE, which
  sends the body from `local` to `saddle` in `nl_saddle_find()`.
- `R/par-template.R:1639-1645`, `nl_domain_start()`: `is.finite(nll(tpl))`
  decides whether to move the start. The Frechet's uncapped
  `exp_neg_capped()` makes the value `Inf` where it was about 1e304.
- `R/autocor-latent.R:897-990`, `latres_row_derivs()` and
  `latres_tk()`: second and third differences of `row_lpdf()` in eta on
  doubles (steps of 1e-3 and 0.02 latent sd), and steps of +/-0.05 in
  the outer parameters. These feed the Laplace bias verdict of a GEV
  with a latent residual. A row stepped outside now gives `Inf - Inf`.
- `R/interop.R:74-77`, `check_custom_family()`: `v0` on doubles. A test
  value outside the GEV support now stops with "lpdf is not finite"
  where the tape is finite.

Fix finding 1 at the root, and these follow.

### 3. MAJOR: the trunc(lb <= 0) fix is incomplete: shifted_lognormal() keeps the same defect

`dev/review-support-trunc.R` (seed 402, n 300, `y = 2 rexp + 0.05`,
`y ~ 1` against the same fit with a bound that is not binding), both
builds:

- Lane: exponential, Gamma, weibull, lognormal, frechet and
  inverse.gaussian all give dlogLik 0 and max|dbeta| 0 under
  `trunc(lb = -1)`, `lb = 0`, `lb = -1, ub = 1e6` (except the Frechet,
  see below), `cens + trunc(lb = -1)` and `cens + trunc(lb = 0)`. The
  same holds for Beta (`lb = -1`, `0`, `ub = 2`).
  `dev/review-support-hurdle.R`: hurdle_gamma and hurdle_lognormal give
  0. zero_inflated_beta gives 5.7e-14 in logLik and 5.9e-12 in the
  parameters. `dev/review-support-cox.R`: frmtmb.survival `cox()` with
  `cens + trunc(lb = 0)` and `lb = -1` gives 0 and 0.
- **shifted_lognormal**: all five forms stop on BOTH builds with "The
  optimizer failed on this model (shifted_lognormal, ML, nlminb): NA/NaN
  gradient evaluation". The untruncated fit works (-522.6712).
  `dev/review-support-slog.R` shows the slot: `cdf_log(c(-1, 0), mu 0,
  sigma 1, ndt 0.02)` gives `lS = 0` with a taped gradient of
  `NaN NaN NaN`. The cause is `shifted_lognormal_cdf_log()`
  (`R/families-cdf.R:1057`): `ad_max(q - ndt, 1e-300)` then `pnorm(z)`
  at z about -690. This is the brief's defect class (a bound below a
  positive support is no truncation, and the fit stops at a NaN
  gradient). It is missing from the survey table. `ndt` is a parameter,
  so the edge cannot be resolved off the tape the way
  `positive_cdf_q()` does. The fix must give `lS = 0` with gradient 0
  wherever `q <= ndt`, and an `lF` with no NaN derivative.
- The Frechet's `lb = -1, ub = 1e6` difference (1.96e-4) is the real
  mass above 1e6: the same with `ub` alone, on both builds
  (`dev/review-support-fr.R`). This fit is degenerate (log nu -21.1)
  on both builds. Not a finding.

### 4. MINOR: a GEV trunc(lb = ) below the support still moves the fit

On both builds, `gen_extreme_value()` with `trunc(lb = -1)` below the
fitted lower end gives dlogLik 7.7e-12 and max|dbeta| 1.77e-7.
`cens + trunc(lb = -1)` gives 2.2e-10 and 1.1e-6
(`dev/review-support-trunc-*.log`). The tape's clamp gives `F(lb)` up to
about 1e-13 where it is 0. This is small and is the barrier's role (out
of scope). But the findings and the help say that only rows within
1e-15 in `t` see the clamp. A bound below the support is a second case.
Name it, or resolve a `trunc()` bound below the support off the tape,
as `positive_cdf_q()` does. Only the parameter-free side can be
resolved that way.

### 5. MINOR: the xbeta claim in the report contradicts the lane's own record

The claim relayed to this review says "frmtmb is closer to a 256-bit
Rmpfr reference than betareg". The record (`dev/support-findings.md`,
lines 272-276) says the opposite on most measures:

| case | frmtmb vs 256-bit | brms/betareg vs 256-bit |
|---|---|---|
| block | 6.55e-14 | 1.49e-14 |
| y = 0 | 1.03e-13 | 3.8e-15 |
| y = 0.3 | 1.3e-12 | 4.69e-14 |
| y = 1 | 1.32e-14 | 2.54e-14 |

frmtmb is closer only at y = 1. The findings' own sentence ("Both sides
are exact to the precision of their arithmetic") is correct. The report
claim is not, so do not carry it into the release record.

## Checked and found correct

- Mixtures on doubles: `RTMB::logspace_add(c(-Inf, -Inf, 0), c(-Inf, 1,
  -Inf))` gives `-Inf 1 0`. A component at `-Inf` gives the other one
  exactly, and a row outside both gives `-Inf`, not NaN
  (`dev/review-support-negxi-*.log`, first line). The lane's test pins
  it.
- GEV with xi near -1 (true xi -0.6, -0.8, -0.95, seeds 1 to 5, n 60;
  four fits reach xi_hat -1.0000 with `t` at max(y) down to 4.4e-9): no
  non-finite reader row. The reader sum minus `logLik()` is at most
  2.8e-14 on both builds (`dev/review-support-negxi-*.log`).
- Laplace, `importance = 500` at xi 0.5 and 0.8, and one-block
  quadrature: identical estimates and logLik on both builds (finding 1
  table, `dev/review-support-is-*.log`).
- `positive_cdf_q()` on the tape: multiplying by `on = 1` is exact, so
  fits with `q > 0` are unchanged. NA bounds still propagate NA.
- Ledger: running `dev/brmsport-bin2-verdicts-2p.R` on a scratch copy
  of `dev/` regenerates `brmsport-bin2-valuechecks-manual.tsv`,
  `brmsport-bin2-verdicts-manual-2p.tsv` and `brmsport-bin2-split.tsv`
  byte for byte (`cmp` on each). It prints "bin-2 values verdicts removed
  (now hold): log_lik:580" and "bin-2 2p verdict moved: log_lik:349 to

  `dev/brmsport-bin2-ledger.R:252` requires ("VALUE CHECK HOLDS but has

- Tests: the tolerances are relative (eps times the value, or 1e-6 of
  the SE), with no registry use. The new assertions fail on the base and

# Review of lane gpstop (round of 2026-11-11)

Reviewer: adversarial review of `wt-r82-gpstop` against e8abed0a (0.81.0).
Diff read with CRLF removed. Scripts are `dev/reviews/gpstop-review-*.R`,
logs are in `dev/reviews/gpstop-review-out/`. Unless a line says otherwise,
each arm comparison runs in ONE process on `wt-gpstop-lib`: arm `lane` is
the build as installed, and arm `norelin` replaces `gp_body_relin_applies()`
with `function(...) FALSE`, which is 0.81.0's route. The arms are
interleaved and their order alternates by row. A counter wraps
`gp_body_relin()`. The machine was loaded by other lanes, so a wall time is
read only against its interleaved twin.

Verdict: **FIX**. The relinearization is a large net gain: 15 of 122 fresh
rows rise by more than 1e-3, up to +173.41. But on 2 rows it keeps a point
that is worse than 0.81.0's: once a Laplace spike in place of a regular
maximum, once a degenerate field with no warning. Its acceptance test
(`better()`) reads only the objective, and the lane's own item 2 shows that
for these families the objective is not a reliable judge. The 5033 NEWS
bullet and backlog closure also say more than the evidence shows.

`gpstop-review-sweep.R` `mk("logis_a", "bernoulli", 40, 2)`. Model as in
MAJOR 1 with `bernoulli()`.

    DEGP lane beta -11.046 beta -135.46 theta 3.5988 theta -323.27

The lane returns, at code 0 with no warning, a log length scale of -323.27
(the field is white noise), `sdgp` 36.6, and `b = -135`. Its "gain" of 3.94
is a Laplace artifact. Importance sampling at that point reads -32.0 and
-28.5 (ESS 3, so it is not a number, but it is nowhere near -20.57). The
model's own limit, an observation-level iid effect in `a`, fitted directly,
reaches only -24.505. 0.81.0's point (sd at 0, which is the no-field model)
is correct: Laplace and importance sampling agree to 0.002. This is a
silent wrong answer that 0.81.0 did not give.

Fix for both (suggestion): `gp_second_descent()` accepts a relin descent
only if it is a regular point. The checks are code 0, an outer gradient
within `grad_tol`, the inner Hessian's smallest eigenvalue not near 0 (the
field's coefficients have a unit prior, so a healthy value is about 1; the
spikes here read 2e-7 to 4e-8), and each length scale within the span of
the positions (for example, from a tenth of the smallest gap to ten times
the range). The alternative-start loop that 0.81.0 already had has the same
blind spot, but the relin rounds add new runs that reach it. Then rerun the
192 rows and the sweep below.

### MAJOR 3. Item 5033: the NEWS Limits bullet and the CLOSED line say more than the evidence shows

`dev/reviews/gpstop-review-student.R` (log `student.txt`) and
`gpstop-review-student2.R` (log `student2-80.txt`). The rows are the lane's
(`set.seed(74000 + 31 + n)`).

Reproduced: at the reference, the smallest inner Hessian eigenvalue is
4.17e-8 (st80) and 1.82e-5 (st160), with `-log(ev)/2` 8.496 and 5.457. The
outer gradient is 1.28e12 and 2.01e11. At the default these are 0.999 and 1,
and 2.49e-5 and 8.15e-6.

But on st80 the model's marginal (importance sampling, two proposals, two
seeds each, 20000 draws) rises monotonically from the default to the
reference:

    t 0.00 laplace 68.5503 | IS 71.2654 71.0254 71.1619 71.0722
    t 0.50 laplace 69.7767 | IS 72.8379 72.5731 72.9694 72.4669
    t 0.90 laplace 70.3410 | IS 73.5728 73.7312 73.6586 73.6183
    t 1.00 (ref)           | IS 73.8216 73.5775 73.5484 73.4801   (ESS 28 to 243)

Six fits started on that segment (t = 0.3 to 0.95) return to the default
(68.5503), to a lower regular maximum (67.1802), or to warned spikes (code
1) whose marginal reads 73.47 to 73.85. So the default IS the best regular
maximum of the Laplace objective. However, on st80 the model's marginal
likelihood is about 2.5 higher elsewhere, and the default stops below it
with code 0 and no warning. On st160 the lane's reading holds: the
importance-sampling value falls from 203.5 to 204.0 at the default to 201.7
to 202.1 at the reference. On both rows the reported log-likelihood is 2.2
to 2.8 below the importance-sampling value of the same point.

So "is not a short stop" (NEWS) and "the student rows are not short"
(backlog CLOSED line) are true of the optimizer on the Laplace objective.
They are false of the model on st80. The limit is the Laplace
approximation, not the optimizer. No cheap check at the stop separates the
two cases: the inner eigenvalue at the st80 default is 0.999, which is
healthy. The eigenvalue does separate the spikes (4e-8 to 7e-4 on every
spike reached, against about 1 at regular points). That is also the guard
MAJOR 1 needs.

Fix: leave 5033 open, re-described as a limit of the Laplace approximation
(`student()` with an exact gp() on a step field: the reported value can be
2.5 below the marginal at a point that the Laplace objective reads only as
a spike). Rewrite the Limits bullet to say that, without "is not a short
stop". Recommend `importance =` or `frm_sample()` for such a model, if
either applies.

## Short check (fix round)

Verdict: **FIX**, small. MAJOR 1, MAJOR 3 and every MINOR are resolved.

### S1 (MAJOR 2 not closed in general). The lower end of the span rule accepts a white-noise field

`gpstop-short-white.R`, log `short-white.txt`. On MAJOR 2's own data and
tape, the first build's degenerate point is placed with the log length
scale at `log(k * mingap / 10)`, and nlminb polishes from there:

    WHITE plain default ll -24.511135 conv 0 nwarn 0
    WHITE plain k 2  ... end ll -20.567787 conv 0 gmax 3.98e-06 inner_ev 1 theta 3.599,-5.878 | ls/mingap 0.2   | regular TRUE
    WHITE plain k 5  ... end ll -20.567682 conv 0 gmax 1.77e-05 inner_ev 1 theta 3.598,-4.673 | ls/mingap 0.667 | regular TRUE
    WHITE tie   k 20 ... end ll -20.567175 conv 0 gmax 4.37e-06 inner_ev 1 theta 3.598,-5.250 | ls/mingap 52.5  | regular TRUE

The white-noise artifact (sdgp 36.6, b = -135, importance sampling -32 and
-28.5 against Laplace -20.57; review MAJOR 2) is a stationary point with
code 0 at a length scale of 0.0094. That is 0.67 of the smallest gap
(0.014) and far below the typical spacing of 40 points on [0, 10]. With
one near-tie (gap 1e-4), the same point passes at 52 times the smallest
gap. `gp_relin_regular()` returns TRUE in each case: the inner eigenvalue
is 1 (a white-noise field is log-concave), so of the four tests only the
span test can catch this, and its lower end, a tenth of the SMALLEST gap,
does not.

The fixed build refuses MAJOR 2's row only because that descent ran to
e^-323. In my 46 new bernoulli rows (`short-tie.txt`, 6 seeds, 4 bodies,
positions as drawn and with a near-tie), every white-noise descent ran just
as far (ls 6.1e-8, ls/mingap 3e-5 and 6e-4) and was refused: 0 rows moved,
0 code or warning changes. So no frm() reproducer, but the guard depends on
where the optimizer stops on a flat ridge.

Fix: take the lower end from the MEDIAN nearest-neighbour gap, for example
a tenth of it (here 0.0094 against about 0.2 would refuse). The lane's kept
descents read ls/range of at least 0.0136. With a median gap of about range
/ n, that is about 0.5 to 5 times the median gap for n 40 to 400, so a
tenth leaves a margin of 5 or more. Measure it on the 122 rows before the
change goes in.

### Other attacks on the rule

- Upper end (ten times the range). On 12 gaussian rows whose field is a
  linear trend (`short-long.txt`, exp_a and pow_a, n 60 and 120, seeds 1
  to 3), the fitted length scale is e^2.2 to e^3.8, 0.9 to 4.5 times the
  range. Relin read no new scale on any row, and 0 rows moved. A refusal
  at the upper end could lose a gain only where the first descent sits
  SHORTER than the truth and relin moves past ten times the range, the
  opposite of the defect. It is not too tight on anything I could build.
- The 1e-2 eigenvalue cut. The regular points read 0.997 to 1 (the lane)
  and 0.999 to 1 (my st80 and st160 defaults). The lowest healthy
  non-stationary point I measured reads 0.505 (st80 segment, t 0.5). The
  spikes read 4e-8 to 7e-4. That is a margin of at least 14 below and 50
  above. As the lane says, on these rows the cut never decided alone.
- Cost of the eigenvalue test (MINOR). `gp_inner_ev_min()` builds the
  whole inner Hessian dense and takes all its eigenvalues. A field beside a
  large group term (`a ~ gp(x) + (1 | subject)`) pays for that on every
  accepted candidate. `eig.txt`: q 500 0.02 s, q 2000 1.17 s (31 MB), q 4000
  12.75 s (122 MB), under load. A sparse Cholesky of `H - 1e-2 I` (it
  succeeds exactly when every eigenvalue exceeds 1e-2) answers the same
  question in the sparse cost.

### The rest

- MAJOR 1 holds: `fx-repro-new.txt` gives 22.393662, c0, w0, the norelin
  value.
- MAJOR 3 is fixed. The CLOSED line is removed, 5033 is re-filed open with
  the importance-sampling numbers, and the Limits bullet has no version
  number. Nit: the bullet says `frm_sample()`, which lives in
  frmtmb.sample. Other core bullets write `frmtmb.sample::frm_sample()`.
- MINOR 4 (2 warnings on mm_K), MINOR 5 (BREAKING; cost as measured,
  matching my 1.006 and the lane's 1.003), MINOR 7 (filed) and MINOR 8
  (9.532225, equal to norelin) are resolved.
- Cold-start rule against nlstop (`frmtmb-wt-nlstop/R/fit.R`): nlstop sets
  `cold_tpl <- is.null(template) || prefit_seeded` after the pre-fit block
  and asks `is.null(bw) && is.null(start) && announce_start && cold_tpl`.
  gpstop sets `gp_cold <- is.null(template)` before that block and asks
  `gp_body && announce_start && (gp_cold || prefit_seeded)`. The two are
  equal on every path:
  - When the pre-fit seeds, `template` is NULL before the block, and
    `prefit_seeded` is TRUE.
  - `gp_body` is frm()'s `is.null(start)`. `gp_body_relin_applies()` tests
    the whitening as `is.null(bw)` does.
  - A saddle or probe refit has `announce_start = FALSE`.

  One shared consequence for consolidation: under `autoscale_choose()` the
  seeded candidate is a template fit with `prefit_seeded` FALSE, so neither
  relin nor `prior_conflict_escape()` runs on it. The plain candidate gets
  both. That is conservative, and the same in both lanes. Fold `gp_cold`
  into `cold_tpl` at the merge, and keep the two hunks' order: relin runs
  inside `gp_second_descent()`, before `prior_conflict_escape()`.
- Runs: I did not rerun them. The lane's record (test-gp-start 247/0, base
  238/2/2 with the right failures, core 317 files 0 fail, gp sessions in
  three orders, OpenBLAS 6 files, check 1 NOTE) is consistent with the
  diff. The check tarball predates a comment-only edit in `R/fit.R`, which
  is acceptable.

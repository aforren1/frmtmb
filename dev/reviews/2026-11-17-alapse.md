The lane's table shows D and E with `gap_j` at most 1.9e-11 because its
`alt_max()` starts at the fit, so it finds the fit again. The findings'
"The joint gaps that remain are all in designs with a constant
estimated quantile" is wrong.

Decision: this is a silent wrong result. The quantile is estimated by
default, the fit reports code 0 and "a maximum of the likelihood", and
the reported coefficients belong to another quantile (B 22: slope 0.556
against 0.733 at the MLE). 0.83.0 warned on every one of these fits.
The proof is local, and the lane knows it (its MEDIUM item), but the
code makes the result look global.

maximum is global. With a fixed quantile and a constant sigma, the
negative log-likelihood is convex in (1 / sigma, b / sigma), so the
proved point is the global maximum. With an estimated quantile, either
search (the quantile process for a constant sigma and quantile, which
the lane's `exact_const()` in `dev/alapse-rq.R` already does), or keep
a warning of its own class saying that an estimated quantile has local
maxima and to fix it. A fixed quantile with `sigma ~ z` was not
measured here; measure it with multi-start before silencing it. Raise
the lane's backlog item to HIGH and say that the lane removes the
warning these fits had.

### Finding 2 (MEDIUM): degenerate vertices on tied data

`rq-lane.txt`, 18 fits with integer y (rpois) and a discrete x or g:
one proved (`ties-sigmag-s4`), 17 with "no optimal vertex of the
quantile regression of response y was reached" and both convergence
warnings. Five of the 17 are at rq's objective
(`ties-fixq0.5-s1` rel 1.84e-11, `-s2` 1.87e-12, `ties-estq-s2`
2.38e-13, `ties-sigmag-s2` 2.39e-11, `-s6` 1.36e-12) and still warn,
as on 0.83.0.

Cause (`alapse-review-ties.R`, `ties-lane.txt`): started at rq's own
solution, `ald_vertex()` returns NULL with 70 and with 5000 iterations;
the solutions have 13, 7 and 12 zero residuals with k = 2. The vertex
test gives each off-basis zero row the subgradient p, which is
sufficient but not necessary at a degenerate vertex, and the ratio test
then pivots with step 0. With the ties broken by a 1e-9 jitter, the
same call proves the same b in 3 to 4 iterations.

Safe (the warnings stay; no wrong value), but item 4982 asked that a
fit at rq's optimum not warn, and quantile regression on counts or
rounded data is common. Fix: at a degenerate vertex, choose the
subgradients of every zero-residual row in their intervals (a small
feasibility LP, or an NNLS), with an anti-cycling pivot rule; add an
integer-y test. Otherwise file it and qualify NEWS ("Over 175 simulated
fits ... every fit was proved" holds for continuous data only) and the
findings ("Not measured on such data").

## 4. The 5449 extension

The premise holds for every parameter value, not only those tested:
in `R/covstruct.R`, each new structure's density is
`dmvnorm(b, 0, s^2 * R(theta_rest))` (hetar1 and toep:
`R * (sd %o% sd)`; gr(prec =): `exp(-2 theta) Q`, or `Q (x) Sigma^-1`),
with R free of the sds. Scaling every sd by c multiplies the covariance
by c^2 at every range, smoothness or correlation value, so the
approximation `max_b (l(b) - b' Sigma^-1 b / 2)` does not fall along
that direction from any point. A range that varies at the same time
does not matter: the direction exists at each point. The lane's
profiles re-optimized the range. I found no refused model with a real
maximum. The only weak case, an inner mode at b = 0 where the
objective is level in sd (no unique maximum), belongs to the
0.83.0 structures too.

Consistency (LOW): the review's reproduction of the quadrature
diagnosis shows the plain `(1 | g)` Laplace profile falling at log sd
+6 after rising at +2 (`dev/alapse-out/quad-lane.txt`, seeds 1, 5, 7,
9: for example seed 1, -93.635, -81.447, -94.527). TMB's inner search
fails there, as the lane says it does for gp(). So that fall is no
evidence against gp()'s proof; keeping gp() open is conservative, but
the reason given is not the right one.

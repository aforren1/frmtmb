# Review of lane quadseam2 (round of 2026-11-13, base ec8178d3 = 0.82.0)

Adversarial review. Lane tree `frmtmb-wt-quadseam2`, library
`wt-quadseam2-lib`, base library `rellib-r21`. Scripts
`dev/reviews/quadseam2-review-*`, logs
`dev/reviews/quadseam2-review-log/`. Every number below is from those
logs; the summary lines are made by `dev/reviews/quadseam2-review-sum.sh`.
Base and lane runs were each pinned to one P-core mask
(`quadseam2-review-aff.cmd`, rtmb-pitfalls.md item 21); two base runs
on different masks gave bitwise-identical output.

## Verdict: FIX

Two items. F1 is a silent wrong answer that the lane introduces (item
5408). F2 is a gate whose stated invariant fails (item 5620). The rest
of the lane (5222, 4836/4861, 4867, 561, 4846) holds under attack.

## F1 (MAJOR, silent-wrong). Item 5408: the rule's escape is skipped on fits that need it

`R/fit.R:1818-1822`: the rule's stationary escape runs only when
`quad_fit()`'s Laplace-side escape left no record
(`if (is.null(esc_lap))`). A record means the Laplace escape RAN, not
that the rule's optimum is off the point. The rule's own optimization
starts from the Laplace optimum and can walk back onto alpha = 0, and
then nothing moves it.

Reproducer `dev/reviews/quadseam2-review-esc2.R` (skew_normal with
`(1 | g)`, `quadrature = TRUE`, every alpha started at 0; 15 seeds of
three designs, 45 fits; logs `esc2-base.txt`, `esc2-lib.txt`):

    ALPHAX/11    base rule 6.667 ll -84.5783086103 alpha 951780,-2644632 | lane rule - ll -91.2452361629 alpha 4.801e-16,-5.186e-15 | lost 6.6669
    SYMSMALL/1   base rule 2.291 ll -84.9253951576 alpha 9359630         | lane rule - ll -87.2167801711 alpha -6.934e-15         | lost 2.2914
    WEAK/4       base rule 1.453 ll -74.1377898690 alpha 3409589         | lane rule - ll -75.5906679346 alpha 5.147e-15          | lost 1.4529
    fits 45 lane below base by >1e-8: 3

On 0.82.0 the rule's escape ran on these 3 and gained 6.67, 2.29 and
1.45. On the lane each fit stops with alpha at 1e-15, on the declared
point, that much below, code 0, no warning: the item-828/5222 failure
this machinery exists to prevent. The other 42 fits agree to 10
decimals.

Why the lane did not see it: `dev/quadseam2-escdup.R`'s SYM and STALL
sets never had the rule's optimum on the point (base "rule 0" on all
50), so "the second escape gained nothing" was measured only on FORCED,
where the declared window (tol 1e6) holds every fit.

Expected: the rule's escape runs whenever the rule's optimum sits on a
declared point, as on 0.82.0 (`escape_stationary()` already returns at
once when `stationary_escapes()` is empty, so ungating costs nothing on
a fit off its point), and the Laplace escape's record is kept beside the
rule's (restore `stationary_escape_keep()` or its equivalent). Then:
`test-quad-escape-record.R` goes back to the record test (its new
`expect_identical(ran, 1L)` at line 38 asserts the defect), add one of
the three cases above as a regression test (seen failing on the lane
build), drop the NEWS bullet "a family's declared stationary point is
escaped once", and remove the 5408 CLOSED line from
`dev/test-backlog.md`. If the FORCED cost matters, gate on something the
rule's optimum says (for example, skip only when the rule's optimum is
within rounding of the Laplace escape's kept optimum), not on the record.

## F2 (MEDIUM). Item 5620: latres_quadratic() is not scale-free in its step

`R/autocor-latent.R:1083-1093`: the differences are taken at a step of
the latent's marginal sd `h`, against an absolute tolerance
`1e-8 * (1 + max|l|)`. A third and fourth difference scale as h^3 and
h^4, so for ANY family the gate answers "quadratic" once `h` is small
enough. `dev/reviews/quadseam2-review-fewbern.R` replaces the step on
four bernoulli `ar(t, g, cov = TRUE)` fits (log `fewbern-lib.txt`):

    gate at step 0.001: TRUE on all 4 seeds
    gate at step 0.01, 0.1, 0.3, 1: FALSE on all 4 seeds

and it happens on a real fit: FEWBERN seed 3 of
`quadseam2-review-latres.R` (4 groups of 5, `set.seed(703)`, sderr at
exp(-10.12), marginal sd 7.6e-5) warned `frmtmb_latres_laplace` on
0.82.0 ("the fit has no curvature to read its first-order correction
by") and is silent on the lane with `latres_quadratic()` TRUE on a
bernoulli:

    CHANGED base  1 lane  0 : FEWBERN 3: ll -8.073525 latres 0 quad TRUE shift Inf
    latres rows compared: 33

That one case is at a variance boundary, where the Laplace bias is
likely small, so the dropped warning is not shown to hide a wrong value;
but the gate's documented claim ("every row's log density is quadratic
in its linear predictor") is false there, and the next caller may trust
it. Expected: a step that does not shrink with the fit, for example
`max(h, 1)` on the link scale (a lognormal is exact at any step, so
nothing is lost), or the differences divided by h^3 and h^4 and compared
with the second difference over h^2. Also line 1093 judges only the
rows whose differences are finite (`any(ok) && all(...[ok])`); a row
that leaves the support at eta +- 2h should make the answer FALSE
(`all(ok) && ...`). I could not build such a fit (poisson identity link
is refused upstream by the mean guard, `POISID` rows of the latres log),
so this half is a hardening, not a demonstrated defect.

Scale in y is not a problem: lognormal with y times 1e-8 and 1e8, both
spellings, seeds 40401 to 40403: gate TRUE, 0 warnings on base and lane.
Gamma (shape 3 and 300), weibull, student, and poisson/bernoulli with 20
rows keep their base verdicts (33 rows, one change, the one above).

## Attacks that found nothing to fix

### 1. Item 5222, the repeated escape

Termination: `max_passes` is the first call's count of predictors on
their point, so at most `n_on` passes of at most `n_on * length(from)`
starts each. The gain test is a strict `<` (`R/fit.R:4395`), so a pass
that gains by rounding buys the next pass; the cost is bounded by the
same cap. On symmetric data, where alpha = 0 can be legitimate
(`quadseam2-review-passes.R`, logs `passes-base.txt`, `passes-lib.txt`):

    MIX2sym/1   base starts 4 ll -1203.123996959 | lane starts 6 ll -1202.767225133 free -1202.767225133
    MIX3skew/1  base starts 6 ll -1196.390373862 | lane starts 12 ll -1169.541757363 free -1169.541757356
    MIX3skew/3  base starts 6 ll -1159.829375533 | lane starts 12 ll -1132.801613294 free -1134.133345337
    HMM2sym/2   base starts 4 ll -1409.171527503 | lane starts 6 ll -1409.171527503 free -1409.171527507

All 12 lane fits reach their own default start's fit within 9e-9 or
beat it (MIX3skew seed 3 by 1.33); on base, 9 of 12 ended 0.075 to
26.85 below base's default-start fit.
The extra pass costs 0.6 to 1.2 s on the 3-component mixtures. A gain
threshold (`se_edge_tol`) would stop rounding-bought passes; optional.

Fits with no predictor on a declared point are untouched:
`quadseam2-review-ident.R`, 18 fits (gaussian lm and glmm, poisson and
bernoulli glmm, bernoulli and poisson one-block quadrature, poisson
quadrature at true sd 0.05, bernoulli quadrature at sd 0, skew_normal
glmm and quadrature at alpha 4, two mixtures, nested quadrature, crossed
quadrature refusal, `hmm(2, gaussian())`, `hmm(2, skew_normal())` and
two symmetric skew-normal fits at default starts): `FIT lines identical:
18`, `SE lines identical: 17` (the crossed fit refuses on both, with the
same message).

### 2. Item 4861, quad_sd_up_escape()

It runs only where an sd sits below 0.01 of its scale. On the design of
item 561 (`quadseam2-review-touch.R`, six families, true sd 0 and 0.05,
seeds 1 to 10; 72 of 120 fits end below sd 1e-3): logLik bitwise equal
on 120 of 120, two runs; 0 restarts kept. Timing is lost in the
machine's load (the two runs disagree in sign: lane 2.0 times and 0.74
times base), so `quadseam2-review-time.R` measured median-of-3 inside
the new paths: 0.00 s in `quad_sd_up_escape()` on all 5 cases. The
stall it fixes is the lane's own test (seen failing on base). Nested
quadrature is excluded by `quad_nested` and was bitwise equal.

Not a defect, a note: the restart's opt replaces `fit$opt` without the
earlier `stationary_escape` record and without priors2's new
`check_map_unbounded()` (see merges).

# Review of lane ordinal (2026-09-30)

Worktree `frmtmb-wt-ordinal`, branch `wt-ordinal`, uncommitted against
57c25589 (frmtmb 0.66.0). Base build `rellib-r4`. Reference brms 2.23.0.
Every script and log named here is in `dev/` of the worktree, prefixed
`ordinal-rev-`.

## Verdict: NOT MERGEABLE

The likelihood work is right. disc, both threshold structures and acat
off the logit match brms's compiled program on 18 new shapes, and the
estimation of flexible fits is unchanged to the bit. Three things
block the merge:

1. A loop-closure bug in frmtmb.sample breaks draws of multivariate
   ordinal models. It is a regression for flexible thresholds, and it
   gives a silently wrong `delta` for equidistant thresholds.
2. The family-default `disc` now shows in the output of every flexible
   ordinal fit. brms shows it in none of them.
3. One new refusal fires on a model that brms declares valid, and its
   message says that brms refuses it.

## Blockers

### B1. frmtmb.sample: `draws_ordinal_cols()` captures `K` and `dd` from the loop frame

`extensions/frmtmb.sample/R/draws-brms.R`, `draws_ordinal_cols()`. Each
`local()` block captures `map` or `inv`, but the closures read `K` and
`dd` lazily from the function frame. After the loop, that frame holds
the values of the LAST ordinal block. A univariate model has one block,
so the bug is invisible there.

- **Regression (flexible thresholds).** Take a multivariate model with
  two ordinal responses that have different threshold counts, for
  example `list(cumulative(), sratio())` on 4 and 3 categories.
  `posterior_epred(ds, resp = "y")` works on rellib-r4. On the lane it
  stops with "number of items to replace is not a multiple of
  replacement length" at `m[, j[seq_len(R)]] <- v`. Sum-to-zero on the
  second response fails the same way. Equidistant on the first
  response fails with "length of 'dimnames' [2] not equal to array
  extent". Script: `ordinal-rev-mvdraws.R`, logs
  `ordinal-rev-log-mvdraws-{base,lane}.txt`. Data seed 20261009,
  sampler seed 5.
- **Silent wrong answer.** The model is
  `list(cumulative(threshold = "equidistant"),
  sratio(threshold = "equidistant"))` with 5 categories each. The
  `delta_y` column of the draws holds `log(delta)`. Its mean is 0.691,
  while the stored `b_y_Intercept` columns are spaced 2.000 apart. The
  largest error is 1.48, and ML `summary()` gives 1.98. `delta_y2` is
  correct, with an error of 0. Script: `ordinal-rev-mvsilent.R`, log
  `ordinal-rev-log-mvsilent.txt`. Data seed 20261010, sampler seed 5.
  `ordinal-rev-mvdebug.R` shows the maps.
- **Fix.** Bind `K` and `dd` inside each `local()` (`dd <- dd`,
  `K <- K`), or build the closures in a factory function. Add a
  frmtmb.sample test with two ordinal responses that have different
  threshold counts and different families (one ordered, one not), and
  check `posterior_epred()` against brms's `d*()` at the stored columns
  (the pattern in `ordinal-rev-draws.R`).

### B2. The family-default `disc` leaks into every flexible ordinal fit

The criterion was that flexible fits and other families stay
unchanged. `ordinal-rev-bitwise.R` fits 26 models under each arm and
`ordinal-rev-bitwise-compare.R` compares 581 outputs: 530 are
`identical()`. Every difference is on an ordinal model, and the
estimation is identical on all of them (`opt$par`, objective, logLik,
`fitted()`, `predict()`, `simulate()`, `emmeans`, `confint()`,
`variables()`, `conditional_effects()`, frmtmb.sample draws). What
changed on every flexible cumulative, sratio, cratio and acat fit:

- `summary()` and `print()` now read
  `Links: cdf = logit; disc = log` and `Fixed dpar: disc = 1`.
  `brms:::summarise_links()` gives `mu = logit` and names the disc link
  only when disc is modeled (`ordinal-rev-brmslinks.R`).
- `fixef(flatten = TRUE)` gains `disc_(Intercept) = 0`, and so does the
  default statistic of `frm_bootstrap()`. `print()`, `confint()` and
  `summary()` of the bootstrap each show a row of zeros.
- `coef()` of a multivariate fit gains a `y_disc` component.
- The invariant that the `fixef()` man page shows as an example,
  `all(names(fixef(fit, flatten = TRUE)) %in% rownames(confint(fit)))`,
  changes from TRUE to FALSE, because `confint()` has no disc row
  (`ordinal-rev-reach.R`, logs `ordinal-rev-log-reach-{base,lane}.txt`).
  `hurdle_cumulative()` already broke it on the base, so the lane
  spreads an existing wart to the four common families.

Reach measured as unchanged: `emmeans` (univariate), `confint()`,
`variables()`, `conditional_effects()`, and frmtmb.sample `variables()`,
draws, `fixef()` and `summary()` of flexible fits.

**Recommendation.** Keep the mapped coefficient (the objective needs
it), but mark disc as a family default that the user did not write.
For example, `ord_family_tail()` sets `fam[["hidden_fixed_dpars"]] <-
"disc"`, and `hurdle_cumulative()` does the same. Then filter that
dpar out where it shows, unless the formula models it:

- the `Fixed dpar` block of `summary()` and `print()`
  (`R/methods-fit.R`, about line 694, and `summary()$fixed_dpars`);
- `family_link_str()`, so that the Links line matches brms;
- `fixef(flatten = TRUE)` (`R/methods-fit.R`), which also fixes the
  default statistic of `frm_bootstrap()`, the table of `allFit`
  (`R/allfit.R:127`) and the man-page invariant;
- the per-dpar list of `coef()`.

Tests to revert or update: `test-thres-refit.R` (back to 4 and 1 +
nraw columns), the link string in `test-ordinal.R`, and the pin on
`summary()$fixed_dpars$disc` in `test-xbeta-zibb-hurdle-cum.R`. Update
NEWS too. Leave the standata view (`lp$constant`), the `parList()`
adjustments in `test-mv-gaps.R` and `test-ordinal-draws-names.R`, and
the fixed shape of `whittle()` as they are. The shape of `whittle()` is
data preparation and correctly visible.

### B3. The sum-to-zero refusal fires when every `thres(gr = )` level has one threshold

`thres_structure_check()` refuses `sum(nthres - 1L) < 1L`. That
condition includes a grouped model in which every level has two
categories. For grouped thresholds, brms declares
`array[ngrthres] int<lower=1> nthres`
(`ordinal-rev-grdecl.R`, `ordinal-rev-log-grdecl.txt`), so the model
is valid there: all thresholds are 0 and `b` and `disc` are still
estimated. The refusal message also says "brms's program declares at
least two thresholds and does not run either". That is true only
without `thres(gr = )`. Probe: `ordinal-rev-refusals.R`, row
"thres(gr): every level ONE threshold".

The case is narrow. Under the rule for this review ("a refusal that
fires on a correct model is a blocker") it blocks, and the user can
downgrade it. The better fix is to fit it with an empty `tau_raw`. If
the fix stays a refusal, restrict the "brms does not run it" clause to
the ungrouped case and name the grouped case under Differences from
brms.

## Minors

- **m1. Test gaps found by mutation.** `ordinal-rev-mutants.R` built
  11 mutants in scratch libraries. `ordinal-rev-mut-par.sh` ran them
  over `ordinal-rev-jobs-mut.txt`, and the table is in
  `ordinal-rev-log-mut-table.txt`. Seven mutants are caught by the
  ungated tests: the offset of delta (`seq_len(k) * d`), the sign of
  delta, both sum-to-zero centerings, the value map of `delta`, the
  prior scale of `delta`, and disc in `ord_cat_probs()`. Four escape:
  - M2 (no disc on the acat logit data path), M2b (no disc in the
    grouped sequential density) and M2c (no disc for cratio) pass
    every ungated test. Only the gated `test-brms-likelihood.R` catches
    them, with 3, 1 and 1 failures. The cause is structural: each test
    compares at the mutant's own optimum. A disc coefficient that the
    density ignores stays at its start value of 0, so disc is 1 and
    brms's density agrees. For M2, logLik moves from -452.277 to
    -453.546 and `disc_z` from 0.910 to 0
    (`ordinal-rev-mutdemo.R`, logs `ordinal-rev-log-mutdemo-*.txt`,
    data seed 20260930).
  - M3b (wrong inverse sum-to-zero map for sratio, cratio and acat) is
    caught by NO test. It makes frmtmb.sample's `posterior_epred()`
    wrong by 0.246 in probability and changes `log_lik()`: sample-test
    data seed 62, sampler seed 3, same script.

  Fix: evaluate each density at a chosen parameter vector with disc
  different from 1 (`obj$fn(par)` against brms's `d*()`), and in the
  sample test compare `posterior_epred()` of the sum-to-zero draws with
  brms at the stored columns. The lane code is correct on every such
  check I ran (`ordinal-rev-draws.R`: 6 univariate shapes, at most
  2.8e-16).
- **m2. Grouped flexible thresholds are not bitwise.** For
  `cumulative("cloglog")` with `thres(gr = h)`, the threshold estimates
  differ by 7.1e-15 relative. The `fixef()` Est.Error differs by up to
  5.1e-11 and Q2.5 by 2.8e-9, and `vcov()` differs by 1.2e-9
  (`ordinal-rev-log-bitwise-mag.txt`). The cause is the new
  `thres_tau()` path under the delta method. `opt$par`, logLik and
  fitted stay identical. NEWS says "unchanged to the bit (estimates,
  ...)": qualify it, or keep the old `ord_tau_from_raw()` per slice for
  flexible grouped thresholds.
- **m3. The record of brms defect 1 is inconsistent.** The comment in
  `test-brms-likelihood.R` says cratio cloglog with disc "has a NaN
  gradient at every point tried". The findings say the gradient is
  finite at other points. The NaN depends on the data.
  `exp(-exp(th))` underflows above about th = 6.6, so `q_k` is exactly
  0 and `p_k = log1m_exp(0) = -Inf` on a threshold below the observed
  category. That term is not returned, but it poisons the gradient. The
  worker's data has 1 such row (th = 8.75). Mine have none, so brms's
  gradient is finite (largest 1.9e-4) and the lp identity is exact
  (`ordinal-rev-defects.R`, `ordinal-rev-lp2.R`). The defect is real,
  but the comment needs fixing, and a gated cratio cloglog row with
  disc can run on data without such rows.
- **m4. A sixth brms defect, not recorded.**
  `hurdle_cumulative_logit_lpmf`, which brms uses when disc is modeled
  under the logit, tests `y == nthres + 2` for the top category. The
  probit branch uses `nthres + 1`. The top category therefore reads
  `thres[nthres + 1]`, "index 5 out of range", whenever it is observed
  (`ordinal-rev-hurdlecheck.R`, `ordinal-rev-log-hurdlecheck.txt`,
  `ordinal-rev-log-lp2.txt`). With disc held at 1, brms uses
  `hurdle_cumulative_ordered_logistic` and is not affected. Defect 4 is
  also wider than recorded: a multivariate model with ONE equidistant
  response fails too ("delta_y not in scope"), and so does a mixture
  (`ordinal-rev-log-defects.txt`).
- **m5. NEWS omissions.** The `y_disc` component of multivariate
  `coef()` and the non-bitwise grouped threshold summaries of m2 are
  not in NEWS.
- **m6. The disc-intercept warning misses cell-means spellings.**
  `disc ~ 0 + h` spans the intercept but gets no warning (the probe in
  `ordinal-rev-refusals.R`). The pattern exists in the hurdle on the
  base, and the lane extends it to four families.
- **m7. `default_prior()` differs from `get_prior()`.** `delta` shows
  `lb = NA` where brms shows `lb = 0` for the ordered families, and
  under `thres(gr = )` no per-level `Intercept` rows are listed
  (`ordinal-rev-priors.R`). The structure enforces the bound. Both
  differences are display only.

### Remaining notes (not blocking)

- **`par_template()` still lists the mapped disc.** On every flexible
  ordinal fit it shows `disc_(Intercept)` (marked "held at a constant,
  not optimized"). These are the 6 differing elements above: the
  template's names and block lengths on the 3 ordinal models. The
  template is internal vocabulary for `start =` and `newparams =`, and
  it showed the same entry for the hurdle on the base. A refit from it
  behaves the same as on the base: logLik is 1.3e-12 relative on both
  arms. It could be hidden too, but then `start =` would have to fill
  in the mapped entry.
- **Draws column order.** In multivariate draws, the threshold and
  `delta` columns that a structure adds come after all sampled columns
  (for example, `b_y_Intercept[3]` after `b_y2_Intercept[2]`). This is
  the documented mixture-weight pattern, and it is cosmetic.

## A. Required fixes

### 1. wiener() identity-link support guard is a no-op on the tape (frmtmb.eam)

`ddm_support_guard()` adds `0 / (x + |x|)`. A constant zero divided by a
variable folds to 0 when RTMB records the tape:

```text
[wguard.txt]  tape of 0 / (p + |p|) at p = -1: 0 ; plain: NaN
```

So under `link_bias = "identity"` or `link_ndt = "identity"` the
objective is finite outside the support (fit `bf(rt | dec(upper) ~ 1)`,
seed 20261016, n = 60, all three links identity; optimum objective
14.53702):

```text
[wguard2.txt]
betad[2] = -0.5: objective 185.0903        (ndt = -0.5)
betad[3] = 1.2: objective 43.59577         (bias = 1.2)
betad[3] = -0.5: objective -10.57031       (bias = -0.5: logLik +10.57,
                                            above the MLE's -14.54)
```

Fits reach it. Data with bias 0.97 (seeds 20261101 to 20261105, n = 80,
`wiener(link_bias = "identity")` against the default logit,
`dev/famuni-rev-wguard2.R`):

```text
[wguard3.txt]
seed 1: identity bias 1.01265 logLik 315.5099 (off-tape density sum NaN) | logit bias 0.98811 logLik 315.5099
seed 3: identity bias 1.02771 logLik 334.7153 (off-tape density sum NaN) | logit bias 0.96815 logLik 327.0756
seed 4: identity bias 1.02423 logLik 338.6626 (off-tape density sum NaN) | logit bias 0.96872 logLik 329.5999
```

3 of 5 fits end at a bias above 1 with convergence code 0 (the probe
muffled warnings, so I do not claim there were none). On seeds 3 and 4
the reported logLik is 7.64 and 9.06 above the valid maximum and is not
a likelihood. `?wiener` and eam's NEWS say the
density "is NaN outside those ranges, which the optimizer treats as a
rejected step". `test-wiener-links.R` ("an identity link returns NaN
outside the support") evaluates `family(fid)$lpdf` on doubles only, so it
passes on the defective build.

Fix: a guard that does not fold, for example
`(x + abs(x)) / (x + abs(x)) - 1` (exactly 0 for x > 0, NaN otherwise,
no warning on doubles), and a test that evaluates `fit$obj$fn()` at an
out-of-support parameter and is seen to fail on the current build.

### 2. frechet(link_nu = "identity") at nu <= 1 is finite on the tape (core)

`frechet_log_scale()` adds `0 * log(nu - 1)`, which folds the same way:

```text
[frechet.txt]
plain doubles, nu = 0.8: NaN NaN NaN
on the tape, nu = 0.8: -0.5425312 -1.428431 -3.120487
```

At nu = 0.8, `lgamma(1 - 1/nu)` is `log|gamma(-0.25)|`, a finite and
wrong scale. `?frmtmb-extreme-families` says "the density is NaN there,
as brms's is"; that holds on doubles only. No fit in my probe crossed
nu = 1 (nu0 1.15: identity and logm1 fits agree, logLik -150.5084), so
this is a latent defect, but the documented guard does not exist where
the optimizer looks. Same fix as 1, with a tape test.

### 3. GEV link_xi = "identity" runs past xi = -1 (core)

The soft support caps the edge at `t = 1e-15`, so for xi < -1 the
log density of the row at the upper end is bounded (about
`-(1 + 1/xi) log(1e-15)`, 10.9 at xi = -1.5 and 21.7 at xi = -3 for
the probe data) instead of unbounded. Under the identity link nothing
stops the optimizer there (`dev/famuni-rev-gevsupport.R`, section C,
n = 60, `y ~ 1`):

```text
[gevsupport2.txt]
xi_true -0.80 | identity: conv 1 logLik -70.5872 xi -1.0067 min t -1.021e-14 true ll -Inf | fgev ll -70.8471 xi -0.9764
xi_true -0.95 | identity: conv 1 logLik -67.6369 xi -1.0075 min t -7.550e-15 true ll -Inf | fgev ll -69.1151 xi -0.8623
```

Both end with the largest observation OUTSIDE the support (min t < 0),
so the true log-likelihood is -Inf while `logLik()` (and so AIC) reports
-70.59 and -67.64. Both do warn (false convergence, gradient 1e+08 at
`sigma_(Intercept)`). The help page says "a fit with one component ends
with every row inside it", which is false under this link, and it
offers `link_xi = "identity"` as "the other choice brms allows" without
saying that it reaches the region where, by the page's own argument, no
maximum exists. Fix: make `xi <= -1` NaN on the tape under the identity
link (with a non-folding guard), or refuse a scalar `xi` on the identity
link, and qualify the sentence. Under the default `log1p` link every fit
I ran ended inside the support (section B1).

`Intercept`, dpar `xi` here and class `xi` for a scalar). Fix: key the

row to the new test. (Gaussian `sigma ~ 1` has the same pre-existing gap:

lane's.)

## C. Observations, not blocking

1. **Frechet near nu = 1.** 60 seeds per arm, n = 300, `y ~ x`
   (`frechet.txt`, `frechet11.txt`): at nu0 = 1.3 every fit converged,
   coverage 0.917 to 0.967. At nu0 = 1.1, seed 31 ran to nu = 1 + 5e-9
   with the mean intercept at 17.38 (exp 17.38 = 3.5e7) and NaN SEs for
   it and nu, flagged by the "Standard errors are not available" warning
   and not by the boundary message. It is brms's parameterization: when
   the sample's best nu is below 1 the mean is infinite and the maximum
   is at the edge.
2. **Outside GEV rows near the exp(700) cap** have a non-finite
   gradient at x in [-20.26, -19.47] (xi 0.06, sigma 1; 80 of 401 grid
   points, `numerics2.txt`), with |gradient| up to 3.5e289 just inside
   the band. In a mixture the adjoint reaching such a row is 0 and every
   local partial is finite, so it does not propagate; 0 of 40 mixtures
   failed. A single-component fit only gets there on a rejected step.
   Latent; worth a comment beside `exp_neg_capped()`.
3. **Non-regular region.** For -1 < xi < -0.5 the help page says the SEs
   "do not have their usual meaning", but fits there return SEs with no
   run-time message (xi -0.63 and -0.89 in A, -0.78 and -0.83 in B).
4. **Rows with 0 < t <= 1e-15** are scored at t = 1e-15 (the clamp
   starts there, not at 0). Harmless at double precision, but "inside the
   support the term is exactly 0" is true only above 1e-15.
5. **Rounding-decided ground (item 9).** The new tests compare against
   eps-scaled bounds or seeded fits at 4 SE; none sits on the `t = 1e-15`
   kink or the |z| = 1 clamps, and the xi = 0 blend is continuous, not a
   branch. The `identical(Fo, 0 or 1)` CDF test has margins of 1e-37 or
   better on its grid. The real test gap is fixes 1 and 2: guards tested
   on doubles, not on the tape.

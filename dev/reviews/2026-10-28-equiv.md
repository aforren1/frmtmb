## Findings

### 1. Two refusals and one unmeasured method are scored as agreement (major)

Evidence (`refusal-check.R`, `p167.R`, `l2err.R`):

- **p158 fcor() x nl, "refusal correct".** The spec writes `fcor(M)` in
  the nl body, where brms 2.23.0 fails with `object 'M' not found`: an
  artifact of the spelling, not a refusal. brms's spelling,
  `bf(y ~ b1 * exp(b2 * x), b1 + b2 ~ 1, nl = TRUE, autocor = ~ fcor(M))`,
  builds AND compiles. frmtmb refuses it ("Residual correlation terms are
  not supported in a nonlinear (nl = TRUE) formula"). The compat note
  records the decision ("brms reaches this model through acformula(),
  which has no analog here"), so the verdict is divergence by decision.
- **p167 poisson x arma(), "refusal correct".** The spec uses the default
  `cov = FALSE`, for which brms refuses an MA part ("Please set cov =
  TRUE when modeling MA structures for this family"). With `cov = TRUE`
  brms builds and compiles the model; frmtmb refuses it, as its compat
  note records. Divergence by decision.
- **p029 mi() x frm_curve, "equivalent" at L4.** L2 never ran: the
  Fixed_param brmsfit failed (`no translation rule for Stan parameter
  Ymi`), so frm_curve was not compared with anything. The verdict rests
  on fitted means at L4, which do not exercise frm_curve. Cannot measure.

A systematic read of all 172 specs (`specdump.R`, `specdump.txt`) found no
other pair whose model omits one of its two features. p044 (s() x REML)
is the borderline case: REML has no brms analog, so only the model is
checked (finding 4).

Required fix: reclassify the three pairs (spell p158 and p167 as brms
does in their specs, so the flow sees brms build them); make the flow
refuse to score a pair "equivalent" when the method half of a method pair
reached no level. Corrected rates (`rate-fix.R`):

| reading | agree with brms, percent |
|---|---|
| lane, as reported | 85.1 [79.9, 90.3] |
| p158, p167 divergence by decision; p029 cannot measure | **83.6 [78.2, 89.0]** |
| the same, measured pairs only | 91.3 |
| the same, also without the L4-posterior-only pairs and REML (finding 4) | 80.5 [74.5, 86.6] |

The refused stratum falls from 18 of 20 to 16 of 20.

### 2. The compat-row corrections miss the nl x family rule (major)

The table says nl is refused for every core family but gaussian (47
rows, the rule `nl x kind:family`). I fitted each family three ways on
one data set (n = 300, seed 4901; `d1-width.R`, `d1-width.log`): the
predictor written linearly, the same predictor as an nl body, and the
intercept-only model. brms 2.23.0 `make_stancode()` was run on the nl
spelling.

| outcome | families |
|---|---|
| nl honored (nl logLik equals the linear logLik), row says refused | 34: Gamma, lognormal, weibull, exponential, frechet, inverse.gaussian, shifted_lognormal, student, skew_normal, asym_laplace, exgaussian, gen_extreme_value, von_mises, poisson, negbinomial, nbinom1, geometric, discrete_weibull, hurdle_poisson, hurdle_negbinomial, zero_inflated_poisson, zero_inflated_negbinomial, binomial, beta_binomial, zero_inflated_binomial, zero_inflated_beta_binomial, bernoulli, beta, xbeta, zero_inflated_beta, zero_one_inflated_beta, hurdle_gamma, hurdle_lognormal, tweedie |
| body DROPPED (nl logLik equals intercept-only), row says refused | 5: cumulative, sratio, cratio, acat, hurdle_cumulative (D1) |
| frm() refuses as the note says; brms builds | 5: categorical, multinomial, dirichlet, logistic_normal, dirichlet_multinomial |
| not tried (constructor not found by that name) | negbinomial2, compois, dirichlet2 |

brms builds all 34 except nbinom1 and tweedie, which brms does not have.
The lane proposes corrections for 2 of these rows (p166, p171). Its
sentence "The rule is enforced for no family the survey met" is true of
the sample and misleading about the table: the rule is enforced for the
five multi-mu families and for nothing else.

D1 is confirmed and is exactly as wide as the lane says: every ordinal
family, hurdle_cumulative included (the lane's repro does not run
hurdle_cumulative; this table does: nl -471.115 equals intercept-only
-471.115, against -463.832 linear). No non-ordinal family drops the body.

Required fix: list the 34 rows as compat-row corrections (works, or
untested until an L1 sweep), the 5 ordinal rows as broken (D1), and the 5
multi-mu rows as correctly refused; for p166 propose "works, no brms
reference" rather than "untested", since nbinom1's nl fit equals its
linear fit (-439.141 both).

### 3. The exact gp() "divergence by decision" verdicts are not supported (major)

p022, p023, p094 and p109 are adjudicated divergences by the recorded
nugget choice (NEWS 0.51.0, `R/covstruct.R` `gp_nugget`), and p136's L1
gap is set aside for the same reason. The record asserts that the nugget
predicts a gap of this size; it does not measure it on these pairs.

I set frmtmb's `gp_nugget` to brms's size in a scratch process
(`assignInNamespace`, nothing installed; `gp-nugget.R`) and remeasured
p094 (lognormal x gp) with the lane's instrument:

| frmtmb nugget | L1 constant at the estimate | at the two stepped points |
|---|---|---|
| 1e-6 (shipped) | -94.43 | -91.72, -98.14 |
| 1e-9 | -83.81 | -82.79, -86.19 |
| 1e-12 | -83.74 | -81.93, -86.61 |

Matching the nugget removes about 11 of the 94 nats, and the remaining 84
do not move between 1e-9 and 1e-12. Either the instrument cannot map
the exact gp() latent field, or the two joint densities differ for
another reason. Neither is the recorded decision.

Required fix: score the four pairs "cannot measure" (the no-defect rate
falls from 87.9 to 86.3 [81.2, 91.4]); state in the record that the gap
is unexplained; file the gap for the next round (translator or model).
The agree rate does not change, as none of these was counted as agree.

### 4. The L4 posterior route is weak evidence on penalized models (minor)

Ten agree verdicts (12 draws) rest on L4 alone. Five (7 draws: p005,
p030, p068, p128, p129) use the optimizing route, whose maxima agree to
1e-9 to 1.6e-6 nats. That is strong evidence. The other five (p029,
p042, p047, p136, p143; 5 draws) use fitted means against the posterior.
p029 is finding 1. For the four penalized ones the margin is thin. My
mutant of p042 that cuts the smooth to `s(z, k = 4)` reads max |z| 0.974
(median 0.401, Rhat 1.01). The lane's own control, a true L1 identity
(`y1 ~ t2(x, z)`), reads 0.851. The bound of 0.5 sits between, so the
route can fail an identity and pass a modest misfit. p136 passes at
0.493. The REML pair p044 is scored "equivalent" on the model alone,
which no brms quantity can check.

Required fix: report the rate with these verdicts counted and without
them (80.5 [74.5, 86.6], table in finding 1), and flag L4-posterior-only
verdicts in `results.tsv`.

### 5. The decision standard is not applied the same way to D5 (minor)

The flow counts a compat note as a decision record for p156, p164, p167
and p171 (`eq_decision_autocor`, `eq_decision_nl`). The refusals behind
D5 are compat notes too: `|ID| x mm()` is "Refused: a merged |ID| block
indexes one level set per observation row, and an mm() row loads several
levels at once", and `gr_cov x mm()` is "Refused: mm() supports the
default (us) and diag structures only". The sampled rows (`|ID| x
mmc()`, `gr_cov x mmc()`) say conditional, so the lane is right that the
rows are wrong. But by its own standard, D5 is a divergence by decision
plus two wrong compat rows, not a defect. Neither reading is agree, so
the agree rate does not move.

Required fix: choose one standard (a compat note is a decision, or only
NEWS/SPEC is) and apply it to every pair. Then restate D5 and the five
decision-flagged pairs to match.

### 9. The bar_crossing reach pattern counts a different feature (minor)

The `bar_crossing` pattern matches nested grouping, `(1 | g1/g2)`,
`(1 | g1:g2)` and `(1 | recipe:replicate)` (brms_multilevel.ltx,
brms_overview.ltx; `weights-audit.R`). The compat feature is the refused
`x * (1 | g)` crossing. That gives weight 3 where about 1 is right, and
four of the 20 refused draws are bar_crossing pairs. Weight 1 moves the
corrected headline from 83.6 to 83.3 (`rate-fix.R`, by a ratio
reweighting of the drawn pairs). In the same way, frm_curve's weight of
18 comes from `conditional_effects()` in 16 of its 17 documents, while its
L2 reference is `posterior_linpred()`. The record states this analog; it
should also state that the reference is not conditional_effects().

Required fix: correct the pattern and redraw at the next frame change;
until then, state the effect.

### 10. D2's remedy is exact (minor, the record understates D2)

The adjudication says the survey "did not measure" frmtmb's spelling
against brms's class "sds" prior. I measured it. frm() with
`set_prior('student_t(3, 0, 1)', class = 'sd', group = 's(x)')`, against
brms with `class = 'sds'`, is an L1 identity: constant -0.667 at all
three points, gradient 1.6e-13 (`mutate.R d2_remedy`, with the sds_
block given its log-chart Jacobian as sd is). The fix for D2 is an alias
of "sds" to that spelling, and the proposal "conditional" now has
evidence.

Required fix: record the measurement and the alias as D2's fix.

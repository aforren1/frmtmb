# Review of lane gpfield (round of 2026-11-13, base ec8178d3 = 0.82.0)

Adversarial review. Lane tree `frmtmb-wt-gpfield` (uncommitted), library
`wt-gpfield-lib` (install7), base `rellib-r21`. Scripts
`dev/reviews/gpfield-review-*.R|sh`, logs `dev/reviews/gpfield-review-out/`.
No git writes, nothing installed, no lane file edited.

## Verdict: FIX

Three items must be fixed before merge (F1 to F3); the rest are minor.
The kernels, the kriging law, the bit-identity of exp_quad fits and the
merges hold.

## FIX items

**F1. The new gp warnings are lost when the nonlinear sign probe returns
its refit (R/fit.R:1975-1989 with R/par-template.R nl_fit_probed()).**
`gp_end_messages()` runs only under `announce_start`. `nl_fit_probed()`
drops the first fit's warnings when `nl_sign_probe()` wins and returns
`pr$fit`, which ran `run(st, FALSE)` (par-template.R:1527), so neither
`frmtmb_gp_laplace` nor `frmtmb_gp_basis` (nor the old
`frmtmb_gp_nugget`) is ever computed for the returned fit. The relin
(`relin = gp_body && announce_start && cold_start`) does not run there
either.

Reproducer (`gpfield-review-dbg2.R`, `dbg2b.txt`, `dbg2c.txt`;
`tk-miss.txt` for importance sampling, lane's `gf_is()`, 20000 draws,
seed 9001): `gf_row("sweep:mm_V:bernoulli:40:1")` of
dev/gpfield-rows.R, `frm()` on wt-gpfield-lib:

    gp_end_messages call 1 returned 0
    at fit end: inner_pd TRUE | tk -0.004760924 | beta 81979,118493   (the first fit, on the ridge)
    ROW sweep:mm_V:bernoulli:40:1 conv 0 nwarn 0
    direct gp_end_messages: 1 frmtmb_gp_laplace                        (on the returned fit)
    tk 60.22554
    final beta 6.999656 -0.978215
    TK sweep:mm_V:bernoulli:40:1 ll -17.6078 conv 0 nwarn 0 theta 3.012,-3.378 | pkg tk 60.2255 | is -39.7473 ess 4 is-lap -22.1395

Expected: `frmtmb_gp_laplace` (the lane's own rule reads tk 60 > 1 on the
returned fit; a white field, ls/mednn about 0.2, sd 20, Laplace about 22
above importance sampling, the 5112 class). Actual: code 0, no warning,
on lane and base alike (pb sweep: base and lane -17.607794 nwarn 0).
Fix: compute `gp_end_messages()` on the fit that `nl_fit_probed()`
returns, beside its `limit()` call (as `nl_limit_message()` already is),
and on `nl_fit_off_saddle()`'s refit path; coordinate with nlstop2, which
owns par-template.R. Add the row as a test (it fits in about 24 s:
`skip_on_cran()`). The NEWS and the 5112 CLOSED line say "both bernoulli
white fields warn"; a third one in the same 122-row sweep does not.

**F2. predict() on an exact gp() is 6 to 20 times slower, unmeasured
and undocumented (R/predict-brms.R gp_field_draw()).** One inner solve
per replicate (`obj$fn()` on the tape of the dense kernel). The lane's
record says only "one inner solve per replicate". Measured
(`gpfield-review-cost.R`, `cost-*-g500.txt`, `cost-*-p300.txt`; base and
lane in separate processes, same seed; per-draw cost from 100 and 50
draws, two repeats):

| fit | call | base s/draw | lane s/draw | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| gaussian n 500 | newdata 6 rows | 0.095 to 0.179 | 0.451 to 0.545 | 3 to 5.7 |
| gaussian n 500 | no newdata | 0.036 | 0.392 | 11 |
| poisson n 300 | newdata 6 rows | 0.021 to 0.076 | 0.280 to 0.358 | 4.7 to 13 |
| poisson n 300 | no newdata | 0.012 | 0.239 | 21 |

At the default `ndraws = 1000`, `predict(fit)` on the gaussian n 500 fit
takes about 390 s against 36 s (the fit itself took 44 s). Memory is not
the issue (122 MB against 117 MB). Fix: either make it cheaper (for a
gaussian identity mean the inner mode is closed form, `z = sd L'
(sd^2 K + sigma^2 I)^-1 (y - X beta)`, one LAPACK factor of n x n, about
20 ms at n 500; elsewhere the inner Newton from the fit's mode could be
capped at one or two steps), or record the numbers and say in
`?predict.frmtmb_fit` that an exact field costs one inner solve per draw.
The lane must at least measure and document it.

**F3. NEWS: the two new-warning bullets are not marked BREAKING
(NEWS.md:30 and :37).** 0.82.0's precedent marks a new warning on fits
that were silent as BREAKING ("BREAKING: `predict()` warns, with class
`frmtmb_predict_na`", NEWS.md:542; NEWS.md:197). `frmtmb_gp_basis` fires
on 13 of 30 ordinary approximate fits (below) and needed 9 test sites
changed; it is a behavior change by the round's rule. Otherwise the
section is right: `# frmtmb (development version)`, no lane names, no
`dev/` paths, no version numbers, BREAKING on the predict(), mean relin
and approximate relin bullets.

## Minor items

m1. **State leak in gp_field_draw() (R/predict-brms.R, new function).**
It restores only `last.par` and `last.par.best`. `obj_state_save()` /
`obj_state_restore()` (R/se-check.R:287) exist for this and predraw2's
`draw_logratio()` uses them. Measured (`gpfield-review-predraw2.R lane`,
`pd-lane.txt`): after one `predict(ndraws = 500)` `last.par.ok` differs
from before on every exact fit (g30, p80, b80, m32), not on the
approximate one. Use the helpers.

m2. **frmtmb_gp_laplace near its gate on ordinary bernoulli fits.**
`gpfield-review-laplace.R white` (`lap-white.txt`): 40 bernoulli
`y ~ gp(x)` fits, n 30 to 120. Of the 20 with `2 sin(3x)`, 2 warn: s1
(n 50) tk 1.51 against importance sampling 0.90 (ess 137 of 10000), s13
(n 50) tk 1.08 against 0.70 (ess 333). Five more sit at tk 0.78 to 0.90.
So tk overstates the error by about 1.5x here, and the rule fires on
ordinary data where the measured error is under the gate of 1. Not a
wrong warning by much, but it is a seed-decided call on common data.
Either raise the gate (st80 3.46, st160 1.72, two160 1.90 still warn at
1.5; wig160 1.0015 and exp_b student 1.37 would not), or record the
rate. No white-field stop occurred among the 40.

m3. **The doc of the Laplace check overstates its scope.** `?frmtmb-gp`
"The Laplace check" says the check runs where every random effect sits
in the field's predictor; the inner-Hessian branch of
`gp_laplace_message()` runs on every fit with an exact gp() (no scope),
reads the eigenvalues of ALL random effects, and names the gp() term.
A group effect in another predictor of a non-log-concave family can
trip it and be blamed on the field. Say so, or scope it.

m4. **test-gp-by.R allows a warning the fit does not give.** The mvbf fit
`fm` now allows `lost`, which includes "Standard errors are not
available"; on the test's data `fm` gives only `frmtmb_gp_basis` (and
0.82.0 gave nothing) (`gpfield-review-gpbymv.R`, `gpbymv-*2.txt`). Allow
only the basis text there. (The pre-existing `fs` allowance of the SE
warning is stale too: neither build gives it on the test's data; not
this lane's.)

m5. **Relin acceptance does not consult the Laplace check.**
`gp_relin_regular()` accepts a higher Laplace objective at a regular
point; it does not ask `gp_laplace_tk()`. On a student() or bernoulli
field a descent could move to a point where the Laplace value overstates
the marginal. No row of the sweeps did (no row moved up and newly warned),
so this is a suggestion, not a defect.

m6. **5319's filed design is stale against parity2.** The item says
frmtmb.sample's derived columns read only outer parameters, so brms's
zgp needs "a new kind of derived column". parity2's tree adds exactly
that kind: `draws_latres_cols()` in extensions/frmtmb.sample/R/draws-brms.R
(internal = the block's theta and its states, `map` appends derived
columns, `inv` takes them back), on the same `ordinal = c(...)` line
5319 would extend. Point the item at it.

### 4. frmtmb_gp_basis: how often, and is it true

`gpfield-review-basis.R` (`basis-lane.txt`, `basis-base.txt`): 30
gaussian fits, f drawn from an exact squared exponential GP with length
scale 0.7 to 3 on x in [0, 10], noise 0.3, n 50 to 200, k 5, 8, 10, 15,
20, 30. It fires on 13 of 30:

| k | 5 | 8 | 10 | 15 | 20 | 30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fires | 2/5 | 3/5 | 4/5 | 3/5 | 1/5 | 0/5 |

Every firing is a true limit: the profile log-likelihood (length scale
fixed, every other parameter re-optimized, two starts) at 0.05 of the
spacing is within 0.012 of the fit, and it falls monotonically above:
-0.03 to -0.31 at 0.25, -0.14 to -1.36 at 0.5, -0.90 to -7.02 at 1
spacing. So the data prefer the white limit; the fit is the maximum
likelihood, and its sdgp (16 to 182) and lscale (3e-5 to 1.6e-4 of the
spacing) are a point on a flat ridge. In all 13 the SE check reports
finite SEs for both (`se_lost 0`), so without this warning a user reads
sdgp = 155 with a standard error as an estimate. The test fits the lane
allowed and did not profile are the same kind
(`gpfield-review-testprof.R`, `testprof.txt`): marginaleffects k 15
-0.000 / -0.015 / -0.105 / -1.136 at 0.05 / 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 spacing;
gp-by `by = w` k 8 -0.004 / -0.097 / -0.446 / -2.072. The allowed
endings are valid.

Recommendation: keep it a warning, do not tighten the rule (no firing
was false), but mark it BREAKING (F3) and change two things. (a) The
text says "the field is white noise over the basis"; a reader takes that
as a rough field, while the fitted function is a smooth sum of k sines.
Say "every basis function gets the same prior weight". (b) File: the
SE check should treat this as a limit (sdgp and lscale SE NaN, reason
"limit", as 0.82.0 does for a coefficient at the end of its range);
then the warning could become a message. A message now would leave the
reported sdgp and lscale and their SEs unflagged.

## 5319

factor of n x n per draw, n at most 500), frmtmb's own z under an

and the variable order silent2 sets in sample.R (`draws_brms_order()`)

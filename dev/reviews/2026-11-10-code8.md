## Findings

### 1. BLOCKER: `room` is not the Newton gain; short stops get `frmtmb_converged_at_max`

`headroom_checked()` returns the probe's best gain (`headroom_probe()`)
in place of the quadratic model's prediction whenever the prediction is
above `grad_tol` and the probe finds at most `grad_tol`. The probe is a
LOWER bound on what is left (a few straight steps), so on a curved valley
it reports almost nothing while the stop is far short. `max_8` then
compares that number with `conv_at_max_tol`, and the message says "a
Newton step from the stop gains ...", which is not what was measured.

Evidence:

- `dev/review-code8-valley.R` (y = A exp(B x), x on [30, 31], n = 60,
  seeds 1 to 3; points on the valley floor at B = B_hat + m SE(B), the
  rest profiled, so the shortfall is exact). For m = 2, 3, 5: shortfall
  1.93 to 10.48, quadratic prediction 2.14 to 21.5, lane `conv_headroom()`
  1.19e-4 to 3.79e-4, and the lane's gradient verdict does not warn on
  any of the 18 points. Base: room = prediction, warns on all 18.
- `dev/review-code8-valley8.R` (same model, x on [100, 101], optim BFGS
  cut at maxit = k and reported as "false convergence (8)", the device of
  the lane's own `conv_fake8()`): the lane gives the classed message on 3
  stops that are 0.262 (seed 1, k 40), 0.0492 (seed 4, k 95) and 0.00288
  (seed 4, k 150) short, with "gains 3.72e-06", "1.44e-06", "2.94e-06".
  Base: Newton room 0.144, 0.17, 0.165, warning plus gradient warning.
  On 10 more stops 0.004 to 9.59 short the lane's warning says "so the
  estimates are at the maximum"; on seed 2 (9.59 short) the SE check then
  calls A, B and sigma "flat ... the data do not determine them".
- Natural optimizer return, no forced code: `dev/review-code8-valley-opt.R`,
  x0 100 seed 2, optim BFGS (maxit 5000, code 1), 8.005 short. Lane:
  "A Newton step from the stop gains ... so the estimates are at the
  maximum", no gradient warning, 3 of 3 SEs lost as flat. Base: code
  warning plus gradient warning, SEs finite.
- Default nlminb fits of the same model do not reach this
  (`dev/review-code8-valley-default.R`, 32 fits, none short on either
  build), so the trigger needs an iteration-limited or user optimizer.

Required: the code-8 message must rest on the quadratic model's gain
(`newton_room()` / the Cholesky prediction), never on the probe. The
probe may at most turn "short" into "unknown"; it cannot certify a
maximum. If Part C needs the probe for the ridge, restrict it to
directions the Ritz step measured flat, and keep the warning otherwise.
NEWS bullet 3 ("If no step gains more than grad_tol, the stop is the
maximum") states the flawed rule to users and must change with it.

## Second fix-round check: design-null directions in headroom_ritz_flat() (2026-11-10)

### Verdict: FIX

### Finding

1. BLOCKER: a design null is flat in the likelihood, not in the
   objective. `se_design_null()` returns the directions where `X d = 0`.
   A prior on those coefficients curves the objective along them, so a
   stop short along such a direction is now certified as the maximum.
   `headroom_off_flat()` removes the direction from the quadratic model
   and asks the objective only by straight steps of at most 10
   unit-diagonal lengths. Where the prior is weak, those steps are far
   shorter than the distance to the maximum along the direction.

   Construction: `dev/review-code8-fix-dnull.R` and `-dnull2.R`.
   - Model: `y ~ x + s(x)`, gaussian, n = 200, seed 5. `s(x).fx1` is the
     exact alias of `x`; the null direction loads on `(Intercept)`
     -0.255, `x` 0.050 and `s(x).fx1` -0.966.
   - Prior: `normal(0, psd)` on both `x` and `s(x).fx1`.
   - Optimizer: nlminb to the MAP, then a step of delta along the null
     direction, reported as code 8 or code 0. The shortfall is exact.
   - Lane build, psd 1e4:
     - Code 8, shortfall 0.00117 and 0.0187: the message
       `frmtmb_converged_at_max`, no warning.
     - Code 8, shortfall 0.117, 1.87 and 11.7: "so the estimates are at
       the maximum".
     - Code 0, shortfall 0.00117 to 11.7: no condition at all.
   - Lane build, psd 1000:
     - Code 8, shortfall 0.0186 and 0.117: called the maximum.
     - Code 0, shortfall 0.0186 and 0.117: silent.
   - Base build, same stops: the gradient warning at shortfall 0.0186
     to 11.7 (psd 1000) and at 1.87 and 11.7 (psd 1e4). No message on
     any stop.
   - With psd 1 to 100 the straight steps reach the prior's quadratic,
     and the lane catches every stop short by more than 1.2e-3.

   Fix: exempt a design-null direction only where nothing in the
   objective but the likelihood reads it. That rules out a prior on any
   coefficient it loads on (`b`, and a `dirichlet` on a `mo()` simplex),
   and any other penalty. Otherwise keep the quadratic model along it:
   its curvature there is the penalty's, which the quadratic model has
   exactly. The `mo()` gap that triggered the change (gradient 1e-11
   along the direction) keeps its benefit under that rule if the fit
   has no simplex prior. Add the construction above to test-se-conv.R
   as the guard's absent case.

I did not test a design null shared by another term or an nl body. In
this code the null is computed per linear predictor on its own
coefficients, and the prior case already breaks the exception.

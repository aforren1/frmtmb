## Verdict: NOT MERGEABLE (2 blockers)

The plot() work, the invalid-effect rule, the ordinal warning, the
`mi(idx = )` refusal, `posterior_samples(pars = )`, `nsamples()` and
`parnames()` are sound, and I found no regression on 29 models that use
no new option. The renamed-level copy of `R/ce-levels.R` is correct
where it is meant to act: I checked it on seven shapes, and on two of
them against brms at the same draws. Two defects block the merge:

- `fitted(sample_new_levels = "old_levels")` makes a new group from
  DIFFERENT seen groups when one grouping factor has two blocks. brms
  never does this, and the lane's records say the opposite.
- The renamed-level fallback has no precondition. A call that 0.66.0
  refused by name now returns an all-`NA` frame without a message.

## Blockers

B1. **`fitted(sample_new_levels = "old_levels")` chooses a seen level
per BLOCK, and brms chooses one per GROUPING FACTOR.**
`old_level_pick_add()` keys the choice by the block's first coefficient
index. brms's `get_new_rdraws()` runs once per group, so every term of
`g` reads the same seen level (source in `dev/ceplot-brmssrc/`). When
frmtmb holds one factor in two blocks, the new group takes its effects
from two different seen groups. Such a group does not exist in the data.

- `dev/ceplot-rev-oldlevels.R` (b), data seed 3, calls at seeds 1 to 12.
  The model is `bf(yl ~ x + (1 | g), sigma ~ (1 | g))`, lognormal, whose
  mean reads both terms. frmtmb reads one seen group for `mu` and
  `sigma` at 0 of 12 seeds; brms reads one at 12 of 12. frmtmb's `mu`
  level equals brms's at every seed; its `sigma` level is the next
  draw. At seed 1 the response mean is 1.42918, and that value is no
  seen group's mean.
- `dev/ceplot-rev-oldlevels2.R` uses `y ~ x + (1 + x || g)`, which
  frmtmb holds as 2 blocks. At none of 12 seeds does the answer at
  `x = 0` and `x = 1` equal a seen group's answer.
- The records are wrong about this. The findings (section 6) say the
  choice "differs only for a block shared by two responses". NEWS and
  the `?fitted.frmtmb_fit` text say the level is chosen "as brms
  chooses it".
- `predict(sample_new_levels = "old_levels")` has had the same
  per-block rule since 0.66.0: `predict_new_level_spec()` did it before
  the lane moved it into `old_level_pick_add()`. The lane makes
  `fitted()` inherit the rule and claims brms parity for it.
- Fix: key the choice by the grouping factor and the level (and the
  by-level for `gr(by = )`), not by the block. Add a test with `mu` and
  `sigma` on one factor, or `(1 + x || g)`.

B2. **The renamed-level fallback turns a named refusal into a silent
all-`NA` answer.** `ce_plan_part()` now takes the rename path whenever
no placeholder serves. It never checks that the row's values are
observed levels. Take `y ~ x + trt + (1 | trt:subj)` with nothing set.
The grid holds `trt = NA`, because `trt` is a grouping variable and
also a locked predictor. The rename label is then `NA`, and the fixed
part is `NA`.

`dev/ceplot-rev-misc.R` item 2 (data seed 41) and
`ceplot-rev-log/pf2/p1-attack-*.txt` (the postfit2 reviewer's P1):

| Band | Base (0.66.0) | Lane |
|---|---|---|
| boot | named refusal (below) | est NA NA NA, width NA, no message |
| draws | the same refusal | est NA NA NA, width NA, no message |
| Wald | est NA (pre-existing) | est NA |

The base refusal: "cannot draw a new level of the group-level term
(1 | trt:subj) at the grid rows with trt = NA, subj = NA ...".

The postfit2 review accepted that refusal as the correct behavior. The
fix is two lines. Refuse with `ce_plan_refuse()` in the rename branch
when any of the block's variables is `NA` in the row. Add the P1 shape
to `test-ce-levels.R` as the guard's positive case.

## Minor

### Blocker

P1-B1. **A grouping factor that two responses share still gets two seen
groups, in `fitted()` and in `predict()`.**

The model is `bf(y ~ x + (1 | g)) + bf(y2 ~ x + (1 | g))`, data seed 3,
calls at seeds 1 to 12.

- **`fitted(sample_new_levels = "old_levels")`:** frmtmb reads one seen
  group for both responses at 0 of 12 seeds. brms 2.23.0 does at 12 of
  12. frmtmb's `y` level equals brms's at all 12 seeds; its `y2` level
  is the next draw. Seed 1 reads 9/4, and brms reads 9/9
  (`oldlevels4.txt`).
- **`predict()`, in ONE call:** the choice recorded per response is
  9/4 at seed 1, and different for the two responses at every seed
  (`oldlevels5.txt`).
- **The cause:** `fitted_mv()` calls `fitted.frmtmb_fit()` once per
  response. Each call makes its own `fitted_old_levels()` draw, and
  `predict()` makes one `predict_new_level_spec()` draw per response.
- **The records:** NEWS and `?fitted.frmtmb_fit` say "every term of
  that factor reads the same seen level", with no exception. Only
  findings section 6 records the multivariate case, as a deliberate
  divergence. It is B1's defect on a different shape: the new group is
  one the data does not have. brms is clearly right here.
- **The fix:** draw the pick once per call over the linear predictors
  of every response, and pass it to each response. Add one test of a
  multivariate fit.

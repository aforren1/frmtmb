## Verdict

MERGEABLE AFTER FIXES.

The core claim holds. On every no-RE fit class I tried, the lane's
covariance is the inverse of a Hessian that agrees with a Richardson
Jacobian of the AD gradient to 1e-9 or better. 0.71.0 was up to 5
percent off on ordinary fits. With random effects, the step rule
removes the scale defect. The merge with illse2 is mechanical, and the
merged build passes the six SE test files of both lanes. One defect is new and must be
fixed: a family whose log density has a kink in a parameter
(asym_laplace) now gets standard errors of exactly 0 and confidence
intervals of zero width. The other findings are documentation and
small items.

## Findings

### 1. MEDIUM: exact-zero SEs and zero-width intervals on asym_laplace

`dev/adhess-rev-kink.R`, n = 400, x ~ N(0, 1), y = 1 + 0.5 x + N(0, 1),
seeds 1 to 5, `asym_laplace()` with `quantile` free and with
`quantile = 0.5` (`kink-lane.txt`, `kink-base.txt`):

    quantile free,  lane: SE 0/0/0/0 on 5 of 5 fits
    quantile fixed, lane: SE NaN/NaN/NaN on 5 of 5 fits
    base (0.71.0):        finite SEs on 10 of 10 (for example
                          0.01705/0.0164/0.05)

`dev/adhess-rev-kink2.R` (seed 2, `kink2-lane.txt`): `confint()` gives
`sigma_(Intercept)` as [-0.917, -0.917] around -0.917, and the same for
`quantile`, and `fixef()` gives NaN for the two coefficients. This is
the "interval whose lower, estimate and upper are
the same number" that the project treats as a wrong answer.

Cause. The asym_laplace log density is piecewise linear in mu, so the
AD Hessian has rows of exact 0 for the location coefficients (diag
`0/0/400/204`). The cross terms are not 0, so H = [[0, B], [B', C]].
solve() succeeds, and the block of the inverse for (sigma, quantile) is
exactly 0. Every one of these fits also has the "Large maximum absolute
gradient ... the curvature there is unusable" warning, so the fit is
"short" (`se_explained == "convergence"`), and
`sdr_rescue_hessian()` returns sdreport()'s inverse without repair. In
0.71.0 the same path returned the inverse of finite differences across
the kinks. That inverse was noise (0.71.0's SE(x) varied from 0.012 to
0.10 over the ten fits, where median regression on these data has an
asymptotic SE of about 0.063), but it was not 0.

Fix. Do not show a variance that is 0, negative or not finite. In
`sdr_rescue_hessian()`, before `if (short) return(sdr)` returns (and
on the `cov_usable(V0)` early return too), set the row and column of
each parameter with `!(is.finite(diag(V0)) & diag(V0) > 0)` to NaN in
`sdr$cov.fixed`. Add a test: an asym_laplace fit has no standard error
equal to 0, and no `confint()` row of zero width. File separately, as
pre-existing: frmtmb gives no valid Hessian SE for asym_laplace on
either arm, and its fits never pass the gradient check. It should
refuse by name, or use a sandwich estimate.

### 2. LOW to MEDIUM: quadrature and importance fits also changed, and the documentation says they did not

A `quadrature = TRUE` fit and an `importance = ` fit have an outer
objective with no TMB random effects (`length(obj$env$random) == 0`),
so they now take the AD Hessian. `dev/adhess-rev-quad.R`, poisson
`y ~ x + (1 | g)`, n = 300, 30 groups, seed 31 (`quad-*.txt`,
`quad1-*.txt`):

    x ~ N(500, 100)   vcov vs Richardson of obj$gr (SE)
                      base           lane
    quadrature        2.72e-03       1.72e-11
    importance        8.48e-01       7.85e-11

On the importance fit the base intercept SE was 0.498. The same model
with x / 100 (an exact reparameterization, `REV_XS=1`) gives 0.26969 on
both arms; the lane gives 0.26969 on the x ~ N(500, 100) design too.
So 0.71.0 was 85 percent off on importance fits with a covariate in
large units, and the lane fixes it. With x at unit scale the two arms
agree to 1.7e-5 (importance) and 4.7e-7 (quadrature).

This is the largest effect of the lane, and it is not claimed. The NEWS
bullet and the vignette say "with random effects it is central
differences", which is wrong for these fits. Fix: one NEWS sentence
("This includes `quadrature = TRUE` and `importance =` fits, whose
outer objective has no inner random effects."), the same in the
vignette paragraph, and a test that pins the importance fit against its
x / 100 reparameterization.

### 3. LOW: "no step moves a linear predictor by more than about 1e-3 per row" is not true

The RMS of a column bounds the average move, not the move of every row.
`dev/adhess-rev-step.R`, poisson GLMM `y ~ x + (1 | g)`, n = 400, 20
groups, seeds 1 to 3, against a Richardson Jacobian of the Laplace
gradient (two base steps agree to 5e-9 or better; `step-*.txt`,
`step-*-bern.txt`). Largest SE error:

    column                    base       lane       1e-3/max|x|
    x ~ N(0, 1e-2)            1.3e-07    1.3e-07    1.3e-07
    x ~ N(0, 1e-4) (units)    1.3e-07    1.3e-07    -
    1000 + N(0, 1)            NaN, lost  1.3e-07    1.3e-07
    5 + N(0, 0.1)             4.8e-07    1.3e-07    1.3e-07
    lognormal, RMS 0.9        5.5e-06    5.5e-06    2.5e-07
    lognormal, RMS 3          6.1e-05    6.8e-06    2.5e-07
    lognormal, RMS 50         1.6e-02    6.8e-06    2.5e-07
    N(500, 100)               2.2e-03    1.3e-07    1.3e-07
    N(500, 100) * dummy       1.7e-02    1.5e-07    1.4e-07
    dummy + N(0, 1)           1.3e-07    1.3e-07    1.3e-07
    bernoulli, N(500, 100)    2.8e-02    1.5e-06    1.5e-06

The base lost the group sd (with a warning) on all nine N(500, 100)
fits (poisson, interaction, bernoulli), and on the three 1000 + N(0, 1)
fits every SE was NaN with a warning. The lane lost none and warned on
none. RMS is
the right scale for a shifted column (RMS equals |mean| there, the
lane's rule handles it), for dummies and for interactions. On a
heavy-tailed column the largest row is 9 to 17 RMS units (max |x| 15.1
at RMS 0.9, 836 at RMS 50), the steps move those rows by up to 1.7e-2,
and the error stays at 7e-6. That is small, but it contradicts the
sentence in NEWS, in the vignette and in the roxygen of
`outer_fd_steps()`. Fix (minimal): say "by about 1e-3 in root mean
square over the rows". The alternative, `1e-3 / max(1, max|x|)`,
measured 30 times more accurate on heavy tails and equal elsewhere, but
it gives up the "bitwise 0.71.0 when every column is at unit scale or
below" property for standardized covariates (max |x| about 3). I do not
require it.

The vignette sentence is also wrong for nonlinear parameters, `mo()`
terms and variance parameters, which keep 1e-3 (NEWS says so, the
vignette does not), and for ill-conditioned designs, where the
covariance comes from the design's QR coordinates.

### 4. LOW: the coupling refusal is one-sided and is not marked BREAKING

`dev/adhess-rev-coh.R`, seed 301, 15 units, `cp_units(eta)` with 4 and
32 segments (`coh-*.txt`). The case where the refusal's condition is
absent behaves: intervals near 1 that stay inside (0, 1) come back, for
example eta = 6 (estimate 12.10 on the logit scale, SE 0.183, upper
1 - 3.9e-6) and eta = 9 with 4 segments (upper 1 - 9.65e-9). The
refusal fired once (eta = 16, 4 segments).

Two problems:

- The test is `plogis(lo) <= 0 | plogis(up) >= 1`. The upper end rounds
  to 1 above a logit of about 36.7. The lower end rounds to 0 only
  below about -745. So a coherence run off toward 0 with no curvature is
  never refused, and its mirror image near 1 is. The edge is also a
  hard threshold on a measured value: eta = 16 with 32 segments has SE
  7.96 and upper 1 - 4.4e-16, and it is returned, from a fit with code 1
  and two warnings.
- `frm_coherence()` now stops where it returned an interval, and the
  coupling NEWS bullet does not say BREAKING.

Fix: test both ends on the logit scale with one bound,
`abs(p$fit) + p$z * p$se.fit > stats::qlogis(1 - .Machine$double.eps)`
(about 36.04), or document the asymmetry; and mark the bullet BREAKING.

### Which covariance wins

Without random effects, se_fit_analysis0() runs cov_from_hessian() on
the AD Hessian at fit time in 0.71.0, in illse2 and in adhess alike.
Where the design is ill-conditioned (unit-diagonal condition at or
above `se_reparam_kappa` = 1e8), se_reparam_cov() returns tier 4 (the
factor), that analysis is stored in `fit$cache$se_analysis`, and
sdr_rescue_hessian() puts it in the sdreport whatever Hessian
sdreport() was handed. So illse2's QR route wins, and adhess's AD
inverse never reaches the covariance there.

Measured, `dev/adhess-rev-acc.R` (a copy of illse2's
`dev/illse2-acc.R`), raw polynomials on [1, 2], n = 300, degrees 4 to
8, seeds 1 to 3, against the 256-bit covariance (`acc-*.txt`,
`accre-*.txt`):

- gaussian, poisson and bernoulli without RE: merged identical to
  illse2 on every row; lane identical to base on every row; `root
  TRUE` on all (the factor).
- gaussian with `(1 | g)`, degrees 5 to 8: merged identical to illse2
  on all 12 fits (correlation error 6.7e-10 to 1.2e-5); lane identical
  to base on degrees 4 to 8 (15 fits; degree 4 does not take the route
  on either). adhess's smaller steps for the wide `x^k` columns change
  the natural difference Hessian that se_reparam_cov() gates on, and no
  gate decision moved.

Is the plain AD inverse accurate there? No.
`dev/adhess-rev-adinv.R` (`adinv-*.txt`), `solve()` of the symmetrized
AD Hessian against the 256-bit covariance:

    unit-diagonal kappa   SE error of solve(AD H)
    4e8 to 6e8 (deg 4)    2e-08 to 8e-08
    1.3e13 to 1.7e13      1.9e-04 to 1.1e-03
    3e15 and above        solve() fails

So above about 1e8 the covariance needs the factor, and the route
supplies it; below 1e8 the AD inverse loses at most about kappa times
epsilon (1e-8). Where the route refuses (more than `se_reparam_np`
outer parameters, a weak direction outside the designs, a fit that
stopped short), the lane's AD inverse is no worse than 0.71.0's
difference inverse.

## Findings

### F1 (FIX, record). The mix40 move is C0, not the swap, and the "higher maxima" are degenerate

`dev/round-20261116.md` ("Semantic conflicts", "Key tables" drawdir and
"What is not done") and `dev/round-handoff.md` ("What to know next
time") say ordstarts' swapped start reaches higher maxima, all code 0,
where predict() cannot recover the covariance. The run says otherwise
(`rel084-review-mix40.R`, logs `mix40-r23.txt`, `mix40-r22.txt`, the
drawdir case builder without its `suppressWarnings()`):

| seed | rellib-r22 | rellib-r23 |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | -378.694443, `frmtmb_mixture_step_start`: "8 of 10 jittered starts reached ... -375.9404 ... component 2 is a step function"; vcov 8 of 8 finite | -375.940361 code 0, degenerate warning "component 2 (a step function ... closest latent distance is 239)", se_lost mu2_x, tau_raw2_1, tau_raw2_2 flat; Hessian eigen 8.7e-11; slope 1070, threshold -3465 |
| 10 | -399.825912, same message (-396.4590) | -396.461499 code 0, `frmtmb_conv_gradient` and the degenerate warning (component 1 a step function); Hessian eigen 3.1e-15; slope 10759 |
| 27 | -378.658143, same message (-375.4300) | -375.429977, `frmtmb_ord_unplaced` (component 1 a step function) |

No r23 fit says `frmtmb_mixture_swap`. The r23 default reaches, without
jittered starts, the same step-function suprema that 0.83.0's jittered
starts found, refused and named. So the move is the cost of C0 (no
default jittered starts) that NEWS already states ("its degenerate
warning rose from 14 to 20 of 40 fits with 300 rows"), at a degenerate
boundary that the fit warns about, with a singular Hessian there.
predict()'s "the covariance of the estimates could not be recovered from
the Hessian, so the draws are taken at the estimates alone" is accurate.
Not a predict() defect and not a regression to file. Fix: correct the
attribution in the three places (C0, degenerate step-function boundaries
with fit-time warnings, seed 10 also a gradient warning; not the swap).

### F4 (FIX, record and backlog). Two measured items the record calls unmeasured or does not have

(a) The separation early return (`check_convergence()`, `R/fit.R:6835`)
leaves `conv_classes` unset, and gpselimit's `gp_basis_message()`
(`R/gp-start.R:1910`) then treats the stop as undoubted. Constructed
(`rel084-review-sepgp2.R`, `sepgp2-r23.txt`): bernoulli, `y ~ g + gp(x,
k = 5)`, a field drawn with length scale 0.7 on [0, 10], n 200, level
"c" of `g` all 1 (quasi-separation). On seeds 2 and 4 of 8 the fit ends
at code 1 with `frmtmb_separation` AND the message `frmtmb_gp_basis` /
`frmtmb_boundary_fit` ("Boundary fit ... The fitted field and its
predictions hold"); with `conv_classes` set to a convergence class the
same call gives the warning instead. On seeds 1, 5, 6, 7 the pair comes
at code 0, which is consistent. Through `ald_cens_sep()` (asym_laplace,
every row `cens(right)`, `y ~ gp(x, k = 5)`, `rel084-review-sepgp.R`,
seeds 1 to 6): separation warned on 6 of 6, no gp message, because the
all-censored fit does not reach the white limit (white FALSE, sdgp 0.33
to 0.69 on seeds 1 to 3); I did not find a case on that route. The
`frmtmb_ald_local` warning cannot meet a gp message: alapse's proof runs
only without random effects. Severity: LOW stays right (a boundary
message beside a separation warning that already says the estimates do
not exist), but record the case in the backlog item's place (a new LOW
line in "Filed at the 0.84.0 release", since item text is not edited)
and in the record's step 4, replacing "not measured".

(b) asym_laplace with a binding bound (`rel084-review-alapse.R`,
`-alapse2.R`, new data seed 8411, skewed errors): the fit is inside its
box and at the constrained maximum (a quantile bound at p 0.086606 gives
logLik -126.55465857, equal to the fixed-quantile fit at that p, which
is proved global), but it warns `frmtmb_conv_gradient` "Large maximum
absolute gradient at the optimum (7.48 at x) ... may not have
converged", and with a binding sigma box code 1 with `frmtmb_conv_code`
too; not `frmtmb_ald_local`, and not its bound advice. The 7.48 is the
kink's one-sided gradient in the location, which the exact finish
otherwise explains. rellib-r22 gives the same classes, so this is not a
regression, and NEWS's "a bound on either ... keeps an estimated
quantile's fit local" is true only for an interior (non-binding)
maximum. A false "may not have converged" on a correct fit: file it
MEDIUM (owner `R/ald-fixed.R`; candidate: on a binding bound, take the
exact LP finish at the bound value and say `frmtmb_ald_local` with the
"bound" advice).

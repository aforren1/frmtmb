# Review of lane squant (round of 2026-11-16): quantile_elf()

Reviewer: adversarial review, 2026-11-17. Worktree
`frmtmb-wt-squant`, lane library `wt-squant-lib` (core 0.83.0 plus the
lane), extensions from rellib-r22. Every number below is from a log in
`dev/reviews/` written by a script of the same stem
(`squant-review-<name>.R` writes `squant-review-<name>.txt`). No package
code was edited, nothing was committed, at most two R processes ran at
once.

## Verdict: FIX

The density, its normalization, the asym_laplace limit, the mean and
variance, the values of the log CDF pair, the draws, the qgam and rq
comparisons, the bias statement, the SE and bootstrap measurements and
the (1 | g) MEDIUM all reproduce. One numerical defect in new code
(item 1) and two documentation gaps that leave the default output
misleading (items 2 and 3) must be fixed before the lane ships.

1. **(MEDIUM, must fix) The tape's derivative of `lF` is wrong where
   `F` is small.** `elf_cdf_log()` returns the right VALUE, but its
   derivative in `mu` and `sigma` has a relative error that grows like
   `eps / F` for `-690 < t < 0`, and is 100 % wrong (exactly 0, or
   doubled) once `F < ~1e-16`. `squant-review-tape5.txt`, `d lF / d mu`
   against the analytic `-f / F`, relative error:

   | p | smoothing | t = -40 | t = -100 | t = -125 | t = -150 | t = -200 | t = -689 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 0.1 | 0.25 | 4e-13 | 4.2e-07 | 0.00011 | 0.028 | 1 | 1 |
   | 0.1 | 1 | 0.11 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
   | 0.5 | 0.25 | 8.9e-16 | 2.2e-16 | 1.8e-15 | 1.8e-15 | 1.8e-15 | 1 (from t = -300) |
   | 0.9 | 1 | 1.5e-14 | 1.5e-11 | 5.8e-11 | 9.3e-10 | 2.4e-07 | 1 (from t = -500) |

   The `lS` side for `t > 0` is right (`squant-review-tape.txt` (i):
   tape 0.71428571 = analytic at t = 355, 400, 689). Located in
   `squant-review-tape3.txt`: `lf0` and `lf_low` have the right
   derivative (0.125 per unit t); the complement correction
   `log1m_exp_ad(ad_min(ls_high, cap)) - log1m_exp_ad(ad_min(ls0, cap))`
   is 0 in value but not in derivative: the first term alone has
   derivative 0.25 at t = -290 and 0 at t = -300, the second 0.125.
   The two terms differ only by `- b * ad_pos(dl)`; `ad_pos()` passes
   the ulp-size derivative of `dl = t - tc` through at `dl = 0`
   (abs'(0) = 1), and `log1m_exp_ad()` near 0 multiplies it by `1 / F`.
   The `lS` side uses `a * ad_min(dl, 0)`, whose derivative cancels
   exactly. At exactly `t = -690` the gradient is garbage
   (`squant-review-density.txt` 2d: -2.09e+74 in mu at p 0.01,
   smoothing 0.25). It reaches a fit through left-censored rows far
   below `mu`: `squant-review-tape4.txt`, rows censored at 60 in data
   near 100, p 0.1: the fit stopped at code 0, no warning, max |tape
   gradient| 1.39e-05 against a finite-difference gradient of 0.0185
   (smoothing 0.25) and 0.0186 (smoothing 1). There the harm was small
   (BFGS on `fn` moved the estimates by 1e-4), because `sigma` grows to
   keep such rows out of the zero band; the Hessian of those rows is
   wrong too. The lane's check was one point ("its gradient the finite
   difference to 10 digits"). Fix: gate the correction with an exact
   switch, or build `ls_high` with the same `ad_min()` form as
   `lf_low`, so that inside `|t| < 690` the correction contributes an
   exact zero to the derivative; add a test of the tape's `d lF / d mu`
   and `d lS / d mu` against `-f / F` and `f / S` over `t` in
   `[-689, 689]` and at `t = +-690`, p 0.1, 0.5, 0.9, smoothing 0.25
   and 1; run it under OpenBLAS.

2. **(must fix, docs) The default standard errors are the misleading
   ones, and the page does not show how to report the right ones.**
   `summary()`, `confint()`, `hypothesis()` and `fixef()`'s Est.Error
   report the Hessian SE with z values and p values and no note
   (`squant-review-integ.txt`: slope Est.Error 0.19, z 15.94;
   `summary(fit, vcov = vcov_cluster(fit, ~ row, "CR0", full = TRUE))`
   gives 0.36, t 8.67, on 300 rows, quantile 0.9, sd 0.5 + x). The
   lane's own table has Hessian/MC sd 0.505 to 0.592 and coverage 0.700
   to 0.777 at quantile 0.9 on every design, 0.760 at n = 2000; on 50
   new data sets the Hessian was 0.526 of the MC sd with coverage 0.68
   (`squant-review-boot.txt`). The memory rule "no special-case
   defaults" rules out switching the default for this family, so the
   documentation must carry it: in "Standard errors" and in the
   example, show `summary(fit, vcov = vcov_cluster(fit, ~ row, "CR0",
   full = TRUE))` and `confint(fit, vcov = ...)` (both work, see the
   log), and `vcov(fit, cluster = ~ row)`; say in one sentence that the
   default summary is the ELF model's and covers about 0.7 to 0.8 away
   from the median. "How to choose" step 2 says to use the sandwich
   for `confint()` but does not say how.

3. **(must fix, docs) With a group-level term the recommended route
   has no calibrated standard error, and the page does not say so.**
   "Group-level terms" recommends `quadrature = TRUE`, and
   `vcov_cluster()` refuses every quadrature fit ("vcov_cluster()
   cannot use a fit made with quadrature = TRUE",
   `squant-review-re.txt`). `vcov_cluster(fit, ~ g)` works on the
   Laplace fit. Say which SE applies to each route, or that none is
   calibrated after quadrature.

4. **(should fix, backlog text) The filed (1 | g) MEDIUM understates
   the miss.** Reproduced: g4, quantile 0.9, seed 10, smoothing 0.25,
   code 0, no condition, logLik -143.8349; the profile in log sd is
   +1.764 at offset -0.5, and `nlminb` from that point reaches
   -142.0713 (`squant-review-re.txt`). At smoothing 0.1 on the same
   data the profile is +3.420 at offset -0.25 and `nlminb` reaches
   -142.8796 against the fit's -146.2993; the lane's coarser grid
   (`re-g4-0.9.txt`) did not see the 3.42. The profile is a sawtooth at
   small smoothing (-2.165, +0.136, +3.420, 0, -3.801 at offsets -0.75
   to +0.25): local maxima, not a single missed one. Reason: the ELF is
   log-concave in `mu`, so each inner mode is unique, but the log
   determinant jumps by up to `1 / (4 lambda sigma^2)` per row as an
   inner mode crosses a data point. The counts 7, 2, 1, 0 of 50 are
   lower bounds from a 10-point grid. Update the item and the
   "Group-level terms" sentence ("by up to 1.76") with this.

5. **(LOW) `elf_quantile()` is wrong at small shapes.** It trusts
   `qbeta()`, which is inaccurate there and warns
   (`squant-review-tape.txt` (ii), against `uniroot()` on the log CDF,
   mu 0, sigma 1): p 0.01, smoothing 0.25, pr 0.9: 177.44568 against
   229.34003; smoothing 0.05, pr 0.5: 24.556743 against 68.313613;
   smoothing 0.001, pr 0.5: 0.70978271 against 68.309686 (and p 0.99
   mirrored). No package code calls it (only the test, whose grid,
   smoothing 0.05 to 2 and p 0.1 to 0.9, misses this), but its comment
   offers it "for a reader that needs it". Polish with Newton steps on
   `cdf_log`, or narrow the comment and the test's claim.

6. **(LOW, docs) The bias formula is a small-width approximation.**
   The exact statement is right: `mu*` equals the smoothed quantile to
   5 digits in all 18 population cases (`squant-review-fits.txt` 3d).
   The formula `-(pi^2/6) (lambda sigma)^2 f'(q) / f(q)` is within
   about 30 % at smoothing 0.25 (exponential p 0.1: +0.00062 against
   +0.00091) but at smoothing 1 it is off by a factor 2 (lognormal
   median: +0.17876 against +0.37569) and has the wrong sign near the
   edge of the support (exponential p 0.1: -0.02268 against +0.01301).
   Say "for a kernel width small against the scale on which `f`
   changes"; the table, from integration, is right.

7. **(LOW, optional) `frm_compat("quantile_elf")` stops with "Unknown
   feature".** Filed by the lane as LOW, as for huber. Measured here:
   `trunc()`, interval and right `cens()`, `mi()` on the response and
   on a predictor whose model is `quantile_elf()`, `mixture()` of two
   ELFs, and `frm_sample()` all fit at code 0
   (`squant-review-integ.txt`). Adding the feature with these rows is
   in the lane's remit; keeping the LOW item is acceptable.

8. **(LOW, findings text) The core argument leans on question 2.**
   "Would most users meet it" is a stretch for quantile regression. The
   decisive reasons are elsewhere in `dev/core-boundary.md`: "What core
   holds" lists the distributional families; guardrail 2, because the
   family reads core internals that no seam exports (`log_ibeta_two()`,
   `lbeta_ad()`, `log1m_exp_ad()`, `dpar_complement()`, `ad_min()`),
   so an extension would first need new exports; and guardrail 3 (small,
   stable, no dependency). Core is right; add these.

## What was checked and holds

### 1. The density

- qgam 2.0.0's `elf()` read in this session: `aic()` is `2 sum(term)`
  with `term = -(1 - tau) r / sig + lam log1pexp(r / lam) / sig +
  log(lam beta(lam (1 - tau) / sig, tau lam / sig))`, `lam = co`, `sig =
  exp(theta) lam / mean(lam)`. With `smoothing = co / exp(theta)` this
  is the paper's `exp((1 - tau) r / sigma) (1 + exp(r / (lambda
  sigma)))^-lambda / (lambda sigma B(lambda (1 - tau), lambda tau))`.
  Lane `lpdf` against `-aic / 2` per row: worst relative difference
  1.89e-15 over 45 settings (tau 0.01 to 0.99, co 0.01 to 2, theta -1.5
  to 1) and 8 responses each (`squant-review-density.txt` 1a).
- Integral of the density: max |integral - 1| 7.55e-15 over 90
  settings (tau 0.01 to 0.99, smoothing 0.005 to 20, sigma 0.05 to 20)
  (1b).
- Limit to `asym_laplace()$lpdf`: max gap 0.0631, 0.00252, 1.63e-08,
  1.63e-10, 1.63e-12 at smoothing 1e-1 to 1e-5 (p 0.01 and 0.99), and
  0.06 to 3.45e-11 at p 0.3 (1c).
- Mean and variance against integration: max |mean - integral| / sd
  2.23e-15, max |var / integral - 1| 2e-15 over 20 settings, p 0.01 to
  0.99, smoothing 0.02 to 5 (1d).

### 2. The log CDF pair, tails, quantile, draws

- Values against integration of the density in log space (the small
  tail, and the large tail through `expm1`), 180 points, p 0.01, 0.5,
  0.99, smoothing 0.001 to 5, `t` from -5000 to 5000 including +-689.9,
  +-690, +-800: max relative error 1.13e-12 (2a). The continuation past
  690 is right in value and slope.
- Draws: KS p-values 0.078 to 0.881 on 1e5 draws at 12 settings down to
  smoothing 0.001 with p 0.01 and 0.99; all draws finite (2c).
- Derivative: item 1. Quantile function: item 5.

### 3. Fits

- qgam, two parametric cases not the lane's (gamma errors, a factor,
  `y ~ x1 + x2 + f`, n 500, seed 301; `squant-review-fits.txt` 3a):
  qu 0.25, lsig -1: logLik difference 1.27e-09, max relative coef
  difference 1.73e-06; qu 0.95, lsig 0.5: 5.23e-12 and 6.04e-07. Both
  code 0, no warning.
- qgam, smooth (`gamSim(2)`, `s(x, k = 15)`, qu 0.3, lsig -1, seed
  302; 3b): max |mu difference| / range of mu 1.46e-07, code 0. (The
  two logLik values printed there are not comparable: frm's is the
  REML marginal.)
- rq(), lognormal heteroscedastic errors, n 500, seeds 311 to 313
  (3c): max |coef - rq| in rq nid SEs at smoothing 0.002 is 0.004 to
  0.074; at 0.25 it is 0.136 to 0.467. All code 0.
- Large sample (n 40000, lognormal errors, seed 320; 3e): intercept
  minus truth +0.0288 (median, smoothing 0.25) against the population
  +0.0297, +0.181 against +0.179 (smoothing 1), +0.0144 against +0.0148
  and +0.250 against +0.235 at quantile 0.9, all within one SE (0.012 to
  0.032).

### 4. Standard errors and the bootstrap

- The SE table in `?quantile_elf` and the findings matches
  `dev/squant-out/se-summary.txt` row for row.
- Bootstrap MEDIUM confirmed on 50 new data sets (homo, n 400,
  quantile 0.9, smoothing 0.25, 100 draws each, seeds 20261200 + i,
  `squant-review-boot.txt`): bootstrap SE 0.400 of the lane's MC sd
  (0.2871, 300 sets) and 0.368 of the MC sd of these 50 slopes
  (0.3118); coverage 0.48; median bootstrap / Hessian 0.726; 5000 of
  5000 refits converged. Sandwich 0.844 of the 50-set MC sd, coverage
  0.86; the lane's 300 sets give 1.002 and 0.913, and the per-set
  sandwich SE has sd 0.097 around 0.288, so the two agree within the
  50-set noise. The sandwich is unbiased but noisy (CV about 1/3),
  which is why it covers 0.91 rather than 0.95.
- Lane alapse lists `frm_bootstrap()` first for asym_laplace; the
  consolidation must resolve that against this MEDIUM.

### 5. (1 | g)

- A maximum exists: the ELF is log-concave in `mu`, so each inner
  problem has one mode; the Laplace objective tends to the
  fixed-effects value as log sd goes to minus infinity and falls as it
  grows. Profiles on g4, quantile 0.9, seed 10 (`squant-review-re.txt`):
  at offset -6 they are 4.47, 6.88, 13.82 below the fit (smoothing
  0.1, 0.25, 1), at +12 they are 225.4 to 228.4 below. The supremum can
  be the boundary sd = 0; no proof of an interior maximum.
- The filed MEDIUM reproduces, larger than filed: item 4.
- `(1 | obs)` with unit weights (3 seeds, smoothing 0.25 and 1): code
  0, the profile in log sigma falls and levels off (seed 1, smoothing 1:
  -92.524, -92.744, -92.744 at -2, -5, -10 against -80.314 at the fit),
  as the bounded exact marginal predicts.

- `trunc(lb = 0)`, interval `cens()`, `mi()` on the response and on a

- Weights check, condition absent: weights 2 with no group term, and

# Review of lane predraw2 (round of 2026-11-13)

Adversarial review of branch wt-r83-predraw2 (base ec8178d3 = 0.82.0),
library `wt-predraw2-lib`, against rellib-r21. Scripts
`dev/reviews/predraw2-review-*`; every number below is copied from their
logs (same names, `.txt`). No lane file was edited; no git writes.

## Verdict: FIX

One HIGH defect (the check fires on ordinary, well-identified GLMs and
the reweighting then makes coverage worse), one MEDIUM (the importance
ratio is not the one the docs and NEWS state on a fit with a narrowed
side). Everything else the brief asked to attack holds: no test sits
near a threshold on any BLAS or rounding, the RNG and cache behave, the
non-fired draws are bit for bit 0.82.0's, the coverage table reproduces
exactly, the merges are clean.

## FIX 1 (HIGH): the check fires on poisson GLMs with many sparse levels, and coverage gets worse

`R/predict-brms.R:2137` (`draw_check_tol <- 1`) with the median excess of
`draw_check()` (`:1825`, `:1849`). The paired excess sums every
parameter's small departure from the quadratic. Each level of a factor
with a few counts has a mildly skewed log-likelihood (no side steep,
none flat, so the side law is not built); over 100 to 200 coefficients
the fourth-order terms add up to a median excess of about
`p * 3 / (24 S)` (S = counts per level), which passes 1. The fits are
interior and well identified: this is not the weak identification the
check is for. Importance sampling in 100 to 200 dimensions then
degenerates (k > 0.7), the draws collapse to 150 to 260 distinct
parameter vectors out of 1000, and the parameter uncertainty shrinks.

Reproducer `dev/reviews/predraw2-review-d.R` (truth known: `y ~ 0 + f`,
poisson, level log means `log(mu) + N(0, 0.3)`, m rows per level;
"base" = the same call with `draw_reweight()` mocked to return its
input, which is 0.82.0's draw since no side law is built; coefficient
coverage = share of levels whose 90% draw interval holds the true log
mean; predictive coverage exact against the true poisson law). Summary
`dev/reviews/predraw2-review-d-sum.txt`:

    pois_K200_m5_mu2     fits  8 fired  8 (excess 1.483-2.27) warn  8 | fired: unique  155 coef cov90 lane 0.829 base 0.913 pred cov90 lane 0.9498 base 0.9602
    pois_K100_m5_mu1.5   fits  7 fired  4 (excess 2.08-2.383) warn  3 | fired: unique  235 coef cov90 lane 0.840 base 0.910 pred cov90 lane 0.9590 base 0.9666 | max unfired excess 1.000
    pois_K100_m5_mu1     fits  1 fired  1 (excess 3.161-3.161) warn  1 | fired: unique  184 coef cov90 lane 0.820 base 0.900 pred cov90 lane 0.9650 base 0.9774
    pois_K50_m5_mu2      (10 seeds) fired 0, max unfired excess 0.676 (n 40)
    pois_K100_m10_mu1.5  (10 seeds) fired 0, max unfired excess 0.334
    pois_K30_m10_mu3     (3 seeds)  fired 0, max 0.273
    bern_K60_m20_p03     (3 seeds)  fired 0, max -0.174

(Seeds whose data have a level with no counts fall back to the plug-in
on both builds and are skipped: a 0.82.0 behavior, not this lane's.)
Expected: no fire on a correct, interior GLM, or at least coverage not
worse. Actual: 13 of 13 fired fits with K >= 100 and 5 counts per level
lose 0.06 to 0.11 of coefficient coverage at 90% (on every single
fired fit, lane < base), predictive coverage falls about 0.01, and 12
of 13 print `frmtmb_draw_pareto` on every predict() call naming
`frm_sample()` for a model that needs no sampler. One fit
(`pois_K100_m5_mu1.5` seed 10) reads excess 1.000 over 40 draws: in
real use the verdict sits on a rounding branch there.

This breaks two acceptance lines of the brief: "keep [the false-alarm
rate] near zero" and "coverage must not get worse anywhere". The lane's
corpus missed it because its many-parameter fits (F1 gaussian f(200),
F2 poisson f(60), F3 categorical 49) have large counts per level.

Needs a user decision on the route; options, each to be measured on
this design and the 4882 mixtures:
- scale the threshold with the parameter count (the quartic sum grows
  like p; the mixtures that fire have p 6 to 9 and excess 1.09 to 940);
- return the raw draws (with the warning) when k > 0.7, which on the
  lane's own corpus would undo 40 of 44 fired mixture fits;
- read the excess per direction (e.g. along the leading eigenvectors)
  rather than summed over all of them.
Reviewer's lean: the first, with the threshold set so that the
`pois_K200_m5_mu2` design does not fire, then rerun the lane's
mixture coverage table.

## FIX 2 (MEDIUM): the importance ratio carries a per-side factor the docs do not state

`R/predict-brms.R:1937`-`:1944` (`draw_logratio()`): the log Jacobian is
`log g'(x) - log g'_side(0)`, anchored separately on each side of a
narrowed coordinate. The proposal density is `phi_V(x) / g'(x)`, so the
log weight of the target `exp(-objective)` needs `log g'(x)` itself;
the anchor multiplies every draw on a narrowed side by
`1 / g'_side(0)` (3.3 times at r = 0.3, 10 times at r = 0.1). The
anchoring keeps the check at 0 on side-law fits, which is right for the
DETECTOR; the WEIGHTS (`r$lw`, used at `:1812`) should not carry it.

Reproducer `dev/reviews/predraw2-review-a.R jac` (log
`predraw2-review-a1.txt`): 1-D target `t^2/2` above 0, `(t/a)^2/2`
below, side law knots `a * (1:4)` (exactly the target's shape on each
side), 20000 draws through `draw_logratio()` and `draw_resample()`:

    a 0.30 exact P(t<0) 0.2308 | proposal 0.5065 | lane reweighted 0.5065 | absolute Jacobian 0.2354
    a 0.10 exact P(t<0) 0.0909 | proposal 0.5065 | lane reweighted 0.5065 | absolute Jacobian 0.0975

Expected 0.231 and 0.091; the lane keeps the side law's 50/50 split.
On real fired fits it reaches 7 of the 19 fired ordinal-mixture fits
with a side law (`predraw2-review-e.txt`: narrowed sides with slope at
0 of 0.08 to 0.92). There the corrected ratio moved the share error
from 0.0373, 0.0405, 0.0435, 0.0202, 0.0345, 0.0374, 0.0295 to 0.0370,
0.0418, 0.0435, 0.0248, 0.0375, 0.0385, 0.0295: no better against the
simulated truth (the flat-prior posterior is not the truth, and k > 0.7
there). So either use the absolute Jacobian in `lw` (keep the anchor in
`e`), or keep the 50/50 split on purpose and say so: `?predict.frmtmb_fit`
(`R/predict-brms.R:149`) and NEWS.md line 9 state "each draw is weighted
by the likelihood over the density it was drawn from", which is false
on such a fit. User decision; reviewer's lean: correct ratio in `lw`,
since the docs promise it and the evidence for the split is noise.

## Item by item

1. Threshold and rounding: clean. `predraw2-review-b.R` (every fit of
   `test-predict-draw-check.R` and `test-predict-draw-edge.R` that
   reaches the check, x times 1 + 2e-15 k, k 0..20) on reference BLAS
   and on OpenBLAS 0.3.26 and 0.3.32 with LAPACK, cores Haswell and
   Sandybridge (`predraw2-review-ob-out/b-*.txt`): the fired fits read
   8.9851 and 52.29 to 52.32 everywhere; every other fit -0.41 to 0.147;
   least distance to any of 0.5, 1, 2 is 0.353 (`E10_lam1`). Both test
   files pass under all four OpenBLAS settings (19 and 45 pass, 0 warn).
   Over the 138 test files (core and extensions) that call predict():
   `predraw2-review-sweep.log`, 138 of 138, fail 0, err 0, warn 0; 182
   check verdicts traced, fired only in the lane's two files; the
   largest unfired excess is 0.323 (`test-gp-start.R`).
2. RNG: clean. `predraw2-review-a.R rng` (`-a3.txt`): under default,
   L'Ecuyer-CMRG/Box-Muller/Rounding and MT/Ahrens-Dieter, a fired fit's
   first call (check computed) and second (cached) give identical
   draws, identical `.Random.seed`, the caller's kinds, and the same
   next `rnorm()`. With no `.Random.seed` before the call, the call
   creates it under the default kinds. The resampling takes one
   `runif()` per stratum from the caller's stream, as documented. Not
   run on R-devel (no `binom.kind` here); the private stream uses only
   `rnorm()` and passes three kinds to `set.seed()`, which leaves a
   fourth kind as it was.
3. Cache: in `fit$cache` (environment). Survives saveRDS/readRDS and
   gives the same draws (`-a1.txt`: "draws identical TRUE", the read fit
   says `frmtmb_draw_pareto` again, as every call does); a fit read back
   before any predict() computes the same verdict (8.985055). The
   verdict does not depend on newdata, re_formula (`re_view()` keeps
   obj, opt and cache) or ndraws; update() and refit() build a new
   cache. Two identical calls differ only in the once-per-fit message,
   the same convention as `frmtmb_draw_held`.
4. Reweighting correctness: right on the normal-law path. Poisson
   intercept-only, flat prior on the log rate, exact Gamma(3, 50)
   posterior, check forced on (`-a1.txt`): quantiles .025/.1/.5/.9/.975
   0.0110 0.0200 0.0524 0.1074 0.1499 against exact 0.0124 0.0220
   0.0535 0.1065 0.1445 (normal law 0.0180 0.0281 0.0594 0.1299
   0.1928). Wrong on the narrowed path: FIX 2. On Laplace fits
   exp(-objective) is the Laplace marginal over the random effects,
   the right target for the outer parameters. `loo::psis()` takes -Inf
   ratios (a draw whose objective is not finite) without error and
   never picks that draw (`predraw2-review-e.txt`).
5. Coverage: the gaussian design rerun, both arms
   (`predraw2-review-mc-sum.txt`), reproduces the record exactly (both
   tsv files equal the lane's in every column compared): fired n 6,
   cov90 lane 0.906 base 0.950, cov98 0.980 against 0.994. Per fit
   (record's tsv): seed 40 goes from 0.917 to 0.884 at 90% (0.987 to
   0.974 at 98%), as far below nominal as base was above; the plug-in
   there is 0.876. Within Monte Carlo error of the intervals, not a
   finding. The lane's claim does not hold outside its corpus: FIX 1.
6. Cost: the check is paid once per fit; the reweighting on every call
   of a fired fit (filed LOW by the lane). Large GLMM (poisson, 10000
   rows, `(1 | g)` 2000 levels, `-a2.txt`): `draw_check()` alone 0.48 s;
   first predict() (200 draws) 5.80 s, second 3.81 s, check recomputed

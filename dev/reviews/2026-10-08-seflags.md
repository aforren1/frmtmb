## Blocking

### B1. Non-estimable hypotheses get finite SEs with no warning (claim 1)

`se_tier3()` sets `flat_tol = min(tau, se_dominant * max(proj))`, and
`jc_nonest()` uses `flat_tol` as the prediction threshold. tau bounds
the subspace error only when it is small. When the cap decides
(tau > half the largest projection), the threshold is a dominance rule
for naming parameters, not an error bound, and it is 30 times looser
than the measured accuracy of the direction.

`dev/seflags-rev-pred3.R` on ported fixture 1, OpenBLAS 0.3.26 BLAS
and LAPACK (`pred3-ob-{lane,base}.txt`). The exact design alias is
`s(Age).fx1 = -0.0464 + 0.190041 Age`, so `b_Age + t * bs_sAge_1` is
estimable only at t = 0.190041. flat_tol 0.359 (the cap), 2 flat
columns, projection of the exact alias onto the stored subspace
0.99993. Cosines are in the unit-diagonal metric `jc_nonest()` uses:

    t         cos(exact alias)  lane Est.Error  warnings | base
    0.190041  0.0000            0.6276          0        | NaN, 1 warning
    0.15      0.1164            0.5603          0        | NaN, 1 warning
    0.10      0.2958            0.4777          0        | NaN, 1 warning
    0.05      0.5040            NaN             1        | NaN, 1 warning

The t = 0.15 and 0.10 rows are not estimable (true variance infinite):
the lane reports finite SEs with no warning. 0.69.0 warned on every
row, the estimable one included (the B1 false alarm the lane fixed).
The lane trades the false alarm for a silent false "estimable" on the
CI runners' configuration, which the project's standard ranks worse.
The same cap leaves `(Intercept)` with a finite SE on fixture 1 under
OpenBLAS, though its unit-diagonal loading on the exact alias is 0.11
(the lane's own figure); that part is 0.69.0's too.

Where tau is small the rule is exact. `dev/seflags-rev-pred.R` and
`-pred2.R` (both BLAS builds) sweep the cosine with the stored
direction from 0 to 0.8 on spread k = 10 (exact Hessian, and with
`b ~ 1 + (1 | h)`), on `y ~ x + s(x) + (1 | g)` at means 1e-5, 1e-3,
0.1, and on that construction plus a collinear pair: flat_tol 3.2e-5
to 0.019, every cosine of 0.05 and above NaN with a warning, cosine 0
finite. Lane nanse's B2 constructions hold: `fitted(dpar = "a")` and
`"b"` NaN with 1 warning each, `b_a_f1` NaN, `b_a_f1 + b_b_Intercept`
0.189063 (= sigma_ML / sqrt(5)), `b_a_f1 - b_a_f2` 0.267376.

Required: the prediction test must not use the parameter cap. Options,
in order of preference: (a) when a flat direction lies in the fixed
coefficients of one linear predictor, test predictions against the
exact null space of that predictor's design (fixture 1's alias is a
design alias, exact to 1.3e-16), at `se_pred_tol`; (b) otherwise, in
the cap regime, keep 0.69.0's conservative answer (NaN) for any
prediction whose projection is above the direction's measured
accuracy, not above 0.359. A test: fixture 1's `b_Age + 0.15 *
bs_sAge_1` must be NaN with a warning under OpenBLAS LAPACK, and
`fitted(newdata)` silent. A reference-BLAS construction with a large
tau is needed for the suite (I did not find one; fixture 1 stops at
code 1 there).

loop removes it.

  settled (the outer Hessian includes the theta and sigma coupling

NaN with a reason that says the Hessian cannot resolve the direction

### B4 (new, blocking). "illcond" predictions get silent, narrow SEs

`se_tier3()` leaves an "illcond" direction out of the covariance. The
prediction test then reads it as a downward direction, by cosine
against `se_pred_tol` = 1e-6. A curvature below machine epsilon of the
largest makes a cosine far below 1e-6 carry most of a prediction's
variance, so the test lets those predictions through with the
direction's variance missing. The parameter rule has a variance share
(`se_ill_share`); the prediction test has none.

raw polynomials on [1, 2], `fitted(newdata)` at x = 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5.
The reference is the exact prediction SE at the fit's own sigma and
sd, `x0' (X' V^-1 X)^-1 x0`, from a QR of the whitened design. It is
accurate to the square root of the condition number; the same
computation reproduces lm()'s SEs exactly (`A/deg7exact-ref.txt`).

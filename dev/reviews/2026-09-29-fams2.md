## Blockers

### B1. xbeta fits fail with "NA/NaN gradient" once the shapes pass a few thousand

The interior term is `RTMB::dbeta(z, a, b)` with
`z = (y + kappa) / (1 + 2 kappa)` on the tape. Its gradient is `NaN`
from a moderate precision on.

- `dev/fams2-rev-nan.R` section 5 (seed 4200, n = 1000, mu 0.047,
  kappa 0.05): the 845 interior rows alone give a finite gradient at
  phi 1e3 and 2e3, and `NaN` at phi 5e3 and 2e4. The 155 boundary rows
  (the continued fraction) are finite at every phi. Section 6: the same
  rows through `RTMB::dbeta()` with a DATA first argument, as `Beta()`
  has it, are finite at every phi.
- End to end (`dev/fams2-rev-phi5k.R`, `dev/fams2-rev-dens.R` B):
  `frm(y ~ 1, family = xbeta())` stops at "NA/NaN gradient evaluation"
  at phi 1e4, 2e4 and 2e5. `Beta()` on the interior rows of the same
  data fits.
- It also pre-empts the lane's own `kappa` check.
  `dev/fams2-rev-dens.R` A1 (seed 4101: an xbeta truth with kappa 1,
  phi 200, mu 0.5, n 2000, no row at 0 or 1) and A3 (kappa ~ x, seed
  4103) both stop with the error. The maximum likelihood point runs to
  phi 2e5 (see M1), where the gradient is `NaN`.

The findings say xbeta covers "the shapes an xbeta fit visits (phi up
to 1e4)". It does not reach 1e4. A likely fix is the explicit log
density `(a - 1) log z + (b - 1) log1p(-z) - lbeta(a, b) - log(d)`.
It must be measured before it is trusted.

### B2. `log_ibeta_half()`'s 50 steps are wrong near its switch point at large shapes, silently

The code comment says "`N = 50` holds the value to 1e-13 of
`stats::pbeta(log.p = TRUE)` over shapes from e^-4 to e^16". The
worker's sweep draws `x` and the shapes independently, so it seldom
lands near `m = (a + 1) / (a + b + 2)`. There the fraction needs
O(sqrt(max(a, b))) steps (Rmpfr run to convergence: 1041 to 1088 steps
at shape 1e6).

`dev/fams2-rev-ibeta.R` (output `.txt`), error
`|ours - pbeta| / max(1, |pbeta|)`:

| shape sum | x within 1e-2 of m, worst (`dev/fams2-rev-thresh.R`) |
|---|---|
| 1e3 | 2.1e-13 |
| 3e3 | 1.6e-11 |
| 5e3 | 5.2e-9 |
| 1e4 | 1.7e-6 |
| 2e4 | 1.0e-4 |
| 1e5 | 2.5e-2 |

At shape 1e6 and x = m, `ours` is -1.693 against -0.693 (pbeta and
Rmpfr agree to 4e-14). N = 200 gives 3e-5 there, and N = 2000 gives
5e-11. The xbeta form (q = kappa / (1 + 2 kappa) near mu, section C):
errors of 5.9e-9 at phi 1e4, 4.0e-3 at phi 1e5 and 0.28 at phi 1e6.

Across the switch, value and derivatives do not join above a few
thousand (section D): at a = 3000, b = 7000, eps = 1e-12, the value
jumps -1.6e-6 where pbeta moves 1e-10, and the gradients are 172.4861
and 172.4912 on the two sides. At 3e4 / 7e4 the gradients are 613 and
809 (the reference is 549). Below shape sum 1e3 everything joins: the
gradient to 10 digits and the third derivative to 5 digits of a finite
difference.

It reaches a fit when B1 does not fire, for example when kappa is held
at a constant (then `z` is data). `dev/fams2-rev-kfix.R`, seed 4200,
n = 1000, `bf(y ~ 1, kappa = 0.05)`, true mu = q (503 zeros),
phi 2e5: `logLik()` is 2711.0102. The reference density at frmtmb's
own estimates is 2712.6852 (a difference of -1.68). The reference
optimum is 2713.3807 at mu 0.045452 and phi 202144. frmtmb reports
mu 0.045431 and phi 191360. At phi 5e4 the difference is 2.1e-6.
The configurations with the true mu at 0.047 are exact to 3e-7.

What to fix (the choice is the lane's): more steps, an asymptotic
branch for large shapes near the mean (TOMS 708's `basym`), or at
least a fit-end refusal or warning when a boundary row's shapes pass
about 2e3 near the switch. In any case, correct the code comment and
the findings.

## Major, not blocking

- **M1. The `kappa` warning's premise is false, so its advice can be
  wrong.** "With no row at exactly 0 or 1 the likelihood rises as
  kappa falls to zero" is false. In A1 (`dev/fams2-rev-a1.R`, output
  `.txt`), the profile log-likelihood rises with kappa: 1660.73 at
  log kappa -6, 1666.51 at 0 and 1666.61 at 1. The reference optimum
  is kappa 47 at phi 2e5. `Beta()` gives 1660.58. When the fit
  completes (`optimizer = "optim"`), it warns "kappa goes to 0, where
  the model is Beta() ... Use Beta()". The data favor kappa > 0 by
  6.04 nats over Beta(). The no-false-alarm side the worker tested
  holds: A2 (473 zeros, no ones, seed 4102) does not warn and places
  kappa at 0.129 (truth 0.2). Recommended wording: without exact 0s
  or 1s, `kappa` is placed only by the interior shape, and it can run
  to 0 or grow without bound.

### Minor (not blocking)

- **m1. Third derivatives in the tie blend of `log_pbeta_ad()` are
  far less accurate than plain `log(RTMB::pbeta())`**
  (`dev/fams2-rev3-near.txt`, relative error against Rmpfr):

  | point | package d3 | plain pbeta d3 |
  |---|---|---|
  | t1 + 0.75 gap at (1e6, 3e6) | 1.2e-1 | 3e-9 |
  | (a + 1) / (a + b + 2) at (1e6, 3e6) | 1.6e-2 | 1.3e-9 |
  | t1 + 0.75 gap at (3e4, 7e4) | 7.7e-5 | 1.7e-10 |
  | (a + 1) / (a + b + 2) at (2e3, 8e3) | 6.1e-7 | 1.1e-11 |
  | (a + 1) / (a + b + 2) at (300, 700) | 9.4e-10 | 1.5e-12 |

  Second derivatives there reach 1.3e-6 at (1e6, 3e6). The cause is
  that the blend weight's k-th derivative scales as gap^-k, with gap
  about 1/(a + b). It multiplies the rounding difference between the
  two exact forms. The band is about 1/sqrt(a) sd wide, so few rows
  land in it. It touches only the Laplace gradient (third order) and,
  at 1e-6, the Hessian. Values and first derivatives are unaffected.

## Second pass

### 1. The new tolerance, and the residual band

The form is now, per pair and per column,

    tol = 1e-8 * max(|a|, |b|)  +  1e-11 * max|finite column|

**The first-pass construction is closed.** `dev/rev2-gddm-band.R`, seed
909, my own install:

```
1/2/3 s beside ten years   max 3.15e+08  floor 0.00315  spread 2  REFUSED
the same column, centered  max 1.57e+08  floor 0.00157  spread 2  REFUSED
```

Both refused, so the centering dependence the first form had is gone.
That was the sharper half of the first-pass finding and it is the half
now measurable on both spellings of the same column.

**The residual band is exactly the floor, and I measured where it
flips.** On the same ten-year column, stepping the within-condition
difference:

| within-condition step | result |
|---|---|
| 1.0e-01 s | REFUSED |
| 1.0e-02 s | REFUSED |
| 3.2e-03 s | REFUSED |
| 3.1e-03 s | accepted |
| 1.0e-03 s | accepted |

The flip sits on `1e-11 * 3.15e8 = 3.15e-3`, so the band is the floor
term and nothing else. The rule that follows is general and is the
honest way to state the limit: **the guard misses a within-condition
difference `s` on a column of maximum `M` when `M / s` exceeds about
1e11.**

**Is that reachable?** I tried two ways and could not, except by a
construction I do not believe in.

- *By dynamic range in the covariate itself.* The widest meaningful
  ranges I can name in this field are a delay from one second to ten
  years (3.15e8), money from one cent to a million dollars (1e8), and
  an epoch-millisecond trial onset against a one-second within-condition
  difference (1.76e9). All are two to three orders below the 1e11
  needed.
- *By a sentinel, which is how 1e11 of dynamic range can appear without
  1e11 of meaningful range.* Real values of 1 and 3 beside a sentinel:

  | sentinel | floor | result |
  |---|---|---|
  | 1e+06 | 1e-05 | REFUSED |
  | 1e+09 | 0.01 | REFUSED |
  | 1e+11 | 1 | REFUSED |
  | 1e+12 | 10 | **accepted** |

  So it is reachable at a sentinel of 1e12 against values of order 1.
  Behavioral data uses 999, -999, 9999 and `NA`; a sentinel eleven
  orders above the data is not a shape I can point at, and a linear
  drift term on such a column is already meaningless for reasons that
  have nothing to do with this guard.

I also checked the other side, because a column has to be usable before
its band matters. `dev/rev2-gddm-band.R` section 3: a fixed-effect
column whose total relative range falls below about 1e-6 is **dropped
from the design as rank deficient**, with a warning naming it (kept at
1e-6, dropped from 1e-7 down, measured on a base of 1e9). The band is
1e-11 relative, five orders narrower than the narrowest range a column
can have and still be fitted at all. That does not by itself close the
sentinel case, whose total range is wide, but it closes every case
where the whole column is squeezed.

**What form would close it completely, if a later round wants to.**
Apply the floor only to COMPUTED columns. The floor exists for `poly()`,
whose noise comes from the model frame RE-EVALUATING a call; a column
named by a bare symbol is data as supplied and carries no arithmetic of
its own, which is exactly the argument `?gddm` already makes for the
`vreal()` comparison being exact. `gd_check_condition_constancy()`
already parses each column name, so `is.call(str2lang(cn))` is the
whole test, and on a symbol column the tolerance would collapse to
`1e-8 * max(|a|, |b|)`, eight significant digits of the entries being
compared, which cannot hide anything at any dynamic range. Recorded
rather than blocked on: the band it closes needs 1e12 of dynamic range
to reach.

### 2. The sweep, which the tightened tolerance could have invalidated

Rerun on my own install (`dev/rev2-gddm-sweep-log.txt`): **21 designs,
20 of which the check could fire on, 0 false alarms; 26 drop cases, 0
missed, 0 unnamed.** Identical to the lane's rerun.

The extra designs from my first pass were rerun under the new tolerance
(`dev/rev2-gddm-designs-log.txt`): unbalanced conditions, a missing
cell, a covariate drawn once per condition, a computed within-condition
constant from `ave()`, and `mo()` all still accepted; all still refused
when a variable is dropped from the index.

**The shape most at risk under the tighter form is a computed column
whose entries are near zero**, where the per-pair term nearly vanishes
and only the floor is left. That is `poly()`, so I scanned it wider
than the sweep does, over 4 to 12 levels and degrees 1 to 4
(`dev/rev2-gddm-band.R` section 5), reporting the worst
`|row - first row| / its own tolerance`:

| levels | worst ratio over degrees 1 to 4 |
|---|---|
| 4 | 2.30e-05 |
| 6 | 4.53e-05 |
| 8 | 8.29e-06 |
| 12 | 9.94e-06 |

The worst case anywhere in that family is 4.53e-05, that is **2.2e+04x
headroom**, against the 8.55e+04 the sweep reports for its own design.
Tightening by 1000x did not manufacture a false alarm on the shape that
would produce one first.

### 3. `gddm_simulate()`, new behavior on a user-facing function

Measured as a paired sweep, the way the density check's was, on both

| arm | legitimate accepted | bad refused, naming the parameter |
|---|---|---|
| BEFORE, `rellib-0552` | 16 of 16 | **0 of 5** |

The legitimate shapes are: scalars; a length-`n` parameter constant

`lapse` among the parameters `...` takes, but the formal argument
`lapse = c("none", "uniform")` swallows the name, so that parameter
cannot be passed at all.

### 4. The compat rows, and whether the resolution rule is real

It is real, and documented rather than inferred. `R/compat.R` scores
each pattern (`"*"` 0, `"kind:<kind>"` 1, `"group:"` 2, exact name 3),
compares two rules on their two specificities sorted descending, and
`frmtmb_compat_validate()` (asserted by `test-compat.R`) errors on a
disagreeing tie that is not marked `override`.
`("gddm", "kind:covstruct")` scores (3,1) against the core default
`("kind:family", "kind:covstruct")` at (1,1), so it wins by
construction. Two extensions already take that route:
`hmm x kind:covstruct` and `lca x kind:covstruct` in `frmtmb.latent`.

Verified by running the table rather than trusting the score:

| family | covstruct rows | statuses |
|---|---|---|
| gddm | 24 | **conditional 24** |
| wiener | 24 | conditional 15, works 9 |
| lba | 24 | conditional 15, works 9 |
| rdm | 24 | conditional 15, works 9 |
| wiener_gng | 24 | conditional 15, works 9 |

All 24 gddm rows carry the family-side note, not the core one. The
other four families keep exactly the 9-works split gddm itself had
before this change, which I printed in the first pass, so the family
row applied where it was meant to and leaked nowhere. `mixture` reads
`refused` and `predict` reads `conditional`.

The `predict` note now records what I measured: neither `vint()` column
is mandatory on newdata today. That was my first-pass finding and it is
stated with the measurement rather than deleted.

### The middle path

I agree with the framing. The 60x prices refining SILENTLY, and it does
rule that out; it does not rule out an opt-in that reports the
condition count it produced, which is the version where the cost is
visible. Filing `gddm_control(refine_conditions = TRUE)` as a plan row
rather than closing the question is the right disposition, and the
message improvement in nit 1 already delivers most of the practical
benefit today.

## What is still open

Nothing that blocks. Three lines somebody should carry forward:

1. The residual band, `M / s > 1e11`, with the computed-column-only
   floor as the form that closes it. Worth a sentence in the plan row
   so the limit is recorded as measured rather than rediscovered.
2. `?gddm_simulate` lists `lapse` among the parameters `...` accepts,
   but the formal `lapse = c("none", "uniform")` makes that impossible.
   Pre-existing, measured on both arms.
3. The residual sufficiency caveat: `mi()` gives two missing entries in
   one condition two distinct latent parameters, and the guard treats
   two `NA`s as one datum by design. Every mixed construction is
   refused because the observed rows differ from the missing ones; the
   fully unobserved case is degenerate.

## The plan

Agreed on **1.0e**, with the tolerance stated as a measured limit
(`1e-8 * max(|a|,|b|) + 1e-11 * colmax`, band at `M / s > 1e11`) and
`gddm_simulate()` named as covered rather than as a gap. The Phase 4
row on `predict(type = "response")` and `conditional_effects()` is
accurate.

### MAJOR

**M1. `hypotheses(fit, joint = TRUE)` tests the group-level effects,
silently.** `dev/review-mfxse-hyp.R`, poisson `(1 + x | g)`, 12 groups.
marginaleffects' `joint_test()` calls `get_coef(object)` and
`get_vcov(object)` with no arguments (`mesrc/joint_test.R`), so the
`hypothesis_is_formula` key never arrives:

|---|---|---|
| `hypotheses(fit, joint = TRUE)` | F 15.44, df1 3, df2 234 | **F 328.3, df1 27**, df2 234, p 0 |
| `hypotheses(fit, joint = c(2, 3))` | F 21.94, df1 2 | F 21.94, df1 2 |
| `hypotheses(avg_slopes(fit), joint = TRUE)` | F 3.02 | F 136.1 (the SE change, correct direction) |

The default joint test is now a Wald test that 24 BLUPs are 0, with the
population-level coefficients. NEWS says `hypotheses()` "reads the
coefficients alone, as before". A string `joint = "x"` is matched exactly
first, so it still reads 1 row; a pattern that is not a coefficient name
now greps the `b.` names. The other `hypotheses()` paths hold on both
builds: `"b2 = 0"`, `"b2 = b3"`, `~ reference`, `~ pairwise`, `vcov = ~ g`,
and `hypotheses(avg_slopes(fit), ...)` agree with `fixef()` to the digits
printed.

On the signal itself (item 3): `hypothesis_is_formula` is an argument of
internal functions (`get_hypotheses()`, `get_hypotheses_skeleton()`,
`hypotheses_exact_jacobian()`), not documented in any Rd of 1.0.0
(`meexp3.log`), and absent from `joint_test()`. There is no public
signal. The only documented extension hooks are `get_coef`, `set_coef`,
`get_vcov`, `get_predict`, `get_model_matrix` (`meexp.log`). A positive
key is more robust than the negative one: the prediction machinery passes
`internal_call` in the dots of `get_predict()` (`why.log`: dots
`allow_new_levels internal_call`); check whether it reaches `get_coef()`
from `get_delta_jacobian()` and key the `b` part on that. Failing that,
refuse `joint =` on a fit whose `get_coef()` holds `b`, by name, as a
stated limit. Add the joint row to the test.

**M2. The new-level warning is swallowed for slopes and contrasts, where
the omission is largest.** `dev/review-mfxse-vcovwarn.R`,
`-follow.R`, `-why4.R`, poisson `(1 | g)`, rows at level 3, `new`, `new2`:

| call | `frmtmb_interop_se_part` warnings reaching the caller |
|---|---|
| `predictions(allow_new_levels = TRUE)` | 1 |
| `avg_predictions(allow_new_levels = TRUE)` | 1 |
| `avg_slopes(variables = "x", allow_new_levels = TRUE)` | **0** |
| `avg_comparisons(variables = "f", allow_new_levels = TRUE)` | **0** |

`why4.log` instruments `interop_rows_check()`: it does raise the warning
in `avg_slopes()`, on the one armed call, and the warning does not reach
the caller. The cause is in marginaleffects: `comparisons()` calls
`get_vcov()` before it builds its plan (`follow.log`: `get_vcov` is the
first call), so the armed `get_predict()` is the estimate pass inside
`get_predict_error()`, which wraps it in `myTryCatch()` and muffles every
warning (`why5.log`). In `predictions()` the estimate pass comes first,
so the armed call is a Jacobian call, which is not muffled.

What the slope omits there (`follow.log`, 1e5 draws of the new level's
effect at the estimates, seed 7412): SD of the slope over the effect 9.48
and 18.75 at the two new rows, against a delta SE of 0.836 and 1.684.

inside the muffled pass (`hyp-before.log` shows it reaching the caller),
so every call on a mixed fit warned; this is a regression in what the
user is told (not rerun on rellib-r18 for this exact call). Fix: do not disarm on the
first armed call; warn on the first call that can reach the user, for
example keep the check armed until a call outside the estimate pass, or
check in every `get_predict()` of the call and deduplicate on a per-call
key that `get_vcov()` resets. Add the slope and contrast rows to
"what no parameter holds is still said, by name".

### MINOR

**m1. Cost: no simple restriction gives most of it back; say what is
possible.** `dev/review-mfxse-cost.R`, poisson `(1 + x | g)`, 500 levels,
20000 rows. `predictions()` on 2 rows (levels 1 and 500) perturbs all 1002
parameters; 6 Jacobian columns are nonzero. 3.93 s, against 0.020 s with
`vcov = FALSE`. Per call: `get_predict()` on 2 rows 1.20 ms, `set_coef()`
0.60 ms, `get_coef()` 1.00 ms. `get_coef()` gets no `newdata`, so it cannot
restrict; a memo in `get_predict()` that returns the cached value when only
unread `b` moved would save about 1.2 ms of the 3.9 ms per parameter, about
a third. The large gain is the analytic Jacobian: `frm_lp_basis()` over all
20000 rows takes 0.29 s against `avg_predictions()` 10.32 s. marginaleffects
1.0.0 reaches an analytic route only for a class whitelist (`lm`, `glm`,
and 9 others, `add_model_matrix_attribute()`), or through the global
setting `jacobian_function` (`settings_get()`, undocumented), which a
package should not set. So the lane's "no exported hook" is right, and
speed is not a MAJOR by the brief's test. One cost is the lane's own:
`interop_joint_from_outer()` inverts `Qrr` dense with no size guard:
`predictions(nd2, vcov = ~ g)` took 21.09 s at 500 levels, and on a
latent-residual fit `Qrr` is `n x n` with no `frmtmb.latres_dense_rows`
check.

**m2. The sandwich claims.** `dev/review-mfxse-vcovwarn.R`, seed 9301.
`"HC0"` and `"HC1"` are refused on every fit with a group-level term
("the random effect 1 | g crosses `cluster`"), so NEWS's and the help's
"a sandwich (`"HC0"`, `"HC1"`, `~ g`) is carried into the group-level
effects" can only ever mean `~ g`. On the algebra: `Qrr^-1 + J Vf J'` is
the first-order prediction-error decomposition of `b - b(theta_hat)` with
`Var(theta_hat)` replaced; it is valid for the outer part and leaves the
inner `Qrr^-1` model-based. That inner part is 0.022 of 0.034 (homoskedastic)
and 0.036 of 0.052 (heteroskedastic) of a level's variance here, which is
why CR1 / default reads 1.000, 1.000, 0.999 and 1.028, 1.004, 1.011.
State in the help that a sandwich makes the outer part robust and the
conditional part stays model-based. A cluster bootstrap was not run: it
estimates the sampling variance of a population average, not the
prediction error of a seen level, so it is not the same target.

**m3. A coefficient-only user matrix gives the known-wrong answer.**
`vcovwarn.log`: with `get_vcov(fit)[1:3, 1:3]` as `vcov`, the level SEs
are 1.818, 0.809, 10.289 where the default reads 0.492, 0.396, 2.565: the
0.79.1 convention, with a warning. glmmTMB and lme4 hold the effects
fixed for every `vcov`; brms has no `vcov`. A better answer exists:
carry the user's matrix as the `beta` block of `Vf`, with the model's
`theta` block and cross terms, through `J`. That is a decision for the
user (question 2). Also test that the sandwich's `b` block differs from
the default's (mutant `sandown`).

**m4. A latent residual's `b` is read by a new row under `cov = TRUE`.**

**m6. Item 7, the exact-`gp()` slope: leave it filed, drop one claim.**
`gp.log`. frmtmb's delta method is internally consistent: `y ~ z + gp(x)`,
`gp(x, gr = FALSE)`, `gp(x, k = 15)` and `gp(x, k = 40)` all read SE 0.0308
(estimate -0.15657); `s(x, k = 20)` reads 0.0428. The inner part
(`Qrr^-1`, theta held) is 0.0288 of the 0.0308, so the derivative of the
field in `x` is applied to the right quantity, at a converged step. The
gap to brms is the length scale. A profile of the conditional mode over
log `lscale` (inner problem re-solved): the average slope moves by
-0.0051, -0.0005, -0.0055, -0.0086 at -1, -0.5, +0.5, +1 SE, then -0.0558
at +2 and -0.1546 at +3 SE. Flat near the mode and steep in the upper
tail, which a posterior with mass there (brms's priors on `lscale`) turns
into a wide SD and a linearization cannot see. Recommendation: leave, not
warn by class: a warning on every field slope would be noise, and the
values a user reads (estimate, `fitted()`, `frm_lp_basis()`) agree. One
claim should go: "half the SD of draws of the joint covariance" (0.067)
is not a reference. The delta method is invariant to centering the field
(the `Qrr^-1` and `J` blocks transform with the map), and a Gaussian draw
in non-centered `(theta, z)` coordinates is not; the 0.067 measures the
coordinates, not the uncertainty.

# Adversarial review of lane impsamp, 2026-11-02

Base d5d0056d (frmtmb 0.78.0). Under review: the uncommitted diff in
`frmtmb-wt-impsamp`, installed in `wt-impsamp-lib`. Scripts are
`dev/review-impsamp-*`, logs are under `dev/review-impsamp-out/` (gitignored).
Every reference is written from base R densities and shares no code with the
package, except `us_sigma()` for the map from theta to a covariance.

## Verdict: FIX

The estimator is right and on the lane's grid the fix does what it says. It
has one defect the grid cannot see: `imp_anchor_slopes()` takes its central
differences at a fixed step in the coefficient's own units. A covariate on an
ordinary scale, such as an uncentered calendar year, moves the predictor far
over that step. Then the tape's mode and curvature are wrong, and the SEs come
back wrong or the iteration walks as it did before, or the lane's new refusal
fires and gives a draw count as the remedy. A one-line change of step removes
all of it (B1). The refusal has a false branch and a memory cost (M1, M2), the
lane's key design point has no test (M3), and the MCSE reads low on designs
where the vignette now says it reads high (M4).

| | finding | what to do |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | step of `imp_anchor_slopes()` is not scale-free: wrong SEs, walking, refusals, on `y ~ year + (1 \| g)` | step in units of the predictor |
| M1 | the refusal says "the same fit settles at importance = 1200" of a fit that is capped and walking | a capped run has not settled |
| M2 | the refusal's worst case: 13.3 minutes and 22.1 GB on 2400 rows | lower the cell bound, catch failure |
| M3 | reverting the total-derivative rate fails no test; no test compares the objective off the anchor with an exact value | add the tests |
| M4 | MCSE too low, no warning, on t and on hard designs; the vignette says the opposite | correct the text |
| m1 to m7 | below | |

## BLOCKER

### B1. The slopes' step is absolute, so the fix fails on a covariate's scale

`imp_anchor_slopes()`: `h <- imp_cross_step * max(1, abs(par))`, with
`imp_cross_step = 1e-4`. For a coefficient on a column of scale `s`, that
moves the predictor by `1e-4 * s`, and for an intercept that an uncentered
column made large, by `1e-4 * |intercept|`. Its comment says the step is "far
below any scale the slopes change on". It is not. The lane's grid has only
standard columns.

Measured with the model unchanged by an affine map of the column, so the
slope's SE, theta and logLik must not change. `y ~ year + (1 | g)`, `year =
round(2000 + 5 z)`, 15 groups of 10, 300 draws, data seeds 1 to 6,
`dev/review-impsamp-year.R`, log `year-after.log`, against the same fit on the
centered column:

| poisson, seed | rounds | outcome | slope SE ratio | slope shift (SE) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 5 | capped, "raise rounds", moved 10.3 | 1.47 | -0.32 |
| 2 | 5 | capped | 1.23 | -0.11 |
| 3 | 5 | capped | 1.22 | -0.12 |
| 4 | | stops: "did not settle" | | |
| 5 | 5 | capped, moved 7.7 | 1.47 | -0.29 |
| 6 | | stops: "did not settle", after 16.5 s | | |

On bernoulli the fit is SILENT: rounds 2, 0 warnings, slope SE ratio 1.02 to
1.09 over the six seeds, fresh gradient up to 0.034 against `grad_tol` 1e-3.
That the base build read 1.00 on all of these (where it fit at all, capped)
makes the SE error a regression. The base build's stops are the defect the
lane was given, so 5 of 6 poisson stops on base become 2 of 6 stops and 4 of 6
capped.

Extreme forms, one data seed, `dev/review-impsamp-scale.R`, `scale-se.txt`:

| column | family | result, lane build | slope SE ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| `1e4 x` | poisson | silent, fresh gradient 19.3 | 1.024 |
| `1e5 x` | poisson | fresh gradient 2.6e209, optimizer stops where it started (moved 4e-15), so the "corrected" fit IS the Laplace optimum; "false convergence" is the only warning | NaN |
| `x + 2000` | bernoulli | silent, rounds 2 | **1.135** |
| `x + 2000` | poisson | capped, moved 55.4 | NaN |
| `100 x + 1e4` | both | stops: "Use importance = 1200 or more" after 15 s, 35 s | |

`plan$hlik_rate` for the slope reaches -8.9e210 at `1e5 x`, and `cross_d`
1.2e15 (`review-impsamp-fixcheck.R`), so `exp(rate' dtheta)` overflows at any
step.

The cause is shown by construction. Mutant `fix_x`
(`dev/review-impsamp-fixx.R`, under `rev-impsamp-src/`) sets a beta
coordinate's step to `1e-4 / max |X_j|`, one edit, nothing else. On it, all 12
year fits settle in 2 rounds with no warning and slope SE ratio 1.000
(`year-fixx.log`); every row above has SE ratio 1.000 to 1.0002 and 2 or 3
rounds; the stops are gone; `test-importance.R` passes 277 of 277. `1e5 x` and
`x + 1e5` still warn "false convergence", as a column that ill-conditioned
may; on `1e5 x` the fit has moved and its SEs are right.

An uncentered column in a dispersion's regression is the same defect on a
smaller scale: `shape ~ year`, 4 data seeds, logLik and rounds fine, fresh
gradient up to 6e-3 where the centered column reads 4e-4 (`year2-after.txt`).
A random slope on an uncentered column has its own trouble below the
correction: the Laplace fit lands 10 to 20 nats away from the centered one on
2 of 4 seeds, so that shape says nothing about this fix.

**Fix.** Scale the step by the predictor, as in `fix_x` (the frame has `lp$X`;
`spHess(random = FALSE)` is no help, since its fixed-effect diagonal is 0 on
this tape: mutant `fix_h` measured that and changed nothing). Do not scale by
`|par|` for a beta. Give a `betad` coefficient its design column too (`fix_x`
covers `beta` alone). A coordinate with no column (theta) keeps today's rule.
Correct the comment on `imp_cross_step`. Add a test: the same fit on `x` and
on `100 x + 1e4`, slope and theta and logLik and SEs equal, as a ratio, on
poisson. It fails on the lane's build and passes on `fix_x`. A warning that
says "raise rounds" is advice to a user whose problem is a step size, so
nothing else helps them.

## MAJOR

### M1. The refusal can state something false

`imp_refuse_draws()` tests `!next_try$rose` and never looks at `capped`.
`dev/review-impsamp-refuse.R b`, `refuse-b.txt`, bernoulli `y ~ xx + (1 | g)`,
`xx = 100 x + 1e4`, data seed 1:

    `importance = 300` did not settle on this model. [...] The worst group's
    effective sample size is 0.97 of its draws, which is 292 of 300. The same fit
    settles at importance = 1200 [...] Use importance = 1200 or more

A user who obeys gets, at `importance = 1200`: 5 rounds, capped, moves 1.76,
5.79, 11.22, 12.75, 12.49, fresh gradient 114. So the count named was seen NOT
to settle, and the message's "a count it saw work" is untrue. The ESS the
message quotes (0.97) says the draws were never short. At `importance = 64`
the same model fits in 2 rounds, so the rise is a matter of the tape's slopes,
not the count. Natural firing exists, which answers the lane's "I found no
natural case": it goes away with B1, so construct a new one after that fix.

The second branch overclaims too. "so more draws alone are not the remedy"
prints after however many doublings the memory bound allowed, which can be one
(`refuse-c.txt`, 20000 rows, 500 draws: "It did not settle at importance =
1000 either ... so more draws alone are not the remedy", after 638.7 s and
20.2 GB). At 1500 draws on those rows it says "A larger count is past the 1000
draws this check tries", a number below the user's own. Say what was tried and
no more.

**Fix.** `settled <- !rose && !capped`. Word the second branch by what was
tried.

### M2. The refusal's worst case

`refuse-a.txt`, poisson `(1 | g)`, 100 groups of 24, 2400 rows (just under
`2e7 / 8192`), `importance = 512`, the lane's own wrapper so that no count
settles:

| | seconds | peak working set |
|---|---|---|
| the ordinary fit at 512 | 23.6 | 2.36 GB |
| the refusal, 1024 to 8192 | 796.7, 33.8 times the fit | 22.1 GB |

The machine was under load, so read the seconds as an upper range. The memory
is not load: 1.1 KB per cell where the comment says "hundreds of bytes per row
and draw". On 2400 rows, a user on a 16 GB machine meets `bad_alloc` instead
of the message, and the message is what this branch exists to give. An
`iterate(m)` that errors is not caught either.

**Fix.** Lower `imp_refuse_cells` to what 2 GB holds (about 2e6), or stop at
two doublings, wrap `iterate()` in `tryCatch()`, and report what it could not
try. NEWS carries the bound and needs the new number.

### M3. Reverting the lane's key design point fails no test

`dev/review-impsamp-mutants.R`, `review-impsamp-mutrun.sh`, each mutant a
source copy in `rev-impsamp-src/` and its own library, with the guard that the
mutant's library loaded. An earlier run of mine lacked the guard, installs
failed, and every "mutant" passed 277 of 277 on the lane's own build; those
logs are not used. `test-importance.R`, `mut2/test-*.log`, baseline 277 pass:

| mutant | edit | result |
|---|---|---|
| `partial_rate` | the partial derivative of the curvature, not the total | **277 pass, 0 fail**; one escaped warning |
| `no_cut` | `abs()` in place of the cut of a negative curvature, q = 1 | **277 pass, 0 fail** |
| `half_jacobian` | half the log-determinant's move off the anchor (6 named tests, `mut2/jac-half.txt`) | textbook, curvature, numDeriv, anchor tests pass; gaussian and scalar-intercept tests error |
| `no_jacobian` | a frozen log-determinant with moving draws (`mut2/jac-no.txt`) | gaussian and scalar-intercept tests error, textbook fails 6 |
| `one_round`, `grad_always` | stop after round 1 whatever the gradient | 276 pass, 1 error: `moves[[2L]]` out of bounds, nothing about accuracy |
| `no_rate` | curvature frozen | gaussian test only, fail 2, error 1 |
| `no_cross` | mode ignores the likelihood's slope | 3 tests fail |
| `no_prior_mode` | the prior's move left out of the mode | 3 tests fail |
| `frozen` | the old estimator | 7 tests fail |
| `map_recycle` | the old `imp_par_list()` | the constant dpar test fails |
| `no_refusal` | the refusal removed | its 2 tests fail |

`partial_rate` is the lane's third design point, "the curvature's rate must be
the TOTAL derivative", with its measurement (4.1 against 8.1, 2 of 648
rising). No test holds it. `half_jacobian` is a wrong density at every theta
but the anchor. Two of six named tests see it, both by an error at the end of the
fit and neither by comparing a value: the textbook test passes 15 of 15. Nothing
compares the objective off the anchor with an exact value. The
whole-file runs of the two Jacobian mutants did not end in 45 minutes, since
every fit climbs the refusal's ladder, and I stopped them; the 6 named tests
are what I have.

Old tests (`oldtest-importance.txt`, rellib-r16's file on the lane's build):
17 fail, 1 error, in 6 tests, each on the expectation the lane's table names,
so the claim that the fix removed what they constructed holds. See m3 for what
the rewrites lost.

**Fix.** Add two tests. (1) On the poisson slope the lane measured,
`obj$he(at)[1, 1]` of the tape against a central difference of the Laplace
gradient, as a ratio, which fails on `partial_rate`. (2) The objective at
theta displaced by 2 SE in each coordinate against `quadrature = TRUE` or an
`integrate()` in the test, within 4 MCSE, which must fail on `half_jacobian`.

## What I verified that held

Item 1, the objective.
- The density: `u` regressed on `z` at a moved theta gives `log|L|` equal to
  the tape's log-determinant to 2e-15 (q = 1, 2, 3); the weights' joint
  density equals the plain joint objective to 9e-9 at moved draws
  (`review-impsamp-unbiased.R`, `-multid.R`, 7 + 7 designs).

  `integrate()`, with the anchor held fixed, at the anchor, sd up and down by

  `re_sigma`, `smallpois`, `t_outlier`, `wtbin`; 0.96 to 1.07 on sd 4 and 0.89

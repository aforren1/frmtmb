## Verdict: NOT MERGEABLE (one blocking item, with a measured cheap fix)

The lane's own claims reproduce, most of them to the digit, and on the
designs they were built for the change is a large improvement: every
scale trap I could construct on gaussian, student, skew_normal,
exgaussian, weights, offsets, `sigma ~ x` and multivariate responses is
closed, and the restart and `mo()` fixes do what they say. The
blocking item is a regression the lane's studies could not see because
they only rescale `y`: the unit is the family's start of `sigma`, which
for gaussian() is `sd(y)`, so a covariate that explains most of `y`
gets a unit far above the residual sd, and fits that reach lme4's
maximum now end at "false convergence (8)" with a warning. Taking the
unit from the residual sd removes it and leaves the lane's scale study
and `mo()` study unchanged (measured below).

## Blocking

### B1. High-R^2 gaussian fits: false convergence warnings at the maximum

Design: `y = 1 + k x + b_g + e`, b sd 0.5, e sd 1, 20 groups of 5,
`y ~ x + (1 | g)`, seeds 1 to 40 (`dev/optrap-rev-r2b.R`, logs
`dev/optrap-rev-log/r2b-*.txt`). Columns: fits with any warning, fits
at a nonzero code, fits more than 1e-4 below lme4.

| k | rellib-r7 | lane | lane, unit from residual sd |
|---|---|---|---|
| 300 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 4 / 4 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| 1e3 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 12 / 13 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| 3e3 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 25 / 26 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 |
| 1e4 | 0 / 0 / 0 | 33 / 34 / 0 | 0 / 0 / 0 |

Every lane warning is "Optimizer did not report convergence: false
convergence (8)", on fits within 1.8e-8 of lme4's log-likelihood
(`dev/optrap-rev-r2.R`, k = 1e4: min logLik - lme4 = -1.79e-08; lane
units 8192 and 16384). k = 10 and 100 are clean on both builds.

Which part does it (same script, `OPTRAP_ABLATE`): without the inner
tolerance 4 / 12 / 29 / 39 still warn; without the sd start 2 / 15 /
28 / 34 warn; without the coefficient units the warnings go but 34,
36, 34 and 35 of 40 end short of lme4 (the sd started at `log(sd(y))`,
far above a group sd of 0.5). So the unit itself is wrong for this
design, not one of its uses.

Candidate fix, measured: `dev/optrap-rev-resid.R` replaces the unit of
a gaussian() or student() `mu` by `2^round(log2(sd(r)))`, `r` the
residuals of the fixed design (`mu_residuals()`, which the lane already
calls for skew_normal()), same exclusions. Results: the table's last
column; the lane's own scale study (`dev/optrap-rev-scalecopy.R`, a
copy of `dev/optrap-scale.R` writing to my log dir) gives the same
numbers line for line (`scalecopy-resid.txt` against
`scalecopy-lane.txt`: 0 short in every row, invariance 1.68e-07 to
1.14e-08); the `mo()` study (`dev/optrap-rev-mo-resid.R`, seeds 1 to
400) gives 200 / 200 and 200 / 200 at the exact maximum with the same
116783 and 102579 evaluations. `test-optrap.R`'s assertion that the
unit is within sqrt(2) of `sd(y)` would need to name the residual sd.
The same reasoning probably applies to the other location families
whose `sigma` start reads `y` rather than residuals; I measured only
gaussian.

## Minors

m1. **The convergence trip-wire's `grad_units()` gives false warnings at
small response scales.** `grad_units()` takes the response's share out
of `par_units` for `check_convergence()` and `grad_verdict()`, so the
trip-wire reads a raw gradient that is `1/u` too large at small `u`.
Student, `bf(y ~ x + (1 | g), sigma ~ x)`, y x 1e-6, seeds 1 to 20: 20
of 20 warn "Large maximum absolute gradient at the optimum (7.03 at
(Intercept)) ... may not have converged" (seed 1) on fits at the
maximum (code 0; logLik within 1.6e-7 of glmmTMB on that seed;
`dev/optrap-rev-warn1.R`). Exgaussian at 1e-6: 20 of 20 warn, 2 at a
nonzero code. Patched to the full `par_units`
(`dev/optrap-rev-gu.R`, `OPTRAP_REV_GU=full` in
`dev/optrap-rev-scale.R`): student 1e-6 warns on 0 of 20, exgaussian
on 2 (the 2 at code 8), skew_normal 3 to 1, every other case and scale
unchanged, objectives identical (the trip-wire does not touch the
fit). The case `grad_units()` was written for, `helper-mo-flat.R` in
`test-se-check.R`, passes 127 of 127 with the full units, because the
column spread is now in `par_units`; the only failures are the two
assertions of `test-grad-verdict.R` that state `grad_units()`'s
definition. Recommend dropping `grad_units()` and the response share
`fit$resp_units`. The scale study's `suppressWarnings()` hid this.

m2. **Parameter precision is 2 to 20 times worse in SE units at
moderate units, by construction of the coefficient units.** On
`y ~ x + z` against `lm()` (`dev/optrap-rev-lmprec.R`, seeds 1 to 20,
both builds pinned to P cores), median max |beta - lm| / SE:

| rellib-r7 | 2.0e-7 | 1.7e-7 | 4.8e-7 | 2.0e-6 | 2.8e-6 | 1.2e-5 | 4.2e-6 | 2.76 |
| lane | 2.0e-7 | 4.0e-6 | 5.6e-6 | 9.6e-6 | 1.0e-5 | 1.0e-5 | 6.4e-6 | 1.5e-5 |

Largest on the lane 8.5e-5 SE, largest log-likelihood gap 5.1e-9. With
`predictor_par_units()` taken out (`dev/optrap-rev-lmprec-abl.R`) the

m3. **Mixtures are still in the trap, silently at 1e3** (claim 1 asked
it measured). `y ~ x`, `mixture(gaussian, gaussian)`, seeds 1 to 20,

### Attacks

- **The 0.1 gate** (`dev/optrap-rev4-gate.R`):
  - The gate is patched to 0.1, 0.01 and 0 (any decrease is kept), and
    every attempted step's actual gain over its prediction is
    recorded.
  - Designs: degrees 5 to 8, with and without `(1 | g)`, y x 1 and
    16, plus the `big` design below; 20 seeds each, 340 fits.
  - Of 505 attempted steps, 1 gained less than 0.1 of its prediction
    (0.087).
  - The quantiles of gain over prediction are 0.38 (1 %), 0.72 (5 %),
    1.00 (median) and 1.65 (95 %).
  - The three gates give the same short counts in every design.
  - No design has a silent shortfall under any gate.

  The gate leaves no fit short that a looser gate would reach.
- **optimHess on the Laplace objective, forced**
  (`dev/optrap-rev4-re.R`):
  - The steps are forced on (the condition number patched to Inf) for
    40 ordinary random-effect fits: 8 designs × 5 seeds (gaussian
    random intercept, random slope, crossed, binomial, bernoulli with a
    random slope, poisson, negbinomial, student).
  - Result: 0 steps taken (every predicted gain is below 1e-6), objectives
    and parameters identical with the steps on and off, the same
    warnings and codes, at most 0.03 s per call.
  - The finite-difference noise never clears the 1e-6 threshold.
- **Designs that trigger the steps as built**
  (`dev/optrap-rev4-kappa.R`):
  - The condition number exceeds 1e5 for an uncentered `year +
    I(year^2)` (2.3e5) and for a covariate near 1e5 (6.5e5).
  - It stays below for `year` alone (465), raw cubics on [1, 2]
    (2.2e3), quartics (2.9e4), `age` to the third power (462), factor
    interactions (395), Unix time (360) and sleepstudy (4.7).
  - `dev/optrap-rev4-big.R` against lmer, seeds 1 to 10:
    - `year_sq`: 0 steps, the same fit as without them.
    - `y ~ big + (1 | g)`: 0.69.0 and rellib-r8 are short on 10 of 10
      (by 1.56 to 8.46, 2 of them silent).
    - Build 32 and the merge bring all 6 code-0 fits to lmer to 4e-7,
      both silent ones included. The 4 at code 1 are unchanged and
      warned.

  A pure improvement.
- **How often the steps run on the core suite** (an `R_PROFILE_USER`
  logger, `dev/optrap-rev4-profile.R`, over all 224 core files on

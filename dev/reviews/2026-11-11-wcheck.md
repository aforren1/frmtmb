## Findings

### 1. BLOCKER: the cell rule ignores the centered intercept's prior

frmtmb evaluates a class `Intercept` prior at the centered intercept
`Ic = b0 + means_X'b` (`R/priors.R`, "Where an intercept prior lands").
In `sigma ~ 1 + fac`, the direction `b_facb -> -Inf` with `b0` held
moves `Ic` to -Inf as well, and a `normal()` prior on the Intercept
stops it. The other ray holds `Ic` and sends `b0 -> +Inf`. It gains
`E_b / n_b - W_a / n_a` per row: cell a's sigma grows and costs its
whole weight. So with a bounding Intercept prior, the cell direction is
unbounded only when the mean excess of cell b exceeds the mean weight of
cell a. `prior_column_verdict()` reads only the class `b` entry, finds
none, says "free", and refuses.

Reproducer: `dev/reviews/wcheck-review-center2.R` and `-center3.R`
(seed 20261111, n = 80, fac alternating, `y ~ x + (1 | obs)`,
`sigma ~ 1 + fac`, weights 0.8 on a and 1.05 on b: E_a = -8, E_b = 2,
total -6).

| case | lane | base | objective (with the prior) |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 normal(0, 1) on the sigma Intercept | refused, "the coefficient `facb` of sigma moves alone" | logLik -94.2889, conv 0, betad -0.49884 0.62605 | max -95.2251 at max abs gradient 8.77e-08; best with b_facb <= -5: -100.4097, <= -10: -101.4642, <= -20: -120.3335; profile -10 to -40: -101.46, -107.94, -120.33, -138.66, -162.94, -193.36, -269.81 |
| R2 no prior (control) | refused | logLik -94.2888, conv 0 (a local maximum) | profile slope exactly 2.000 = E_b from b_facb -10 to -30 (-84.92 to -44.92), -35.27 at -35: above the local maximum, unbounded, so this refusal is right |
| R3 normal(0, 1), w_a 0.3, w_b 1.5 (E_b / n_b 0.5 > 0.3) | refused | logLik 612.75, conv 1 | profile rising 16.4 per unit at -14: unbounded, so this refusal is right |

R1 is an ordinary heteroscedastic fit (obs sd at its boundary, log
sigma -0.50 and +0.13) that the lane refuses with a false message. The
lane's own arm "1 + fac, normal on Intercept only" is bounded as well:
with m = 1/2 the maximum sits at `Ic = -2 E_b = -24.6`, and every start
of `wcheck-review-center.R` stops at `Ic` -23.5 to -24.9. The findings'

the test assertion, add R1 as a guard-absent case and R2 as the
refusal, and reword NEWS bullet 3.

### S2. BLOCKER: the sample route takes the MAP objective's link power

The MAP objective is a density in eta, so its power of x carries the
link's Jacobian power j. That is what `natural_jacobian_power()` gives,
and it is right for MAP. A posterior's integrability is a different
quantity. With the Jacobian included,
`int p(x(eta)) |dx/deta| L d eta = int p(x) L dx` for every link. So the
condition is `order + 1 - E > 0` toward 0 and `p - 1 - E > 0` toward
infinity, with power 1 whatever the link. These are 0.81.0's sample
conditions. `prior_entry_verdict()` applies `margin(order + j - E)` and
`margin(p - j - E)` to both routes.

`wcheck-short2-routes.R` calls `check_weights_unbounded()` on each
route (seed 20261111, n 80):

|---|---|---|---|

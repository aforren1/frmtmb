# Review: lane quadesc, round of 2026-11-16

Adversarial review of `frmtmb-wt-quadesc` (branch `wt-r84-quadesc`,
nothing committed), lane library `wt-quadesc-lib`, base `rellib-r22`
(0.83.0). The lane's change is compared with `git show b255350e:R/fit.R`
and a plain file compare (`diff --strip-trailing-cr`; no `git diff`).
Scripts and logs: `dev/reviews/quadesc-review-*`, run through
`dev/reviews/quadesc-review-run.sh` (reference BLAS, Rtools at the end of
PATH, `NOT_CRAN=true`). At most two R processes at once. I did not build
OpenBLAS; for OpenBLAS I read the lane's logs. `dev/ciharden-out/` does
not exist.

## Verdict: FIX

The change is correct where it acts. It removes the CI failure's
rounding branch on every rounding measured, it is never below 0.83.0 in
exact marginal log-likelihood, and it is bitwise neutral where no restart
raises. Three items need work before it ships. Two are small code
changes, and one corrects the record:

1. The record is wrong about the cause. The NaN gradient is a defect
   at its source: RTMB's `pnorm(log.p = TRUE)` derivative in the deep
   lower tail (finding 1). The lane's LOW says the opposite. Correct
   the LOW and refile it as MEDIUM. Add an entry to
   `dev/upstream-bugs.md`.
2. The veto is a rounding branch. A restart that ended on its own
   counts as "gained" on any strict decrease of the objective, even
   when it ends back on the point. On SYMSMALL that restart ends
   6.917e-10 above the stall on all 21 roundings. On the other side of
   that sign, the abandoned point is dropped, and the fit stays on
   alpha = 0 with code 0 (finding 3, shown by construction). Count a
   restart as a gain only when it left the point's window.
3. The abandoned result carries no `evals` and no `nonfinite_trials`
   (finding 5). Set both from the tally.

Optional: finding 6 (the test) and finding 7 (NEWS).

## 1. The diagnosis (NaN, raise, drop)

Reproduced on rellib-r22, reference BLAS, ALPHAX (the test's data) at
k = 9 and k = 1 (`quadesc-review-chain.R`, logs
`quadesc-review-chain-base-ref.txt` and `-lib-ref.txt`). The rule's
escape gets 2 starts. Both raise `NA/NaN gradient evaluation`
(`OPTERR rule restart 1` and `2`). `escape_stationary()` returns the
stall unchanged (`in 91.24523616 out 91.24523616`), and the fit ends at
alpha (1.67e-15, -4.77e-15), logLik -91.2452361629, code 0. The only
warning is the standard-error warning ("alpha_x ... toward -Inf"). This
is the same warning the CI printed. So 0.83.0's stall was not silent,
but it was short by 6.14 exact. The diagnosis of the chain holds.

The cause is wrong. The lane says "RTMB's `pnorm(log.p = TRUE)` is not
the source: value and derivative are exact to x = -1000". Its probe
stopped at -1000. A run-off alpha (1e6 to 1.4e7) together with the
rule's frozen far nodes puts the argument near -3e9.

- `quadesc-review-iso.R` (log `quadesc-review-iso-ref.txt`). A plain
  RTMB replica of the model, taped with the same GK spec at the rule's
  own anchor (`obj$env$parameters`), gives the rule's value and
  gradient to every printed digit: fn 84.56003508 at the first
  NaN-gradient point x*, gradient `NaN` in the five beta and betad
  components, theta finite. Fitted one group at a time, the NaN is
  confined to group 11. That group's rule value at x* (6.0384) is
  within 0.022 of its exact value (6.0606). So the rule has NOT run out
  of mass there, and the value is sound. Only the derivative fails.
- `quadesc-review-iso2.R` (log `-iso2-ref.txt`): RTMB 2.0's
  `pnorm(log.p = TRUE)` derivative is non-finite at 174 of 4001 grid
  points in x in [-9.9e13, -3.1e9]. It is already wrong far earlier:
  the relative error against -x is 3.7e-5 at -1e6, 3.2e-3 at -1e7,
  0.34 at -1e8, 6e18 at -1e9, and the value is Inf at -1e10. The
  value is correct throughout. This is cancellation in exp(log phi -
  log Phi). Group 11's log integrand has a non-finite gradient for u
  from 231.6 to 9840, which is where `marginal_gk`'s infinite-range
  nodes lie. At the first such u, alpha * z is -3.0e9 to -3.9e9 for
  each of its 5 observations. The node's weight is exp(-5e18) = 0, and
  0 times Inf is NaN in the reverse sweep.
- `quadesc-review-clamp.R` (log `-clamp-ref.txt`): a soft floor on the
  argument, `-1e6 + logspace_add(0, w + 1e6)`, gives the same group-11
  value to 10 decimals at x*, at the lowest finite point and at the
  anchor, and a finite gradient at x*. The cost is an ulp-level change
  everywhere: the anchor's alpha gradient moves from -4.3e-16 to
  -3.4e-14. RTMB refuses `pmax()` and comparisons on its AD type. So a
  source fix in `fam_skew_normal()` is possible. It changes the
  family's density for all fits at the ulp level, which is outside this
  lane. File it, MEDIUM. The lane's salvage is still the right safety
  net, because with a finite gradient the restart still runs off on the
  plateau.

## 2. The fix's semantics in EXACT log-likelihood

My own exact marginal log-likelihood uses its own skew-normal density
in base R (not RTMBdist) and piecewise `stats::integrate()` split at
every observation's cliff (rel.tol 1e-11). At the Laplace fit it agrees
with the lane's trapezoid to 6 decimals (-86.009814). At a run-off point
the lane's trapezoid reads 0.0048 high (k = 9: lane -85.101071, mine
-85.105859), because the trapezoid steps across the cliffs. This is
immaterial to every conclusion.

| ALPHAX 9 | -91.245236 | -86.009814 | -85.105859 | 0.904 | 0.546 |
| ALPHAX 1 | -91.245236 | -86.009814 | -85.105884 | 0.904 | 0.546 |
| SYMSMALL 3 | -87.216780 | -85.961442 | -85.191784 | 0.770 | 0.266 |

| WEAK 13 | -75.590668 | -75.420328 | -74.580660 | 0.840 | 0.443 |

Logs: `quadesc-review-chain-lib-ref.txt`,
`quadesc-review-exact2-lib-ref.txt`, `-exact2-lib-ref-weak.txt`. The
kept point beats the stall by 2.0 to 6.1 and the Laplace record by 0.77
to 0.90 on every rounding checked. The 0.27 to 0.55 overstatement never
makes it worse than the Laplace record here. It is the same size as
that margin, so on a design whose true gain over the stall is small this
is not guaranteed. I found no such design. Not demonstrated, recorded as
a residual.

Minor: the kept point is chosen by the frozen rule's objective, and the
rule ranks the two abandoned restarts the wrong way. On ALPHAX k = 9,

exact-worse of its two candidates. Valid endings across roundings span
-85.106 to -84.974 exact (the settled endings near alpha (5920, -16445)
are higher). Accepting each is right. Note the 0.13 spread in the

## 3. Which fits reach `escape_track()`, and the veto

`escape_stationary()` runs for every fit that is not under
`importance =`, whose family declares `post$stationary`, and whose
predictor lands within `tol` of the point. That covers `skew_normal()`
(the only core declaration, `R/families.R:4414`), mixtures that carry it
(`R/families.R:6279`), `hmm()` states (frmtmb.latent `R/hmm.R:468`), and
any user family. It covers both routes: Laplace, and quadrature (in
`quad_fit()` on the Laplace objective, and again on the rule in
`fit_assembled()`). The new code sits after
`if (!length(starts)) return(opt)`, so a fit with no stationary dpar is
unchanged by construction.

`quadesc-review-bitwise.R` (log `-bitwise-ref.txt`) runs one process:
the 0.83.0 escape, then the lane's `escape_stationary()` and
`escape_track()` parsed from the worktree into the same namespace, then
0.83.0 again. `identical()` holds for par, objective and logLik, and
base equals base, on all six cases: skew normal driven to the point
(gain 24.69), skew normal with `(1 | g)` (0.95), two skew-normal
mixture components (6 starts, 27.55), `hmm(2, skew_normal())` from
frmtmb.latent (21.2), SYMSMALL with quadrature at k = 0 (2.291, code 1,
no restart raised), and a gaussian GLMM (no escape).

The veto (FIX 2). In the lane's code, `gained` becomes TRUE for any
restart that ends on its own with `o$objective < best$objective`.
`quadesc-review-gainhole.R` prints every restart of the rule's escape.

- SYMSMALL, ref, k = 0..20 (`-gainhole-lib-ref-symsmall.txt`): the
  restart from -2 always ends back at alpha -0.001699, `obj-stall
  6.917e-10`. That is above the stall, so it does not veto. The
  restart from +2 raised on 10 of 21 roundings.
- With `QUADESC_REVIEW_NUDGE=1`, the same returning restart reads 1e-12
  below the stall (`-gainhole-nudge-lib-ref-symsmall.txt`, k = 3 and
  4). The abandoned point (2.29 better) is dropped, and the fit ends
  at alpha -0.001699, logLik -87.2167801718, code 0, on_point TRUE,
  with only the standard-error warning. That is the CI's ending again.

The nudge is constructed. The real margin is the sign of a 7e-10
difference that nothing controls. Fix: let a restart that ended on its
own veto the abandoned point only if it moved the predictor out of the
point's window (the `moved()` and `tol` test of `stationary_escapes()`,
or simply `stationary_escapes(obj, o, frame)` returning nothing for it).
Alternatively, require its gain to exceed `grad_tol`-scale noise.

WEAK, ref, k = 0..20 (`-gainhole-lib-ref-weak.txt`): on k = 9, 11 and
16, one restart ended on its own (code 1, alpha 4e5 to 2e6) and the
other raised. The normal ending is kept, as the second build intends.

## 7. Smaller findings

5. FIX 3: `escape_track()`'s result has no `evals` and no
   `nonfinite_trials`. `escape_stationary()` then skips its trial
   total, so the fit reports none. `se_check_at_fit()` reads
   `fit$opt$evals` and, with more than 10 outer parameters, skips the
   check at the fit. Set `b$evals <- tally$evals` and
   `b$nonfinite_trials <- tally$n` (or carry the run's own), as
   `mixture-starts.R` and `gp-start.R` do.
6. Test: see section 4 (optional k = 0).
7. NEWS: the bullet sits under "## Quadrature". The change acts on every
   route the escape runs on, including Laplace fits and the Laplace
   escape inside `quad_fit()`. Say so, or move it to the convergence
   section.
8. The lane's trapezoid exact reads about 0.005 high at run-off points
   (section 2). Its "below by more than 1e-3: 0" comparisons are between
   bitwise-identical fits and are unaffected.
9. Process: the lane recorded that `git diff --no-index` was denied and
   that it did not substitute. That is correct under the rules; its
   hunks are listed in its findings. I compared with `git show` and a
   plain `diff`. The hunks are exactly the lane's description: the
   `escape_stationary()` restart loop and the new `escape_track()`. No
   other hunk in `R/fit.R`.

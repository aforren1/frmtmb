1e6`, data seeds 1 to 5 of the lane's grid generator, against the same model on

reaches `B$pos` (R/fit.R:3118) and nothing tells the user. The lane's record
cites only "poisson (1|g), 1e5, b with lb = 0: right", and at 1e5 the base build

warn by class."

and `p_u = A q_b + T q_u`, `A = -(X_u' X_u)^-1 X_u' X_b` projects the bounded
columns off the free ones, so the box stays a box; for a bound on the slope this
is centering the intercept. If that is not done in this round, at least warn by
class when bounds force natural coordinates on a block with a condition number of
1e4 or more. Test: `lb = -5` at 1e6 equals the centered fit, and the binding
bound fits or warns.

## MAJOR 2. The new tolerance keeps columns that neither the basis nor the optimizer can use

`dev/review-bigx-collin.R`, seeds 501 to 503, `yy ~ poly(xx, k, raw = TRUE)`,
`xx` uniform on [1000, 1010], k = 5 to 8, gaussian, against glm():

| | base | lane |
|---|---|---|
| columns kept | 4, with a message naming the dropped ones | 6 to 9 (as glm) |
| SEs | finite (6.4e5 to 8.0e5) | every SE but one NaN, on 12 of 12 |
| warnings | none | 12 of 12: "Standard errors are not available", gradient, singular or false convergence |
| basis | n/a | none on seeds 501 and 502; on 503 it engages and stops at false convergence (8), gradient 50.3 |

Cause (`dev/review-bigx-polydbg.R`): the frame's test is `qr(X, tol = 1e-11)` on
the unscaled design, and `design_whitener()` runs `qr()` at the same tolerance
on the column-scaled design. They disagree: degree 5 has rank 6 in the frame and
5 scaled, degree 8 has 7 against 6, with scaled condition numbers of 3e14 to
3.5e16. So the frame keeps a column, `design_whitener()` returns NULL, no basis
engages, and nlminb runs in natural coordinates on a design at 1e16. The claim
that one tolerance now governs every fixed-design rank test is true of the
number, not of the test.

The lane's fit is not worse than glm in log-likelihood (glm itself is unreliable
at 1e16; on seed 503 frmtmb is 0.02 above glm), but the user now gets a fit with
no SEs where base gave a reduced model with SEs and a message. brms keeps all
columns (`standata()` K = 6 and 9, `dev/review-bigx-brms.log`).

Fix: one test for both, on the scaled design (or drop in the frame what the
whitener calls aliased). Add a test on a raw polynomial far from zero. NEWS bullet
2 should say that a kept near-alias can come with SEs near 1e8 or NaN.

Also measured, not a defect: a near alias `x2 = x1 + 1e-9 * noise` and
proportions that sum to 1 to 1e-9 are now kept with SEs 6.6e7 to 1.6e8, within 1
percent of glm (the ratio is the ML against the unbiased sigma), basis on,
log-likelihood within 6.4e-7 of glm, with no warning and no message (base dropped
them with a message). This matches glm and brms; a message naming such a kept
column would replace the one base gave.

## Merge hazards (read-only reading of the other worktrees, no trial merge)

- **nestquad.** `freeze()` (R/quadrature-nested.R:837) builds each round's tape
  with `RTMB::MakeADFun(qo$fn, otpl, map = omap)` (:847) and no basis; the warm
  start (`optimize_obj(lap_obj, ...)`, :835) gets the basis because `lap_obj` is
  the object fit_assembled() attaches it to. Its findings (lines 625 to 631) ask
  consolidation to attach the basis to each tape. That alone does nothing for the
  rounds: each round's box (:875 to 876) is finite in every coordinate, so
  `opt_coords()` returns natural coordinates (R/fit.R:3118). The tapes' `obj$par`
  has the same names and order as the Laplace tape's, so attaching is safe.
  Consolidation must: attach `lap_obj$env$.frmtmb_opt_basis` to each `freeze()`
  tape (so `escape_stationary()` and `mo_search()` on the final tape use it), and
  either give `opt_coords()` a way to take a box already in q coordinates
  (`to_q(par) +- radius / q-units`) or state that nested rounds run natural.

  insertion in `fit_assembled()`: no textual conflict. Its units feed `par_units`
  and so `opt_coords()`, which replaces `units[B$pos]` with `B$unit`: not doubled,
  but overwritten for an nlpar sub-design that gets a block, and `B$unit` is 1 for

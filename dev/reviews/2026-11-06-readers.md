## Verdict: FIX

The shared fix is right where it is tested: new-level means over seven block
shapes match closed forms to 1e-10 or better, the two-direction hurdle to
1e-10, and the frmtmb.sample, item 4 and item 8 claims hold. 152 of 166
regression comparisons on 18 fits are bitwise identical; the other 14 are
the by-design `se__` and nonlinear-body changes. Four things need a fix
before the merge: the infinite-mean rule returns `Inf` on routine models
whose brms mean is stable and finite (M1), its threshold contradicts the
help (M2), item 5's band leaves the support and is up to 6 times narrow
with no warning (M3), and the Cholesky cross term that the shared fix
rests on has no test (M4).

## Findings

### MAJOR

**M1. `fitted()` reads `Inf` on a lognormal with `sigma ~ (1 | g)` at a new
level, where brms's mean is finite and stable to 0.5 percent.**
`dev/review-readers-inf.R` case 2 and 2b, data seed 7102, estimated
`sd(g)` on sigma 0.2745:

| reader | value at `x = 0`, new level |
|---|---|
| 0.79.1 (median of sigma) | 2.0038 |
| lane `fitted()` | `Inf`, `Est.Error` `Inf`, `frmtmb_mean_not_finite` |
| brms-style mean of 4000 draws at the estimates, seeds 1..20 | 2.0805 to 2.1031 |
| same, 4e6 draws | 2.0897 |

The integral does diverge, but only through draws beyond about 10 sds. Any
sampler's mean, and brms's `posterior_epred()`, reads a finite 2.09 here,
and 0.79.1 read 4 percent low. Now the whole `Estimate` column is `Inf`
for a model as common as a group-level term on `sigma`. The help defends
`Inf` with "a mean of brms's draws there grows with their number". That is
true at `sdx = 0.8` (case 1: 20 seeds read 4.5 to 61.2). At sd 0.27 it is
false. Fix: decide by the stability of the truncated integral, not by
divergence at infinity. One way: return the integral over plus or minus
K sds and warn when it moves by more than a set share between two K. Or
return `Inf` only when the outer weight test fails at a modest K (for
example 6 sds, which covers what 4000 draws can reach). The user decides
which (Q1).

**M2. The `Inf` rule is a numeric threshold, not the stated divergence
test, and it contradicts the help and NEWS both ways.** `inf.R` case 1 (seed
7101, lognormal `sigma ~ me(zo, sz)`, one row) and case 3 (seed 7103,
poisson `y ~ me(xo, sx)`):

| case | link sd of the draw | `Estimate` | stated |
|---|---|---|---|
| lognormal sigma | 0.005 / 0.025 / 0.05 / 0.10 / 0.15 | 2.6907 / 2.6929 / 2.7000 / 2.7293 / 2.7825, no warning | `Inf` "at any size of the draw" |
| lognormal sigma | 0.25 / 0.40 | `Inf`, warning | `Inf` |
| poisson, log-linear | 6.4 | 0.9924 of `exp(eta + s^2 / 2)` | finite |
| poisson, log-linear | 6.6 / 7 / 8 | `Inf`, warning | finite: the mean is `exp(eta + s^2 / 2)` |

So `?fitted` ("infinite at any size of the draw ... The estimate and
`Est.Error` are then `Inf`") and NEWS ("has no finite mean over it, and
`fitted()` gives `Inf`") are false below link sd about 0.2. The jump from
2.78 to `Inf` between `sz` 0.3 and 0.5 is the outer node's weight crossing
1e-6. Above sd 6.5 a finite log-linear mean reads `Inf`. The help does
state that threshold, but it calls the test a divergence test. Shifted
lognormal behaves the same (`inf.R` case 6: finite at `sz` 0.05, `Inf` at
0.8). Gamma and negbinomial with a draw on `shape` read exactly `mu`
(relative 3e-15). A lognormal with the draw on `mu` alone matches the
closed form to 6e-5 at sd 5. Those hold. Fix: with M1, one rule and help
text that says what it does.

**M3. Item 5: `conditional_effects()` through a body whose nlpar reads
`me()` gives a band that leaves the support and is up to 6 times narrow,
with no warning, while `fitted()` on the same row is right.**
`dev/review-readers-body.R` part B: `bf(y ~ exp(a), a ~ me(xo, sx), nl =
TRUE)`, gaussian, data seed 8202, one row `xo = 0.5`. Monte Carlo seed 41,
1e6 draws. The draw's link sd 0.24 equals the data's own `sx = 0.3`:

| link sd | `fitted()` Est / Err | MC mean / sd | ce `estimate__` | ce `se__` | ce band | MC 2.5 / 97.5 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | 1.874 / 0.477 | 1.874 / 0.476 | 1.816 | 0.455 | 0.925 to 2.707 | 1.112 to 2.966 |
| 0.50 | 2.058 / 1.097 | 2.059 / 1.097 | 1.816 | 0.908 | 0.036 to 3.597 | 0.681 to 4.843 |
| 1.00 | 2.994 / 3.925 | 2.997 / 3.929 | 1.816 | 1.816 | -1.744 to 5.376 | 0.255 to 12.91 |
| 1.50 | 5.594 / 16.30 | 5.602 / 16.25 | 1.816 | 2.724 | -3.523 to 7.155 | 0.096 to 34.44 |

NEWS says "at first order", but the brief's standard is "refuses or warns
by class instead of returning the wrong number". A band below 0 for a
positive mean, at a draw size of 0.5, is the wrong number. `fitted()`
integrates this row exactly, because the nlpar `a` is a dpar and
`me_row_loads()` reads it. It matches the log-link fit of the same mean to
every printed digit. So the display can read what `fitted()` reads, as
`ce_cat_draw_band()` does for categories. Fix: band and `se__` from
`fitted_me_moments()` on these rows, or a classed warning past a stated
draw size.

### MINOR

**m1. NEWS and the help say "as brms's `posterior_epred()` is". The
implemented mean is brms's `sample_new_levels = "gaussian"`, not its
default `"uncertainty"`.** `dev/review-readers-brms.R`. brms 2.23.0 at
default priors, 4 chains of 1000, seed 3, epred seed 21. Data as the lane's
item 1:

| fit, `x` = -0.5 / 0 / 0.8 | frm Est | brms gaussian mean | brms uncertainty mean | frm / gaussian | frm / uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| poisson | 2.492 / 3.000 / 4.037 | 2.688 / 3.233 / 4.350 | 2.576 / 3.101 / 4.178 | 0.927 / 0.928 / 0.928 | 0.967 / 0.967 / 0.966 |
| bernoulli | 0.4615 / 0.5208 / 0.6141 | 0.4696 / 0.5269 / 0.6165 | 0.4760 / 0.5350 / 0.6266 | 0.983 / 0.989 / 0.996 | 0.970 / 0.973 / 0.980 |

0.79.1 read 1.513 on the first poisson row (0.56 of brms gaussian). The
remaining gap is ML against the posterior: frm sd(g) 0.999, brms posterior
mean 1.047 (bernoulli 1.593 against 1.744). That is not this lane's to
close. frmtmb refuses `"uncertainty"` by name, so the NEWS sentence should
name `"gaussian"`.

**m2. A new level's `Est.Error` leaves out the uncertainty of the sd
itself.** In the same run, `Est.Error` over brms's sd is 0.901 / 0.904 /
0.906 for poisson against `"gaussian"`, and 0.966 / 0.967 / 0.970 for
bernoulli. Most of it is the posterior's larger sd. It is not stated. Help
sentence, or a delta term in `theta`.

**m3. Past four directions the integral keeps the first four by dpar
order, not by influence.** `dirs.R` part B: `mixture(gaussian, gaussian)`
with five `me()` predictors, data seed 6202, MC seed 52, 2e6 draws. The
order is `mu1 sigma1 mu2 sigma2 theta2`. The two `sigma` directions do not
enter the mean, and `theta2`, which does, is dropped. `fitted()` reads
1.3395 against MC 1.0736 ± 0.0011 (+25 percent; 1.33938 is the value with
`theta2`'s draw at 0). `Est.Error` reads 0.610 against MC sd 1.547. The
warning `frmtmb_draw_dims` fires once per `fitted()` call and once per
`conditional_effects()` call, as claimed. Fix: drop directions with zero
gradient first (`me_band_dir()` already forms the gradients), then order by
size. The same zero-gradient directions also cost nodes for nothing: a
gamma with a draw on `shape` integrates 21 x 21 nodes for a mean that does
not read `shape`.

**m7. Item 8 is not fixed under `allow_new_levels = TRUE`. `Est.Error` is

`dev/review-readers-body.R` part A, data seed 8201. Both arms read

## For the user

- Q1 (M1, M2): what should `fitted()` return where the mean over a draw
  diverges only in the far tail? Options: `Inf` with a warning (lane), the
  integral truncated at a stated K with a warning (closer to brms at
  realistic sizes), or a refusal by class. On a lognormal `sigma ~ (1 |
  g)` the lane's choice makes every new-level row `Inf`.
- Q2 (m1): keep frmtmb's `"gaussian"` default and say so in NEWS, or accept
  `"uncertainty"` as a mean over the seen levels' modes (frm 2.467 /
  2.971 / 3.998 against brms's 2.471 / 2.972 / 4.001 over its seen levels'
  draws)?
- The OpenBLAS run (`dev/ciharden-openblas.sh 0.3.26 lapack`) is still
  open for the Gauss-Hermite product rule, `chol_rows()` and the body's
  Jacobian.

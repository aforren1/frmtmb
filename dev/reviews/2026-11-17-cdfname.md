## Verdict: FIX

The rename is complete and correct, the script reproduces the tree, and
every route the lane lists refuses with the classed error. Two items
need a change before merge: a 0.83.0 fit still reaches one post-fit
path that reads the CDF slot without the check and changes its answer
silently (item 1), and the new prefix relation `cdf` / `cdf_log` makes
`$cdf` an error on a family that has no `cdf` element, which
frmtmb.eam's `wiener_gng()` is (item 2). The rest are small.

it; `dev/release/`, `dev/equiv-*.R` and `dev/ciharden*` do not. About
130 historical `dev/` files keep `lcdf`, as the findings state.

## Findings

### BLOCKER

**B1. Joint Laplace on an unread `mi()` response gives wrong estimates, and
NEWS understates it by an order of magnitude.** The open decision, with
numbers in the section "The open decision". Script
`dev/review-refuse-decision.R` (seed 9400 + family, 20 replicates per cell),
`dev/review-refuse-mi-exact.R`, `dev/review-refuse-brms-exg.R` (seed 10600).

- exgaussian, 200 rows, 60 missing, true sigma 0.5, beta 2. brms 2.23.0 (4
  chains, 2000 iterations): sigma 0.363 joint and 0.365 on the observed rows,
  beta 2.147 and 2.153. `frm()` on the observed rows: sigma 0.336, beta 2.115.
  `frm()` joint: **sigma 0.859, beta 1.592**. brms's posterior is the
  complete-case one, and the joint fit is not near it.
- student, 200 rows, 60 missing, true nu 6: median `nu` chart value 19.1 joint
  against 1.89 on the observed rows, so the fit is a gaussian. Largest shift
  of the chart value 33 SE. At true nu 15 with 15 missing: median 11.0 against
  2.89.
- NEWS says the error moves "at most, a location coefficient by 0.10 standard
  errors on 200 rows with 15 missing (`exgaussian`)". On designs I chose, same
  size: 0.74 SE (skew_normal, alpha 8), 0.68 SE (exgaussian, beta/sigma 4).
  NEWS and the lane's table measure location only; the distributional
  parameters move 1.44 SE (exgaussian) and 7.9 SE (student) at the same size,
  and 4.8 SE and 33 SE at 60 missing.
- NEWS calls the per-value gap "a constant". It is that only for exponential,
  weibull, frechet (-0.08106, verified) and gaussian, lognormal (0, verified).
  Elsewhere it is a function of the shape, which is why the maximum moves:
  exgaussian -0.006 to -0.36, student -0.015 to -0.18, skew_normal -0.006 to
  -0.25, beta -0.014 to -0.24, von_mises -0.03 to -0.27 (and -0.86 near the
  window's end, m6). The numbers in NEWS are one shape each.
- Model choice: AIC picks a true student at 200/60 in 21 of 30 joint runs
  against 28 of 30 on the observed rows (`dev/review-refuse-decision2.R`, seed
  9500).

Student has carried this since 0.16.0, as the lane says. This lane extends it
to 11 families and writes that it is small. Fix: the user decides between the
two routes; I recommend the observed rows. If the joint route stays, NEWS and
the compat row must carry these numbers, and `frm()` should warn.

### MAJOR

**M1. Terms under one key with different weights: brms reads the first term's
weights for every term, frmtmb gives each its own, and NEWS puts "their
weights can differ" beside the L1 claim.** `dev/review-refuse-mm-weights.R`,
seed 9810. brms holds `W_1_1`, `W_1_2` per group, not per term. At hand-chosen
parameter values, brms's log_prob equals the hand density with the first
term's weights on both predictors to 2.8e-14 and misses the hand density with
each term's own by -0.122 (terms swapped: -8.5e-14 and -0.223). frmtmb's
`fitted(dpar = "sigma")` equals its own weights to 4.4e-16 and misses the
first term's by 0.77. Same data, `logLik` -202.63 with own weights, -205.72
with brms's model. Through the instrument a key with `scale = FALSE` on one
term reads a defect: constant -0.236, points -0.188, -0.329, gradient 0.27
(`dev/review-refuse-mm.R`, seed 9800). The lane's L1 never saw this: the
translator stops on a weighted `mm()` ("VarCorr has no block"), and p070 has
no weights. The test "a key follows the members: the weights can differ"
checks the block count only. brms silently dropping a term's weights is a brms
defect, so either refuse different weights under one key, or keep frmtmb's
model as a stated divergence. Say which in NEWS and the compat row, and file
it upstream. With one set of weights the identity holds by hand (held list).

**M2. `gr(id =, cor = FALSE)` drops the key, also when another term under that
key asks for the correlation.** `dev/review-refuse-misc2.R`, seed 10203. `bf(y
~ x + (1 | gr(g, id = "p")), sigma ~ (1 | gr(g, id = "p", cor = FALSE)))` fits
as two independent blocks with no message, `logLik` -240.83. brms reads the
first term's flag and has `L_1` and `cor_1`; the correlated fit is -236.46. In
the opposite order brms has no correlation, as frmtmb. On rellib-r16 this
spelling was refused, so the lane turned a refusal into a silent difference of
4.4 nats. NEWS says "the key does nothing, as in brms", which is true only
when every term under the key says `cor = FALSE`. Fix: refuse mixed flags, or
read the first term's as brms does. Pin it.

**M3. `frm_sample()` does not sample `mi()` under a mixture of positive or
unit families, though NEWS and the refusal say it does.**
`dev/review-refuse-misc3.R`, seed 10300. `mixture(lognormal(), lognormal())`,
`mixture(Gamma, Gamma)`, `mixture(Beta(), Beta())`: `frm()` and `frm_sample()`
both stop on "response must be strictly positive" (or "lie strictly in (0,
1)"). `mi_placeholder()` puts 0 in the column for `no_mode`, and `valid_y`
reads it. The user never reaches the stated reason. brms builds all three
(`vector[Nmi] Ymi`, no bound, `dev/review-refuse-misc4.R`). `mixture(gaussian,
gaussian)` and `asym_laplace` do sample. Fix: the placeholder is `mean(obs)`
on every chart (the comment's reason, "0 is not in the support", holds for any
family with a `valid_y`, and it also removes the misleading stop for a custom
positive family with no chart). Then a positive mixture needs a chart on the
sampling route, or a refusal that says so.

**M4. Beta `mi()` fails with a mean near 1, and only on that side.**
`dev/review-refuse-misc.R`, seed 10200. Grid of `eta0` in +-2..5 by `phi` in
0.3, 2, 8, 200 rows, 20 missing: 4 of 12 stop with "NA/NaN gradient" on the
positive side, 0 of 12 on the negative side; the complete-case fit is fine in
all 24. On the tape the joint is finite to u = 36 and Inf from u = 37, and
finite down to u = -700: `1 / (1 + exp(-u))` rounds to exactly 1, and
`log1p(-y)` in the density is `-Inf`. The comment on `mi_chart_logjac()`
handles the Jacobian's own rounding and misses the density's. A slow upper
tail (`b = (1 - mu) phi` small) sends a Newton step there. Fix: hold the value
away from 1 in `mi_chart_inv()` on the tape, or bound the inner step. A
failure is loud, so this is not a wrong answer, but it is a correct model that
does not fit.

**M5. `miss_<k>` is a wrong number in the draws a user reads; the sampler
itself is right.** `dev/review-refuse-sample.R`, seed 9700. Mapped through the
chart, `frm_sample()`'s draws equal brms's `Ymi`: KS median 0.021, 0.017,
0.019 on Gamma, beta, von_mises (15 values each), largest 0.035; posterior of
b and of the dpar equal brms's (Gamma shape 2.230 against 2.229; beta phi
3.571 against 3.559; von_mises kappa 3.274 against 3.296). So the Jacobian is
correct. But the column a user reads: `miss_1` under Gamma ranges -3.05 to
3.01 where brms's `Ymi[1]` ranges 0.04 to 15.3; under beta -2.9 to 12.1
against 0.02 to 1.00; under von_mises -5.8 to 8.2 as an "angle" against -1.74
to 3.11. Nothing in the draws or `?frm_sample` says so; only a NEWS line does.
Fix: report the value in the draws and keep the chart inside the tape. That
needs one exported seam from core, because frmtmb.sample reads no internal.

## The open decision

The lane asks: keep the joint Laplace on a missing value no predictor reads,
or fit such a response on its observed rows and compute the imputations
afterward. **Recommendation: the observed rows, on `frm()` only, row by row.**
The numbers:

Largest |shift| from the complete-case fit in its SE, 20 replicates, three
shapes per family (`dev/review-refuse-decision.R`):

| family | gap per value, range | location 200/15 | location 200/60 | dpar 200/15 | dpar 200/60 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| exgaussian | -0.006 to -0.36 | 0.68 | 0.94 | 1.44 | 4.79 |
| skew_normal | -0.006 to -0.25 | 0.74 | 2.00 | 0.73 | 2.54 |
| student | -0.015 to -0.18 | 0.25 | 0.78 | 7.90 | 33.3 |
| gen_extreme_value | +0.018 to -0.24 | 0.24 | 0.91 | 0.71 | 2.36 |
| von_mises | -0.03 to -0.27 | 0.17 | 0.51 | 0.27 | 1.16 |
| beta | -0.014 to -0.24 | 0.10 | 0.19 | 0.25 | 1.03 |
| inverse.gaussian | +0.017 to +0.054 | 0.18 | 0.63 | 0.11 | 0.45 |
| Gamma | -0.010 to -0.11 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.15 | 0.68 |

An SE here is the complete-case fit's, so a value of 1 is a shift as large as
the whole sampling error. exponential, weibull, frechet, gaussian and
lognormal show 0 to 5 digits (`dev/review-refuse-mi-exact.R`, seed 9200 +
family).

**Why the observed rows are the right answer.** A missing value no density
reads integrates to 1, so the complete-case likelihood is exact. brms's
posterior for every other parameter is that one: exgaussian above; Gamma shape
2.229 joint against 2.223 observed; beta phi 3.559 against 3.555; von_mises
kappa 3.296 against 3.292; student nu 2.693 against 2.687
(`dev/review-refuse-sample.R`). So this is the route that matches brms, and
the joint route does not.

**What breaks, and what it costs.**

| reader | under the observed rows |
|---|---|
| Predictions at missing rows | Nothing. The rows stay in the frame and leave only the objective, as `subset()` does; the joint and observed fits' `fitted()` differ by a median 0.084, max 0.189 on exgaussian 200/60, and the observed fit's is the right one. |
| An `mi()` read by another response | Stays latent, row by row: only rows no density reads leave. With 20 of 60 read, dropping the 40 unread moves log sigma from -0.003 to -0.404 and log beta from 0.484 to 0.689 (`dev/review-refuse-misc4.R`). Laplace stays on the read rows: -0.114 Gamma, -0.098 exponential, -0.078 weibull, -0.076 von_mises, -0.079 exgaussian, -0.037 lognormal, +0.021 inverse.gaussian against `integrate()` (`dev/review-refuse-mi-exact.R`, 80 rows, 8 missing). That is the ordinary cost of Laplace and should be stated, not hidden. |
| `mi(sd)` | Stays latent: its density reads every row. |
| `fit$estimates$miss`, an imputation | Goes for the dropped rows. What it held is a chart artifact anyway: the mode in u, 1.41 where the fitted mean is 2.16 on exgaussian, the mean on Gamma. brms's `Ymi` is a predictive draw, and `simulate()` and `predict()` already give that. |
| L1 against brms | Survives, and gets better. brms's mi() log_prob minus the density at `Ymi` equals `frm()`'s complete-case `logLik` at any `Ymi`: max difference 8.5e-14 over three values, Gamma and gaussian (`dev/review-refuse-decision2.R`). The translator subtracts that density, or compares with brms's program on the observed rows, which the survey already reads. |
| L4 | Starts to pass: on exgaussian 200/60 it would fail today. |
| `frm_sample()` | Unchanged: `dry_run = "objective"` keeps the latents, and the sampler is right (M5). |
| Refusals | `asym_laplace` and `mixture` with unread missing rows stop being refused by `frm()`, so m5's and M3's frm() side shrink to read rows. |
| `logLik`, `AIC` | Exact. Today a family's AIC carries a shape-dependent penalty; true exgaussian minus gaussian is -39 joint against -73 observed at 200/60. |

If the user keeps the joint route, B1 is still a defect in NEWS, the compat
row and the missing warning, and the numbers above go into them.

## Blockers

B1. **Defect 1 (`sx_1`) is described wrongly in the headline, and its
cause and reach are missing.** The finding itself is correct and
important; its characterization is not.

- The headline (`vigport-findings.md` lines 66 to 69) says the two
  coefficients past `|z| = 1` sit "on a column with the opposite sign
  and 6.8 times the scale of brms's". On the rows of that model
  (`brms_distreg` `fit_smooth1`, data from `port_seed("brms_distreg.11.1")`)
  the factor is **+5.372** for `s(x1)` and **+5.431** for `s(x2)`: same
  sign. `dev/vigport-rev-sx.R` section 6, log
  `vigport-rev-out/sx.txt`.
- The factor depends on the data in size and sign. On
  `mgcv::gamSim(eg = 6, n = 200, scale = 2)`, seeds 1 to 6, it runs
  from 5.03 to 7.68 in magnitude and is negative in 3 of 12 columns
  (seed 1: -6.800 and -6.856; seed 3: -7.683 for `s(x2)`). The lane's
  -6.80 is seed 1 only, and the record does not say it varies.
- Carried onto brms's column, frmtmb's estimates are 9.835 and 29.69
  against brms's 9.73 (sd 3.44) and 30.1 (sd 12.4): `z` of +0.03 and
  -0.04. So the two "outliers" of the table are the defect alone, and
  the ML fit agrees with brms on that model.
- A third coefficient has the same defect and the record misses it:
  `sigma_sx0_1` (frmtmb -0.0737, brms -2.245, sd 2.416, `z` 0.90, the
  reason the model's median `|z|` is 0.899). Its factor is +6.084; on
  brms's column frmtmb reads -0.448 (`dev/vigport-rev-sx0.R`).
- The cause, which the record should state so the fix is aimed: brms's
  `data_sm()` builds the basis with
  `smoothCon(absorb.cons = TRUE, diagonal.penalty = TRUE)`, and frmtmb
  (`R/frame.R:3110`) with `smoothCon(absorb.cons = TRUE, modCon = 3)`
  and the default `diagonal.penalty = FALSE`. Both then call
  `smooth2random(type = 2)`. Rebuilt with mgcv alone, each route
  reproduces its package's column exactly (`all.equal` TRUE, both
  terms). The random part agrees: `max|Z Z' - Zb Zb'| / max|Zb Zb'|` is
  7.5e-16 and 8.2e-16, so `sds_sx_1` is on brms's scale and only the
  null-space column differs.
- It is a coefficient-scale defect, not a naming one. The name, the
  prior table and the draw name (`bs_sx1_1`) all say brms's
  coefficient, and the number is a different parameter. A prior with
  `coef = "sx1_1"` targets it: on seed 1, brms's `normal(0, 0.1)` on
  its column is `normal(0, 0.015)` on frmtmb's, and the sign of any
  one-sided hypothesis on `sx1_1` flips wherever the factor is
  negative.

Fix the headline sentence and defect 1 with the numbers above, name
`sigma_sx0_1`, and record the cause.

B2. **"6 of 62 variance components past `|z| = 1`, all ML below brms
at a boundary" is false for 3 of the 6, and the convergence filter
covers brms only.**

- Of the 6 (all on brms fits with Rhat <= 1.05), three are residual

### Remaining minor findings

r1. **The n - p correction is applied as an identity, but here it is
an approximation.** The factor `sqrt((n - p) / n)` = `sqrt(12/16)` =
0.8660 is the right arithmetic for 16 observed `bmi` rows and 4
coefficients. Dividing 2.5310 by it gives 2.9225, and z goes from
-1.049 to -0.538 and -0.518, which I checked. But "ML divides by 16
where an unbiased estimate divides by 12" is exact only for least
squares on fully observed regressors. In this `mi()` model, 3 of the
16 rows with observed `bmi` have `chl` missing. `chl` enters the `bmi`
formula, and those rows are integrated over its latent value. So the
correction is approximate, and the record should say so. "About half
the gap" stays a fair reading.

r2. **Inconsistent denominator for "other" rows.** The `pr1 spell
change` block gives "other spell OK: 97 -> 97 of 106", because
`vigport-pr1-spellcmp.R` counts the SETUP-SKIP row. The headline
convention, and my count, is 97 of 105. The count is a cosmetic
inconsistency, not a wrong count.

## MAJOR 1: `quad_settle()` warns "not the maximum" on fits that are at the maximum

Mechanism. The loop runs while the gradient of the rule taped at the
estimate is at or above `grad_tol`. A round optimizes the frozen rule from
the estimate. If nlminb stops at once (relative convergence, step 0), the
re-tape at the same point gives the same value. The round is accepted
with a gain of 0, and the loop repeats until all 10 rounds are used. It
then warns: "...the log-likelihood rose by 0 over them (0 in the last
step). The reported estimate is not the maximum of the integral. A
log-likelihood that keeps rising as a standard deviation falls has no
maximum...". The loop has no exit for "no move", although the nested
route has one (`if (ok && step < 1e-8) break`). This is a fixed-point
repeat, so the claim "the loop cannot cycle" is false in practice. A
rejected round also ends in the same warning, because a break leaves
`settled` FALSE.

Reproducer with the documented control `frmtmb_control(grad_tol = 1e-4)`
(the example in `?frmtmb_control`). `quadseam-review-gradtol.R lane 1:3`
fits a Bernoulli model with 500 groups of 4 and sd 1.2, without
instrumentation. Log: `gradtol-lane.txt`.

    FIT seed 1 lane logLik -1247.0969997014 settle 10/0/2.56e-04 secs 37.2 nwarn 1
      WARN frmtmb_quad_unsettled : ... rose by 0 over them (0 in the last step). The reported estimate is not the maximum ...
    FIT seed 2 lane logLik -1264.0873225110 settle 0/0/6.36e-05  secs  9.5 nwarn 0
    FIT seed 3 lane logLik -1233.2483306926 settle 10/0/4.14e-04 secs 28.3 nwarn 1

Seed 1's estimate is the exact maximum: its err is -5.4e-8, and base's
estimate at the same tolerance is bitwise the same point (exact
1247.0969997552 on both, `settle-*.txt`). On base the fit takes 23.7 s
and gives no warning.

How often it happens, measured with `quadseam-review-settle.R`, which
logs each round of a copy of `quad_settle()` patched into the namespace
of the review process only. Logs: `settle-lane.txt`, `settle2-lane.txt`,
`settle-all-lane.txt`. Designs: bern5, bern500, bern20big, bern2sd3,
bin500, bin50, binz, pois8, pois200 and poissd2 (see `gen()` in
`quadseam-review-acc.R`), seeds 1 to 5. Every one of these fits has a
maximum. The lane's err is at most 5.4e-6 on all of them.

    grad_tol 1e-4: 49 fits, rounds > 0 on 17, frmtmb_quad_unsettled on 13
    grad_tol 1e-5: 48 fits, rounds > 0 on 28, frmtmb_quad_unsettled on 25

The `simpleWarning` entries in the instrumented logs come from the reviewer's `exact_at()` (integrate/optimize) inside the patched copy. The uninstrumented `gradtol-lane.txt` has only the class warning. Most of the warned rounds show `step 0.000e+00 dq 0.000e+00` 10 times
(for example, poissd2 seed 4 at 1e-6: 10 rounds, step 0, 5.2 s against
2.1 s). pois200 seeds 1 to 4 also add `frmtmb_conv_gradient`.

It also happens at the default `grad_tol = 1e-3`
(`quadseam-review-acc.R lane bern500,pois200,bern2sd3,poissd2 6:35`, log
`acc3-lane.txt`; base `acc3-base.txt`; glmer `acc3-glmer.txt`). Of 120
fits, 9 needed a round and 2 warned:

    bern2sd3 35  rounds 1, re-anchored grad 2.85e-3, frmtmb_quad_unsettled
                 exact at the lane estimate -128.680088997531,
                 at glmer(nAGQ = 25)'s     -128.680088982944 (1.5e-8 apart)
                 base: same estimate (exact difference 0), no warning
    poissd2 29   rounds 1, grad 1.06e-3, frmtmb_quad_unsettled + frmtmb_conv_gradient
                 exact at lane -212.516048533433, at glmer -212.516049101256
                 (the lane estimate is the better one); base: no warning

Both are fits at the maximum that now get a warning saying they are not.
The lane rules say that a warning on a correct model is worse than none.
The lane's "130 fits with a maximum needed no second round" is true of its
three designs and not in general.

Fix (suggested): end the loop when a round does not move (step at or
below about 1e-8 in units, or a gain within the acceptance tolerance),
and treat that as settled. In that case the re-anchored gradient is the
rule's anchor noise, not a missing maximum. Reserve the warning for a
loop that keeps gaining (the runaway shows gains of 80 or more per round)
or that is rejected after a move. Then rerun the three numbers above.

## MINOR 7 (merge, boundfam): a coefficient run-off under one-block quadrature stays silent

Lane boundfam's `se_edge_limit()` keeps its own
`!isTRUE(fit$quadrature)` gate. Poisson with a factor level that has only
zero counts, plus `(1 | g)` (`quadseam-review-limit.R`, logs
`limit-boundfam.txt`, `limit-lane.txt`):

    boundfam lib  Laplace: fc -22.53 se NaN, warning "... toward -Inf ..."   quadrature: fc -22.53 se 7471, no warning
    lane lib      Laplace: fc -22.53 se NaN, "flat" warning                  quadrature: fc -22.53 se 7471, no warning

So under quadrature the user gets an SE of 7471 and no word, on both
builds. Can the check run on the one-block route through
`se_probe_fn()`? Partly. The direction test (`se_limit_dir()`) needs only
objective values, and `se_probe_fn()` supplies them. But the
"undetermined" test, `se_limit_curv()`, takes `obj$he(x)` when the tape
has no random effects (the quadrature tape has none) at a point moved by
`se_edge_step`. On the frozen tape that is a read away from the anchor,
which the lane itself measured as garbage (+2.5 to +40.7,
`dev/quadseam-probe1.R`). It would need a Hessian of a rule taped at `x`
(one tape plus `he()` per point). Recommendation for the consolidation:
open the gate for the one-block route with `fn <- se_probe_fn(fit)` and a
re-taped `se_limit_curv()`, or record the silent SE 7471 as a backlog
item. Without it, 3477's promise ("same message and NaN SE as under
Laplace") holds for sds only. Neither lane created this gap; quadseam's
seam makes it fixable.

Semantics:

- nlstop (`prior_conflict_escape()`) sits in the Laplace branch, above
  `} else if (!is.null(qnest))`. It never runs on a quadrature tape, so
  no settle is needed.
- ordstart (`mixture_starts()`) sits inside
  `if (is.null(imp) && is.null(integrate))`, so it does not run under
  quadrature. Note: `mixture_starts_count()` reads "random effects" from
  `obj$env$random`, which is empty on a quadrature tape. If ordstart's
  hunk ever leaves that gate, it would run 10 jittered starts on the
  frozen rule and call `newton_polish(..., ill = ill)` with `ill`
  unbound. That is a trap for later, not a defect now.
- boundfam: the hunks do not overlap (as the lane says). Its new
  `cov_from_hessian()` block receives `edge` that can now be TRUE under
  quadrature (from `se_edge_sd()`) and passes it as `skip`. That is
  consistent. `se_boundary_stop()` now accepts "limit" too. Under
  quadrature "limit" never appears (gated), so there is no interaction.
- wcheck: see MAJOR 2.
- Backlog: every lane adds a "Filed in the round of 2026-11-11" header at
  the same place. That conflicts at consolidation (textually trivial).

### 2. The headroom fallback for a non-finite AD Hessian

Scope, from the code: `grad_headroom()` reads `obj_he_cached()` only when
`headroom_pulled_hessian()` gives NULL. `conv_headroom()` reads it only
when the tape has no random effects, because a Laplace tape with random
effects takes the differences path already. So the fallback can reach
only fits without random effects (quadrature tapes and fixed-effect-only
fits), and only when `he()` is not finite and the gradient has tripped
`grad_tol`.

Measurement: I wrapped `obj_he_cached()` in the test process only, to log
every non-finite answer and the fit-end callers on the stack
(`quadseam-short-hetrace.R`, `-hetrace-ext.R`).

- **The tracer works.** On the lane's own case (poissd2 seed 29,
  quadrature; `validate-*.txt`), it logs NaN in all 9 entries from
  `se_check`, `fit_outer_hessian` and `grad_headroom`, on both builds.
  The forced headroom is NA on base and 5.5e-9 on the lane.
- **Core, 50 test files** that exercise convergence and SE verdicts
  (`files52.txt`): 7808 pass, 0 fail, 0 err. There were **0**
  non-finite `he()` answers.
- **Extensions:** 82 files of frmtmb.survival, eam, latent, coupling and
  ode, run on the lane core: 81 completed with 4184 pass, 0 fail, 0 err.
  There were **0** non-finite answers. The 82nd file, eam
  test-sample-ndt-default.R, a Stan sampling test, hung for 8 minutes
  and I stopped it.
- **Forced headroom** on about 150 fixed-effect fits (lin, int and nl
  for every single family, `dev/review-ci-nlhead.R` on
  test-nl-family.R; `nlhead-*.txt`): base and lane output is
  **identical** line for line, including its 10 NA headrooms (backlog

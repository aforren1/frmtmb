## 1. The math, and what "exact" means

For a whitened REML fit, the tape's coordinates are
`natural = M gamma`, where `M` is `T` on the `beta` rows and identity
elsewhere. With `r` the inner positions (`gamma`, `b`) and `f` the outer
ones (`betad`, `theta`), the joint covariance that TMB's
`jointPrecision` encodes is:

    V_gamma = [W + J Vf J',  J Vf;  Vf J',  Vf],
    W = Qg_rr^-1,  J = -W Qg_rf

`Vf` is `Schur(Qg)^-1 = cov.fixed`, or the repaired covariance where a
parameter is lost. For a load `a`, `var = (M' a)' V_gamma (M' a)`.
`jcs_joint_cov()` with `white = TRUE` holds exactly this:

- the supernodal Cholesky of `Qg_rr`;
- `J` by solves;
- `Vf` (or its factor `Rf`);
- `M`.

`jcs_white_load()` applies `M'`. `jcs_rowvar_inner()` reads `W` from the
selected inverse, from direct solves for pairs outside the pattern, or
from dense solves for 200 or fewer positions. All three are exact. The
code computes the exact form. It is not an approximation.

### Independent reference

the gaussian random-intercept model analytically. The reference does
not use `obj$env$spHess(random = FALSE)`: on this tape that call returns
zeros outside the inner block (`sparsese-review-probe2.R`). The
reference:

- uses its own whitener (a QR of the scaled raw design, not the
  package's);
- forms `H_rr = C'C/s^2 + diag(0, I/sd^2)`;

### F1 (FIX). `frm_lp_basis()` and `frm_curve()` on the sparse route

`lp_basis_out()` keeps `V_sparse` for a whitened fit. `lb_dense()`
(the exported `frm_lp_basis()`) then forms the natural block `V`, but
the result has no `V_root` and no `V_rest`. Three consequences:

- `frm_curve()` refuses on the base design: "disagrees with
  frm_linpred(se.fit = TRUE) by 0.000967 relative" (`designs-base.txt`),
  and by 1.44e-5 on 5 grid rows (`follow-base.txt`). It refuses on the
  gaussian profile design by 0.000799. On the dense route it returns
  with `cov_rel_error` 0 to 2.4e-16.
- At degree 5, `cov_rel_error` rises from about 2e-16 to 3.0e-8
  (crossed), 3.2e-8 (slope), 7.4e-8 (poisson REML), 1.1e-7 (dpar) and
  2.4e-7 (poisson profile). This is the start of the same loss.
- `frm_lp_basis()` documents `rowSums((A %*% V_root)^2) + ...` for an
  ill-conditioned design. With no `V_root`, `A V A'` is 9.7e-4 off
  `se.fit` (base) and 1.26e-2 off (gaussian profile).

Above 4000 parameters at the default limit, `rellib-r22` gave a band
4.5 percent low with no warning. The lane changes that to a refusal,
which is better but is not a fix. Below the limit the problem appears
only if the option is lowered, which is the lane's MEDIUM
recommendation.

Fix: give `frm_lp_basis()` a `V_root` (and a zero `V_rest`) from the
whitened coordinates, for example
`M[pos, ci] %*% t(chol(V_gamma[ci, ci]))`, with `ci` the `gamma`
columns the rows load. Add a test: `frm_curve()` and the `V_root`
recipe on the small base design under limit 20.

### F2 (FIX, or scope and file HIGH). `fit_fd_se()` readers on the sparse route

`fd_joint_root()` (R/brms-shapes.R:764) needs `get_joint_cov()$root` at
the `b` rows. On the sparse route that root is NULL, so `fit_fd_se()`
takes its per-parameter central differences. Its own comment says that
route "loses every digit" of `J V J'` on a raw polynomial.

Cumulative REML, degree 5, `(1 | g)`, 38 joint parameters, limit 20
against 1e6 (`cum.txt`):

- `Estimate`: identical on both routes.
- `Est.Error` of new rows: up to 95 percent off.
- In-sample `Est.Error`: quantiles 0.026, 0.069, 0.100, 0.149, **1.51**
  sparse, against 0.026, 0.037, 0.077, 0.104, 0.223 dense. A standard
  error of 1.51 for a probability is impossible.

At 4508 parameters, default limit (`cum4500-*.txt`):

- Lane equals `rellib-r22` to 6 digits: same fallback, so not fixed.
- Lane differs from the dense route (limit 1e6) by up to 1.2e-3.
- Time: 0.11 s against 62 s.

This is not a regression at default settings. But NEWS claims accurate
`fitted()` above the limit, and the recommended lower default would
apply this route to every whitened cumulative or categorical REML fit.

Fix: give `fd_joint_root()` a factor from the sparse object over the
drawn and `b` positions, or keep the dense root rows it needs. If not
fixed here, scope NEWS and file HIGH.

## Findings beside the lane (filed as pre-existing, not blockers)

- **P1 (LOW). A precision floor in the whitened tape.** TMB's whitened
  Hessian `Qg_bb` differs from the analytic `(X T)'(X T)/s^2` by 7.2e-8
  relative to its largest entry. Elementwise, a near-zero entry is 18
  times its true value. The tape forms `T'(X'X)T` through the natural
  design, whose kappa is 2e8 (`floor-3500-s1-g3.txt`). Conditional SEs
  of `x` in 1 to 2 are therefore 3.64e-7 off on both routes at
  group sd 3, and 1e-8 at group sd 0.
- **P2 (LOW). Outer covariance accuracy.** `vcov(full = TRUE)` differs
  from the Richardson-on-AD-gradient inverse Hessian by 3.8e-6 (100
  groups) to 1.9e-5 (3500 groups). This drives 3e-7 to 3e-6 of the
  `b`-loading SEs on both routes.
- **P3 (LOW). emmeans on whitened REML or profile fits.** emmeans takes
  the design route (`held` ignores `joint_root`, interop.R:2324) and
  forms `X V X'`. `emmeans(~ f)` SE is -1.6e-4 (dense) and 1.5e-5
  (sparse) off `frm_linpred()` on the base design.

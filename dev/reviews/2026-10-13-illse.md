**R2-1. The flat-row rule can call a stop far short of the maximum "at
the maximum".** `headroom_flat_rows()` leaves out a row whose largest
entry is at most `se_empty_row` (1e-6) when |g u| is at most
sqrt(2 se_empty_row grad_tol), about 4.5e-5. That bound assumes the
row curves by at least `se_empty_row`. An empty row curves by less, so
the gain along it has no bound.
- Synthetic (`dev/illse-rev2-flat.R`): a row with largest entry 1e-12
  and gradient 4e-5 is left out. A Newton step at that curvature gains
  800. Gradients of 1e-4 and above, and a non-finite row with gradient
  1e-2, are kept, so a large gradient is never hidden.
- Real case (`flat-*.txt`, `flat2.txt`): `y ~ x + (1 | g)` with a true
  group sd of 0.5, started at log sd -15, `iter.max = 3`. On seeds 1
  and 4 the stop is code 1 and 91.6 and 139.0 below the fit from the
  default start. The sd's row is empty and its gradient is 5.6e-10.
  - The lane warns: "A Newton step from the stop gains 0.000164 ...,
    so the estimates are at the maximum and their standard errors are
    judged as for a converged fit."
  - 0.70.0 warned: "Large maximum absolute gradient ... The fit may not
    have converged."
  - The lane's SE check then names theta_1 "stopped short of the
    maximum", so the fit is not silent. But the two warnings contradict
    each other, and the convergence signal of 0.70.0 is gone.
  - The coefficient SEs are 0.70.0's on both arms (0.036 for the
    intercept, where the sd of 0.47 implies about 0.105). So no NaN
    became finite.
  - On seeds 2 and 3 the lane still says "short".
- Fix: a left-out row of a log sd counts as at the maximum only where
  the upward probe of the SE check (`se_sd_at_zero()`) holds.
  Otherwise the clause says the stop is short. Add a test on seed 1
  above. NEWS ("when a step along it can gain at most `grad_tol`")
  claims more than the rule measures.

## Verdict: MERGEABLE AFTER FIXES

The main mechanism is right. I checked it independently, entry by entry,
and every route that reads the factor is exact to six digits or better.
The guard-absent side is clean, and so are item b and the item c
judgment. But the change adds five regressions outside the routes it
converted. Each is a finite wrong number or a lost verdict where 0.70.0
gave NaN with a warning, or gave correct standard errors. Items 1 to 5
must be fixed. Item 6 is documentation.

1. **`vcov_cluster()` returns finite wrong matrices where 0.70.0 gave NaN.**
   It forms `B S'S B` from the whole bread. Degree-8 raw polynomial with
   `(1 | g)`, seeds 101 to 103 (`dev/illse-rev-lmm.R`,
   `dev/illse-rev-cluster.R`; `lmm8-lane.txt`, `cluster-lane.txt`). The
   comparison is against the exact sandwich with the same scores `S`:
   - Entry errors on the correlation scale are 2.33, 6.31 and 12.3.
     Under OpenBLAS 0.3.32 they are 2.92 to 4.68.
   - `hypothesis(vcov = vcov_cluster(fit, full = TRUE))` gives the SE of
     the fitted value at x = 1.5 as 0, 1228 and 1511 times the exact one
     (OpenBLAS: 1145 to 1434).
   - On 0.70.0 all of these are NaN (`lmm8-base.txt`).
   - The intrinsic floor: the exact bread rounded to double still gives
     entry errors of 3.5 to 10.1. Formed through the factor,
     `root (K'K) root'` with `K = S root`, the errors are 6.4e-6 to
     1.5e-5, and the SE taken through that factor is exact to six digits.
   - The call itself never warns. On a fresh fit the warning comes from
     the inner `vcov()`, names `vcov()`, and says the entries are
     accurate, which is false for this matrix. After any earlier
     `vcov()` call it is silent.
   - At degree 7 the lane's sandwich is 0.5 to 2.6 percent off on the
     diagonal, and its hypothesis SEs are 19.9, 0 and 23.5 times the
     exact ones. 0.70.0 was wrong in the same way (34.8 and 0), so that
     part is not new.

   Fix: form the sandwich through the factor, hold its factor
   (`root K'`), and route `hypothesis()` and `summary()` with
   `vcov = vcov_cluster(...)` through it. Where that is not done, return
   NaN with the products warning. Add a test against an exact sandwich.

2. **`insight::get_varcov()` hands out the whole matrix with no warning.**
   `interop_vcov()` through `insight_param_scale()` has neither the
   `get_vcov()` gate nor `warn_cov_products()`. Fresh degree-8 fit
   (`dev/illse-rev-insight.R`): the matrix is finite, there are 0
   warnings, and `a' V a` from it gives 0.779 of the SE from lm()'s QR at
   x = 1.5. On 0.70.0 the coefficients were lost and this matrix was NaN.
   This is B6's failure class. ggeffects, modelbased and parameters read
   this method. Fix: give it the same NaN-and-warning as `get_vcov()`
   (or at least the warning), and add a test.

3. **marginaleffects is silently wrong on factor fits that are not marked
   lossy, and NEWS says otherwise.** `get_vcov()` returns NaN only when
   the natural kappa is above `se_product_kappa` (1e12). Between 1e8 and
   1e12 it returns `V`. `V` is accurate there: a caller's own `a' V a`
   is within 2.7e-6 (`mfx-lane.txt`). But marginaleffects differentiates
   numerically, and the true cancellation `|a|'|V||a| / a'Va` is 3e10 to

### F1 (MEDIUM). 5353: a transformed factor in a bar keeps the session's contrasts

`R/frame.R:1481-1495`, `re_term_contrasts()` evaluates the bar's left side
on `mf`, the model frame. For `(1 + factor(cyl) | g)` that frame has a
column `factor(cyl)` and no `cyl`, so `model.frame()` fails ("object 'cyl'
not found", `silent2-review-dbg2.R`), the function returns `NULL`, and the
component stores no contrasts. `pred_design()` then codes the term under
the session's option, as on base.

Reproducer `dev/reviews/silent2-review-probe.R` (b) and
`silent2-review-contr.R`, lane arm:

```text
RE-only (1 + factor(cyl) | g) sum -> helmert, max |diff|: 0.4826564 ; in-sample vs fit-time newdata: 0
(1+factor(cyl)|g) sum -> helmert (same names)   fit-opt vs in-sample 0 ; reset vs fit-opt 0.538
(1+factor(cyl)|g) sum -> treatment              ERROR ... Random-effect design for `1 + factor(cyl) | g` does not match
```

Expected: 0 in both (as `(1 + f | g)` gives on the lane, and as the
fixed-only `y ~ factor(cyl)` gives on base and lane: 0). Actual: a silent
wrong prediction when the two codings name their columns alike (sum vs
helmert), a refusal otherwise. Same on base (0.538), so not a regression,
but the CLOSED line under 5353 claims the general case.

Fix: read the factor columns of `mf` by the deparsed variables of the
left side (`attr(terms(~lhs), "variables")`; `mf[["factor(cyl)"]]` is
already the factor), or evaluate on the data before the model frame. Add
`(1 + factor(cyl) | g)` under sum then helmert to the test in
`test-predict-newdata.R`.

The rest of 5353 holds (lane arm, all 0 after the reset): `(1 + f | g)`
sum to treatment and to helmert; ordered factor with poly to treatment;
character column; `(1 + f:x | g)`; `(1 + f || g)`; `gr(g, by = )`;
`mm(g, g2)` (base 1.81 silent under helmert, lane 0); a factor whose

## 5214: what it is and the options

Backlog 5214 (LOW): an ordinal mixture with random effects runs no
jittered starts by default, because each start is a Laplace fit. Measured
by ordstart (`dev/ordstart-cost.R`, `y ~ x + z + (1 | g)`, n = 1000, 50
groups): 4.50 s to 103.41 s, 2.47 to 37.09, 2.79 to 41.26 (15 to 23
times). How often the starts move such a fit was never measured. Refits
(bootstrap, `influence()`, `refit()`) and `frm_allfit()` run none either.
This lane adds a second blind spot to the same fits: the threshold-SE
test is `NA` when a random-effects fit defers its SE check (filed by the
lane), so a random-effects ordinal mixture gets neither the starts nor
the flat-threshold warning at fit end.

Options:

- A. Keep off (status quo). Cost 0. Say in the docs of `starts` that
  random-effects fits need `starts = 10`.
- B. On by default with random effects (10 starts). Cost 15 to 23 times
  the fit; benefit unmeasured.
- C. Cheap starts: run the jittered starts on the fixed-effects-only
  mixture (seconds), then one Laplace fit warm-started from the best.
  Cost about 1 to 2 times the fit plus the no-RE starts (5 to 10 times a
  much cheaper fit). Benefit unmeasured; needs a lane.
- D. Measure first: the 40-seed two-class process with `(1 | g)` at
  n = 1000, starts 0 vs 10, counting moves of logLik and of the
  degenerate verdict (about 40 x 40 to 100 s per arm), then pick A, B or
  C.

Recommendation: D, and meanwhile A plus two zero-cost changes: when the
degenerate check fires on a random-effects fit, its message names
`frmtmb_control(starts = 10)`; and the threshold-SE test computes the
covariance when the fit is an ordinal mixture (force the SE analysis for
that family only, whose cost is the Hessian the first `summary()` builds
anyway).

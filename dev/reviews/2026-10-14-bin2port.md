## Findings

### 1. `no-pointwise` is not a design decision; `log_lik:130`, `:133` belong in the denominator (medium)

The ledger defines a decision as "a question frmtmb does not answer
on purpose or cannot be asked", and every existing decision class
rests on scope (no Stan, no MCMC, no exported numeric d/q functions,
SPEC.md:153-154). `no-pointwise` is different: frmtmb fits
`arma(cov = TRUE)` and has `log_lik()` of draws, and refuses the one
quantity brms answers. That quantity is well defined: it is the
leave-one-out conditional density of one observation given the rest
of its group (the non-factorized LOO of Burkner, Gabry and Vehtari),
which brms's own suite computes in closed form at
`tests.log_lik.R:179-229` (the FCOR student-t block, `log_lik:230`).
frmtmb's refusal says "leaving one out would drop part of a joint
density", which is not so for a conditional density.

The refusal is documented only in the extension API page
(`R/sampling-api.R:73`, "a `cov = TRUE` covariance block has no such
column") and in the error message. It is not in SPEC.md, in the
compat table (`R/compat.R` has no `log_lik` entry for `cov = TRUE`),
or in `vignettes/brms-migration.Rmd:595-598`, which lists what
`cov = TRUE` refuses and does not list `log_lik()`. The project
tiebreaker is to match brms unless brms is clearly wrong, and here it
is not. The lane also keeps `posterior_predict:93`, `:97`
(`arma(cov = TRUE)` draws) in the denominator as cost, so the two
halves of the same model get different treatment.

Fix:
- `dev/brmsport-bin2-survey.R`, `bin2port_g34`, block "log_lik for
  ARMA models runs without errors": class `"absent"` with a reason
  that starts "gap: frmtmb fits arma(cov = TRUE), and log_lik() of
  its draws refuses it (...); brms's row is the leave-one-out
  conditional density of one observation given the rest of its
  group, which frmtmb does not compute". A survey row cannot be a
  divergence, because no ported run exists; a gap keeps it in the
  denominator.
- `dev/brmsport-bin2-ledger.R`: remove `"no-pointwise" = "decision"`
  from `ct_kind` and the sentence added to the class comment.
- `dev/bin2port-findings.md`: "For the consolidation" says the
  denominator moves from 709 to 706, not 704; regenerate the blocks.
- `dev/test-backlog.md` (at consolidation): file "log_lik() of draws
  for `cov = TRUE` ARMA, cosy and unstr: the conditional normal or t
  density of each row given its group, as brms's
  `log_lik_gaussian_time()`".

### 2. The value check's tolerance is a mean relative difference and misses errors on small entries (low to medium)

`brms_value_compare()` holds on `isTRUE(all.equal(ref, cur))`, which
is a mean relative difference over the whole vector. Where the values
span many orders of magnitude, an entry can be badly wrong and still
hold. On the logistic-normal block (`dev/bin2port-rev-tol.R`, seed
534, log `dev/bin2port-rev-out/tol.txt`), |ref| runs from 0.263 to
5.84e6, and one entry of |ref| 0.263 off by 0.01 (3.8% relative)
holds; off by 1 it does not. The `catlpdf` mutation (every log
density + 1e-6) is caught on categorical, dirichlet and dirichlet2,
and not on logistic_normal (`log_lik:534`). The 40 current "holds"
are not in doubt: their messages record a maximum elementwise
relative difference of 1.26e-9 (`log_lik:475`) or less. The guard is
weak for the next build.

Fix, in `brms_value_compare()`: also require an elementwise bound,
for example
`held <- same_inf && isTRUE(all.equal(ref, cur)) &&
all(d <= sqrt(.Machine$double.eps) * pmax(abs(ref[ok]), 1))`.
Every one of the 40 checks that hold today stays within it (the
largest, `log_lik:475`, has abs diff 4.46e-8 at |ref| about 35,
against a bound of about 5e-7). Rerun the driver and confirm 40
holds.

### 4. The cumulative defect is real and the fix is safe, but the defect is wider than recorded (low)

Reproduced: at thresholds (0, 0, 0), (-1, -1, 1) and (-1, 1, 1) every
end category next to a tie is NaN on doubles, and finite when exact.
The cause is right: `ord_cumulative_logpmf()` (R/families.R:4227)
evaluates `ord_log_interior(a, b)` on every row and masks it with
`(1 - i1 - iK) *`, and at `a == b`, `logspace_sub(-b, -a)` is
`log(0)`, so the masked term is `0 * -Inf = NaN`. The `cs()` path
(`ord_cumulative_logpmf_cs()`) evaluates per category and is not
affected.

Not recorded: `fam_hurdle_cumulative()` calls the same function on
`pmax(y, 1)` and masks it with `(1 - i0) *`, so a hurdle zero row
(`y = 0`) is NaN too at a tie of the lowest pair. At tau = (0, 0, 1),
hu = 0.2, y = (0, 1, 4): unpatched NaN, NaN, -0.777499; patched
-1.609438 (log 0.2, exact), -0.916291 (log 0.8 - 0.693147, exact),
-0.777499.

The proposed fix `a <- q(disc * (tau[ym] - eta)) + (i1 + iK)`:
- matches the exact value at every tie for logit, probit and cloglog
  (all 18 end-category cases "ok" in `cumfix.txt`);
- is bitwise identical to 0.71.0 on 2000 random rows at distinct
  thresholds for logit, probit, cloglog and cauchit (the cauchit has
  no `logit_eta` and does not reach the changed line);
- leaves a fit unchanged: a cumulative `frm()` on 400 rows has
  identical `logLik()`, `coef()` and `vcov()`, and an identical
  objective and gradient at a perturbed point, patched and unpatched.
  This supports the tape claim: CppAD folds both `0 * x` on the
  masked rows and `x + 0` on the interior rows.

So the fix is safe to apply in the other lane. Fix to this lane:
name the hurdle_cumulative zero rows in the findings and in the
manual reason of `log_lik:462`. The fixing lane needs a test seen to
fail on rellib-r9 that pins cumulative `y = 1` and `y = K` and
hurdle_cumulative `y = 0` at tied thresholds, and the OpenBLAS run
that lane-env asks of numerical code.

### 7. `posterior_predict:420` fails on a container, not on the policy it is named for (low)

`posterior_predict_wiener()` returns `rcontinuous(..., types = "q")`,
an ndraws x 1 matrix; the assertion is `nrow(...) == ns`. The reason
says "divergence (policy)" because frmtmb.eam conditions on the
boundary, but brms's assertion does not test that: it tests the
container. The adapter already converts containers elsewhere (it
`unname()`s categorical draws to match brms). This verdict is
conservative (the row does not pass), so the rate is not inflated.

Fix: either return `matrix(out, ncol = 1)` for wiener in
`brms_adapt_posterior_predict()` and let the row pass (shape) with a
reason that names the conditioning, or keep the divergence and say in
its reason that the assertion fails on the matrix shape, and that the
conditioning on `dec` is a further difference brms's row does not
test.

### The newdata defect: reproduced, and wider than filed (high, core and frmtmb.sample)

Reproduced on rellib-r9 (`dev/bin2port-rev-newdata.R`, seed 7). On a
wiener fit with `dec(upper)`, `newdata` carries the `upper` column:

- `fitted(newdata =)` runs (3 x 4);
- `predict(newdata =, summary = FALSE)` fails with "simulating a
  response time needs the boundary each row ended at, which is
  missing";
- `predict(summary = FALSE)` without newdata runs (20 x 150);
- `simulate(newdata =)` runs.

**The defect is not specific to wiener, and for binomial it is a
silent wrong answer.** With
`frm(bf(y | trials(N) ~ x), binomial())` and newdata `x = 0` with
`N = 1000` and `N = 2`:

- `predict(newdata =, summary = FALSE)`: draw means 0.59 and 0.56,
  maximum 1 in both columns. Every draw is Bernoulli.
- `simulate(newdata =)`: row means 575.6 and 1.17, which is correct.
- frmtmb.sample's `posterior_predict(draws, newdata =)`
  (`dev/bin2port-rev-newdata2.R`, one chain, 150 draws, seed 7):
  means 0.587 and 0.547, maximum 1, while `posterior_epred()` on the
  same newdata gives 562.2 and 1.12.

The simulators default a missing term (for binomial,
`aterms[["trials"]] %||% 1`, R/families.R:1605-1631), so no error is
raised.

Location: the newdata branch builds the addition terms only under
`trunc()`, `rate()` or `thres(gr = )`, and passes `list()` otherwise:

- core, `predict_simulate()` in `R/predict-brms.R:599-610`, the
  condition `has_trunc(rspec) || has_rate(rspec) || "thres_gr" %in%
  names(rspec$aterms)`;
- frmtmb.sample, `posterior_predict.frmtmb_draws()` in
  `extensions/frmtmb.sample/R/methods-draws.R:1081-1092`, the same
  condition;
- core, `arma_cond_fill_dpars()` in `R/autocor.R:1362`, which reads
  `trunc()` only. This is the `cov = FALSE` ARMA newdata fill, so
  gaussian and student only, where it would drop `se()`.

`simulate(newdata =)` (`R/predict.R:5212-5216`) and `fitted()` call
`aterms_for_newdata()` for every term, and both are right. Scope:
every addition term a simulator reads, other than the trunc bounds,
`rate()` and `thres(gr = )`:
- `trials()`, which covers binomial and the trials-carrying families
  built on it;
- `se()`;
- custom families' `vint()` and `vreal()`;
- every registered extension term, such as frmtmb.eam's `dec()`.

It is the same fault in all of them: the simulator never sees the
term.

Fix: in those places, take
`if (length(rspec$aterms)) aterms_for_newdata(rspec, newdata) else
list()`, as `simulate()` does. Pin it with a binomial `trials()`
newdata test seen to fail on rellib-r9 (draw maximum 1 where newdata
has `N = 1000`), and a wiener `dec()` newdata test, in both core and
frmtmb.sample.

Required change to this lane's record: in "To file", item 3 of
`dev/bin2port-findings.md`, retitle it to "predict(newdata =) and
posterior_predict(newdata =) drop every addition term but trunc(),

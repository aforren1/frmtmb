# Adversarial review of lane ncgp (round of 2026-10-29, base d5d0056d)

Reviewed: the uncommitted diff in `frmtmb-wt-ncgp`, installed in `wt-ncgp-lib`,
against `rellib-r16`. Scripts are `dev/review-ncgp-*`. Logs are in
`dev/review-ncgp-out/`. brms 2.23.0 was run, not recalled. I read `lib:` in every
log. I wrote nothing into the lane's files or libraries. The mutants ran in process.

## Verdict: FIX

The route is right. The tape, its value, its order, the readers and the merge
all hold. The start does not. On ordinary curves `frm()` stops up to 499 nats below
the closed form's maximum, with conv 0 and no warning. It does this more often than
0.78.0, and it does it at `y * 1000` where it did not at `y`. The lane's table of 24
rows did not see this, because its designs do not contain a trend plus a wiggle.

The fix is small and I measured it: a closed-form warm start, about 100 lines, no
reader changes. See "The recommendation" below.

## The recommendation (item 8)

**Add the closed form now, as a warm start for the optimizer, not as a route.** `fit$obj` stays the
z tape, so every reader, the joint precision and the sampler stay on one path, and the section 7 table
of the lane holds unchanged. Only `start` changes.

`dev/review-ncgp-margin.R`, about 120 lines, a prototype that reads only the frame:
a coarse scan on numbers, then `nlminb` on a tape of `dmvnorm`. `review-ncgp-margtest.R`, 14 spellings
at noise 0.1 and 1e-3, 28 rows: plain, covariate, `by = f`, `by = w`, `cmc = FALSE`, two terms, `(1 | g)`,

Gains: `by = w` 35.0 and 34.6 nats, replicates 1.38. It is generic through `cs_entry()$vcov()`.

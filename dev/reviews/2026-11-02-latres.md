# Review of lane latres, 2026-11-02

Base d5d0056d (frmtmb 0.78.0). Under review: the uncommitted diff in
`frmtmb-wt-latres`, installed in `wt-latres-lib`. Before-build: `rellib-r16`.
Every script is `dev/review-latres-*`, every log `dev/review-latres-out/`
(local). I read the diff and ran attack 1 and started attack 2 before I read
`dev/latres-findings.md`. The clocks ran with other lanes on the machine, so I give the
minimum and the maximum of three fits.

## Verdict

**FIX.** The model is right: the program identity holds by a route that does not
use the lane's translator, and the mutants fail. What is wrong is what a user
reads after the fit: `predict()` returns 1e300 on ordinary fits, binary and
post-link fits are silently biased with no statement anywhere, a boundary fit
prints `ar[1] = 1` with a zero standard error, and one guard refuses a model brms
builds while saying that brms refuses it. M1 to M5 need code in this round. M6 and brms-45 need a decision of the user. M7 is older than
the lane and is filed.

## Findings

against 0.44. Binomial(3), seed 3: sderr 1.02 against 1.26. A user who moves a

Fix: a warning from `frm()` on a binary or `trials() <= 5` response with a latent
term (a message class the user can silence), which names `frm_sample()`; and a
statement with these numbers in `?frmtmb-autocor` and in the row
`group:autocor x kind:family`. The reference numbers are in the table above.

**M2. `predict()` returns 1e300 on ordinary fits, and only on this route.**
`dev/review-latres-predict-ctrl.R`, seeds 1, 3, 7, 9, 13, 17, 19. The same data and
the same model written as `ar(t, g, cov = TRUE)` and as `ar1(ft + 0 | g)`, poisson,
60 rows, largest count at most 10:

| seed | sderr | SE of log sderr, latent / ar1 | largest `predict()` Estimate, latent | ar1 | latent, `propagate_error = FALSE` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | 0.056 | 12.0 / 0.44 | **3.7e+304** | 6.5 | 5.3 |
| 17 | 0.035 | 9.8 / 5.4 | **7.5e+304** | 3.3 | 3.2 |
| 19 | 0.035 | 28.9 / 0.45 | **1.6e+301** | 5.8 | 5.8 |
| 1 | 0.36 | 0.43 / 0.40 | 5.8 | 5.7 | 5.1 |

Cause: the block holds `err = exp(theta_1) * b`, so a parameter draw of `theta_1`
with an SE of 10 multiplies the latents by `exp(30)` before the inverse link. A
random effect holds `b` on its natural scale, so its draws cannot do this. The fit
gives code 0 and no warning on several of these, and `predict()` warns only when a
draw reaches `Inf`. `dev/review-latres-boundary.R` has the same on `y ~ x + ar` over
data with no latent at all: seeds 6, 13, 15 read 3e300, code 0, no fit warning.

Fix: in the draw of a latent block, hold `theta` at its estimate where its SE is
above a stated limit, or draw `err` from its own Gaussian (the delta method on
`err`, which `rr_jacobians()` already forms). Test: a fit with a small `sderr`, the
ratio of the largest `predict()` Estimate to the largest count.

**M3. A boundary fit does not tell the user what happened.**
`dev/review-latres-boundary.R`, seeds 1 to 15, a random intercept and no AR in the
data (a common shape: 8 of 15 seeds stop this way). Seed 1 prints:

    Correlation Structures:
          Estimate Est.Error l-95% CI u-95% CI
    ar[1]        1         0        1     1.00
    sderr        0         0        0     0.09

with one warning that ends "so the estimates are at the maximum and their standard
errors are judged as for a converged fit". `diagnose()$singular` is `NULL`. The SE
of log sderr is 1.4 to 192 across the seeds, and of the `ar` chart 1300 to 47000.
`ar(cov = TRUE)`, `cosy()` and `(1 | g)` read one logLik, -107.8513, because `ar -> 1`
with `sderr -> 0` is the random intercept with a marginal sd of 0.55, which is finite,
and that is what the fit has found. The lane read this correctly in its section 5 and
"did not change how the optimizer reports them". On bernoulli it is 26 of 200 fits.
The zero SE and the interval [1, 1] are false: `ar` is not known to be 1, and
`sderr` is not known to be 0.

The boundary message also names `theta_2 (in cosy(t, g))`, not `cosy`.

Fix: a message at the fit naming the limit (`ar[1]` at 1 with `sderr` at 0: the data
read as `cosy()` or `(1 | g)`, and the marginal sd is the number that is identified),
and NA, not 0, in `Est.Error` and the interval of these two rows. Name parameters
by their brms names in the boundary message.

or bound it. The lane's "warned, not refused" is right only if a warning always
fires, and today on Gamma it does not.

**M5. A guard fires on a correct model, and claims brms refuses it.**
`dev/review-latres-guard.R`, seed 20261102:

    bf(y ~ x + ar(t, g, cov = TRUE)) + nlf(shape ~ exp(a)) + lf(a ~ 1), negbinomial()
    ERR: ar(): Latent residuals are not implemented for non-linear models. brms
    refuses the same call: ...

`dev/review-latres-refuse.R`: brms builds this and `rstan::stanc()` passes. brms's
guard is `is.btnl(bframe)` in `stan_ac()`, the mu predictor alone. `check_autocor_latent()`
tests `length(resp$nlpars) || length(resp$nl_dpars)`, so a nonlinear `shape` or `zi` beside
a linear `mu` is refused, and so is any auxiliary nlpar. No test holds the absent case.

Fix: refuse only when `mu` itself has a body (`"mu" %in% nl_dpars`, or the primary dpar
of the response). Test the absent case with `nlf(shape ~ ...)` and `nlf(zi ~ ...)`, and the
identity of that model against brms's `log_prob`. Lane nlfix adds
`bf(nl = TRUE, autocor = )`; the refusal must still fire there.

`dev/review-latres-newrows.R`, seed 167, parameters held by Stan's Fixed_param, 4000
draws of `err`, x = 0, intercept 0.5, `ar` 0.5, 5 new rows:

| sderr | marginal sd | E[y], by hand | brms `posterior_epred` | `fitted(newdata)` | brms / frm | brms `posterior_predict` | `simulate()` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.25 | 0.29 | 1.72 | 1.71 | 1.65 | 1.04 | 1.73 | 1.74 |

| 1.5 | 1.73 | 7.39 | 7.52 | 1.65 | **4.56** | 7.59 | 7.37 |

Bernoulli: 0.6225 against brms's 0.5613 at sderr 2 (brms / frm 0.90), 0.5925 at
sderr 1. Fitted data, poisson (`dev/review-latres-sample.R`, seed 3): `fitted(newdata)`
0.93 against brms's 1.35; with ar 0.9, 1.01 against 1.50. A real fit, seed 167:
`mean(fitted())` 1.92, `mean(fitted(newdata = d))` 1.73, mean y 2.02,
`mean(predict(newdata = d))` 2.04.

`predict()` and `simulate()` are right on new rows, within Monte Carlo error of brms
and of the hand value (subject to M2). `fitted()` is at `err = 0`. This is stated in
`?frmtmb-autocor`, NEWS and the compat row, and it has a precedent: `fitted()` on a new
level of a random effect is also at 0 (`exp(b0)` in the same script).

Recommendation: `fitted()` on new rows on the response scale returns the expectation
over the latent, by Gauss-Hermite at each row's marginal sd. The integral is one
dimensional and needs no draw, and it is brms's expected value, so "an ML point has no
draws" is no obstacle. `frm_linpred()` stays at 0, where 0 is the mean on the link scale.
If the user keeps `err = 0`, `fitted(newdata)` needs a message on a non-identity link,
because the current state is a documented number that a reader takes for a mean, off by
a factor of 2 at a marginal sd of 1.15.

series, and have `fitted()` skip the SE with a message above a row limit, as an 80 GB
request is worse than an NA. A row variance from the sparse factor (`solve(L, a_i)`, or a selected inverse)
removes it for every model.

### MINOR

**m1. `coef()` lists the latents as a group.** `dev/review-latres-consumers2.R`: `y ~ x + z +
ar(t, g, cov = TRUE)` gives `names(coef(fit)) = "g"`, a data frame of 150 rows, whose
Intercept varies by row. With `(1 | g)` beside it, a second element named
`ar(t, g, cov = TRUE)`, of 150 rows. brms says "No group-level effects detected"
(`dev/review-latres-coef.R`). `ranef()` was fixed and `coef()` was not, and
`?frmtmb-autocor` says they are not random effects of a group. Fix: filter the block
in `coef.frmtmb_fit`, and refuse as `ranef` does for a fit with none.

**m2. An opaque error on new data with no time column.** `simulate(fit, newdata = d[,
c("x", "g")])` with a time variable named `t`: "anyNA() applied to non-(list or vector) of type
'closure'" (`dev/review-latres-misc.R`), because `t` resolves to `base::t`. `predict()` the
same. Fix: `latres_new_spec()` checks the time and group variables against `newdata` and
names the term. `fitted(newdata)` with no group column answers silently, which is
consistent with `err = 0`.

**m3. A refusal brms does not have, in an internal name.** `asym_laplace()` x every term
(`dev/review-latres-grid.R`, 6 cells): brms builds and stanc passes, `frm()` says "The Laplace
approximation of this model's likelihood has no maximum. `latres(ar(t, g, cov = TRUE))` gives...".
The refusal is sound, since the density has a kink. But `latres(...)` is a name the lane made
reserved, the compat row `group:autocor x kind:family` does not list it, and the "154 to 0"
claim holds only because the grid did not hold this family. `dry_run = "frame"` accepts it and
the fit refuses. Fix: print the term's label, add its own compat row, and say so in the findings.

**m4. All-single-row groups are refused where brms builds.** `dev/review-latres-refuse.R`:
`ar(id, id2, cov = TRUE)` with one row per group, brms builds; `frm()` says the coefficient "does
not enter the likelihood". Under `cov = TRUE` it does enter, as `sderr / sqrt(1 - ar^2)`, but
is not identified. The refusal is the better behavior. It is a divergence that no list
records, and the sentence is false on this form. Same for `cosy(gr = id2)`.

**m5. `frm_sample()` names.** `variables()` on draws lists `b[1]`...`b[n]`, `theta_1`, `theta_2`,
`ar[1]`, `sderr` where brms has `zerr[n]`, `err[n]`, `ar[1]`, `sderr`. Three things are worse than the lane
filed. `b[k]` in brms is a population-level slope. Under `ma()`, `arma()` and `cosy()`
there are more `b[k]` than rows (72 for 60 rows, `dev/review-latres-misc.R`), so a
user cannot derive `err` from them without the internal `latres_expand()`. They are not `err`
on any chain with `sderr` != 1. Severity MINOR, since `posterior_epred()`, `log_lik()` and the
summaries read them correctly. Fix: add derived `err[n]` columns on request (`latres_expand()`
per draw is one gather), and rename the state columns.

**m6. The `residuals_osa` row is right except on bernoulli, where the base is broken.**
`dev/review-latres-osa.R`, 20 datasets, against the exact one-step predictive: Gamma mean 0.011, sd
1.007, lag-1 correlation 0.027, correlation with the exact value 0.999; poisson mean -0.020,
sd 0.994, 0.037, 0.975 (randomized against mid-P). So the row is right on those, and on negbinomial,
binomial and unsorted rows (`dev/review-latres-osa2.R`). On bernoulli every residual is
near -3.7 (seed 1: mean -3.71, sd 0.34) on latent fits, and on rellib-r16 too with no
term at all (`dev/review-latres-osa3.R`: plain `(1 | g)`, mean -4.33). That is an older defect
worth filing at MAJOR, in `residuals(type = "osa")`, not in this lane. The ordinal residuals read
the same three decimals under a latent term and under `(1 | g)` (seeds 1 to 3): check that too.
Fix here: the note says "not on bernoulli".

**m7. The note on `group:autocor x kind:aterm` is true for what it names and silent on the
rest.** `dev/review-latres-compat.R`: weights, cens, trunc, rate, trials, thres, subset all fit
where brms builds; se, vint, vreal are refused where brms refuses. The mi() row is wrong on these
families: brms builds `y | mi() ~ x + ar()` on lognormal and Gamma, `frm()` says "mi() responses need
a gaussian or student model", and the row's note says mi() works under cov = FALSE. Also,
`group:autocor x fitted` is "works" with a note that describes a condition, so it should read
conditional.

Two rows are false outright (`dev/review-latres-compat2.R`). `fcor() x residuals_osa` moved from
refused to conditional with the note about latent residuals; `fcor()` has no latent residual on any
family, and `residuals(type = "osa")` refuses it on gaussian. The specific `ar() x fitted` row still
says "Under cov = TRUE the mean structure is untouched", which is false on a latent fit: `fitted()` carries
`err`. The reasons in `ar() x quadrature` and `ar() x importance` ("a joint density over each group") are false
on a latent fit, though the refusal is right. Fix these rows.

**m8. Refusal under `cov = FALSE` in `autocor_matrix()`.** The message says "a regression of each
row on the earlier residuals of its group", which is false on a latent term (a moving average
of latents, with a well-defined correlation). `?autocor_matrix` now says it gives the latent
correlation. Fix the wording, or compute it.

**m9. `latres_steps()` is quadratic on one series.** `simulate(fit, newdata = d)`, one series:
2.25 s at 20000 rows, 49 s at 100000 (`dev/review-latres-perf.R`); 0.01 s with 200 groups. The `while`
loop rescans `rest` per generation, and one series has `n` generations. Fix: the generation of a row is
its position in its group, which `autocor_cond_positions()` already holds, so one `split()` does it.

## The decision that needs the user: brms-45

Reproduced (`dev/review-latres-brms45.R`, seed 167, `ar` 0.6, `sderr` 0.5):

| | `log_prob` | `sum(log_lik) + log N(zerr)` | gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| poisson, `cov = FALSE` | -531.5209 | -524.7437 | **-6.78** |
| Gamma log, `cov = FALSE` | -747.8454 | -634.3678 | **-113.48** |
| poisson, `cov = TRUE` (control) | | | -1.7e-13 |

By hand: `sum(dpois(y, exp(eta + err + ar * lag(err))))` is -531.5209, and with no lag term
-524.7437. brms's `posterior_epred()` equals `exp(eta + err)` to 0 relative; frm's `fitted()` at the same
point equals the program's mean within 4.4e-16.

The code: `predictor_ac()` in `predictor.R` is `if (!is.null(prep$ac[["err"]])) eta + err else if
(has_ac_class(..., "arma")) .predictor_arma(...)`. With latent residuals the first branch always
takes it, and `prepare_predictions_ac()` does prepare `ar`, but nothing reads it. `stan_ac()` writes
`Err[n, 1:Kar] * ar` into the model block. So the sum is in the posterior that was sampled
and not in any method. `?ar` says of `cov = FALSE` that it "is only available for gaussian
models and some of its generalizations", so this path has no documented contract.

The consequence on data (`dev/review-latres-sample.R`): `ar(t, g)` poisson, `loo` elpd brms -244.2,
`frm_sample()` -224.6, 19.6 nats; on bernoulli brms's `log_lik` reads `-Inf` and its `loo` has no value.

**Decision: brms is clearly wrong.** Its pointwise densities do not add up to the density it
sampled, its `loo` scores a model no chain ran, and the gap is not small or at an edge. Recommend
keeping the lane's reading, keeping `test-latres-brms.R`'s pin of both halves, and reporting
brms-45 upstream, which is still "not reported". Reading `err` alone would buy digit parity with a
defect.

## What I verified that held

1. **Independent identity.** No translator: `dev/review-latres-id.R` hand-maps (b0, b, sderr, ar, ma,
   cosy, cortime) at three chosen points that are not estimates, solves brms's own `log_prob` in `zerr`
   by Newton on `rstan::grad_log_prob`, and compares with `obj$fn`. Largest difference over 24 cases: 7.2e-8,
   the finite-difference error of my Hessian (ar, ma, arma, cosy, unstr; unsorted, ragged, single-row
   groups beside longer ones, time gaps, `time =` absent, `gr =` absent; poisson, Gamma log, bernoulli,

**RF1. Replace M1's condition with the first-order Laplace correction.** On a fit, per row, `h3` and `h4` are the third and
fourth derivatives of `-log density` in `eta`, and `v = A H^-1 A'` is `Cov(err | y)` at the inner mode. Then

    C = -1/8 sum(h4_i v_ii^2) + 1/8 sum(h3_i h3_j v_ii v_ij v_jj) + 1/12 sum(h3_i h3_j v_ij^3)

## Findings

### MAJOR 1. The restart's message is lost when a later step moves the fit

Merge step 4 moved `prior_conflict_message()` from the restart (lane
nlstop: `R/fit.R`, right after `prior_conflict_escape()`) to the end of
the fit, where it reads `fit$opt$prior_escape`. Between the two,
`escape_stationary()`, `mo_search()` and `sd_up_escape()` can replace
`opt` with a new optimizer result, and none of them carries
`prior_escape` over (`newton_polish()` copies `opt` and keeps it) (`escape_stationary()` returns the
run `o`, `R/fit.R:4309`; `mo_search()` likewise, `:4907` to `:4977`;
`sd_up_escape()` returns its own `o`, `:4403`). Only `mixture_starts()`
was meant to drop it. So a fit that the restart moved to the mode at the
prior's location, and that a later escape then improved, returns that
mode in silence: the 0.80.0-type "lower maximum in silence" the lane
fixed, now in the higher-mode direction (the user is not told that the
posterior has two modes or that frm() picked one).

Reproducer: `dev/reviews/rel082-review-probe2.R` (data of
`test-fit-restart.R`: y near 1e6, slope 5e4, `normal(0, 1000)` on `b`,
`normal(1e6, 1e6)` on the Intercept; seeds 6201 to 6206; a skewed
residual for `skew_normal()`, a group shift for `(1 | g)`, a step for
`mo(m)`). In all 24 fits the restart won (traced).

| rellib-r21 (`probe2-r21.log`) | 17 of 24 | 17 of 24 | as below |

### MINOR 1. `gp_inner_pd()` reads a non-finite inner Hessian as positive definite

`R/gp-start.R:1312`. CHOLMOD factorizes a matrix with NaN or Inf on the
diagonal with no warning and no error (Matrix 1.7.6), so the function
returns TRUE (`probe1.log` case 4 on a 3000 x 3000 matrix; `probe3.log`,
the function itself with a NaN and an Inf diagonal entry: TRUE, TRUE).
On a matrix that is not positive definite Matrix 1.7.6 warns ("CHOLMOD
warning 'not positive definite'") and then errors ("leading principal
minor of order 2 is not positive"), so finite input is judged right
either way. Other warning sources: any warning inside the `tryCatch()`
is a FALSE verdict, so a future Matrix deprecation warning from
`Cholesky()` would turn the relinearization off on every fit with no
sign; and `obj$fn(par)`, `spHess()` and the `as()` coercion are outside
the handler, so their warnings reach the user from inside
`gp_relin_regular()`. TMB's `spHess(random = TRUE)` is a `dsCMatrix`
with `uplo = "L"` (`probe1.log`), so `forceSymmetric()` keeps every
off-diagonal entry; that part is right.

Fix: `if (!all(is.finite(A@x))) return(FALSE)` before the factor; catch
only the CHOLMOD "not positive definite" warning; a unit test with a
NaN entry.

under `GIT_OPTIONAL_LOCKS=0`. The index sha256 stays the same after

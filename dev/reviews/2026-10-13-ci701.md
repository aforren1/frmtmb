## R1. D2 on a block of dimension 3 or more (fix 1)

`se_edge_cor()` tests `|t| > se_cor_clamp_theta()` (70.7) on every
correlation parameter of a `us` block, and `se_pull_back()` sets the
parameter to +/-70.7. That holds only for `d = 2`. `us_chol_L()`
normalizes rows: `corr(i, j)` is a dot product of normalized rows, so
for `d >= 3`:

- `|t| > 70.7` does not mean `|corr| > 0.9999`: `test-v16.R`'s
  `us(spp + 0 | site)` ends at `t = (1318.4, -707.9)` in row 3 with
  correlations 0.881 and -0.235 (`d3.log`);
- moving one parameter changes the direction of its row, and with it
  every correlation that uses that row.

Construction (`dev/ci701-rev-d3c.R`, case A seed 3, `(1 + x + z | g)`
with the x slope a multiple of the intercept, start `t21 = 2000`):
`se_edge_cor()` fires on `theta_4`. After the pull, `corr(2, 1)` is
0.99990, but `corr(3, 2)` is 0.20613 where the fit has 0.19674, a
move of 4.8 percent in an interior correlation. The check then gives
the three log sds SEs 0.13471, 0.14912 and 0.15022, read off the
Hessian at that other point. noD2 calls them flat. No reference
validates either number. (That fit has code 1 and a gradient warning,
so on 0.70.0's rules nobody reads its SEs. After lane illse, more
code-1 fits keep the analysis, see R7.)

D2 did not misfire on the reachable cases: on `test-v16.R`'s block,
on the K = 4 block of frmtmb.sample `test-brms-pins.R` (`t` up to
692) and on 10 rank-deficient `d = 3` constructions with `t31, t32`
from 675 to 75000 (`d3.log`, `d3b.log`), `se_at_edge()` was FALSE and
lane = noD2 at every printed digit. But all the lane's evidence for
D2 is `d = 2`: the review-v29 block, the rs20 fits, RB4 and the
`(mo(x) | g)` fit. Restricting to `d == 2` loses nothing that was
measured. The general case (a pull in correlation space) belongs in
the next minor, with the Cholesky-form `us` density the lane already
defers there.

`dev/ci701-rev-batt.R` uses the review-v29 data moved by 0 to 4 ulps,

## R5. Rounding: where the branch went

- The `|t| > 70.7` cut is continuous. At `t` near 70.7 the pull is a
  tiny move, and below the cut the 0.70.0 route gave the same numbers
  (seeds 2 and 17).
- `se_at_edge()` uses `se_edge_tol = 1e-6`. That is absolute, not
  relative. The change it reads at the stop is 1.2e-8 to 3.3e-8 on
  all 8 rs20 fits past `|t| = 58` (`dev/ci701-rev-rsdl.R`), -1.8e-8
  to 5.3e-9 at the natural review-v29 stops, and 3.0e-7 at
  `t0 = 500` (3.3 times below the threshold). On the review-v29 data
  the threshold is near `t = 335`. That is an estimate from the value
  at 500 and a change that goes as `1 / t^3`, not a measurement. No
  natural stop is near it. Where
  a stop does cross the threshold, the jump is the difference between
  the fit-point and clamp Hessians, at most 5.3e-3 relative on the
  measured fits. The test allows 1e-2. The new branch has a bounded
  effect. The old one switched between "flat" and finite.
- P/E-core pinning changed nothing at printed precision on this fit
  (80 of 80 lines).

## R7. Lane illse

A three-way `git merge-file` (base `d2ef6312`, line endings
normalized) gives one conflict hunk in `R/se-check.R`
`cov_from_hessian()`: the lane's `edge <- se_edge_sd(fit, H) | ec`
against illse's `se_reparam_cov()` block after `edge`. Resolve it by
keeping both. The reparam block is then skipped on an edge-correlation
fit, as on a fit with an sd at zero. `R/confint.R` and both test files
merge cleanly. `NEWS.md` conflicts only at the shared top heading.

Semantic: illse changes `sdr_rescue_hessian()` so that the analysis
applies whatever the optimizer's code, unless the stop is short
(`se_explained == "convergence"`). Its comment cites this same
review-v29 fit. After the merge, the SEs that D2 decides reach users
on more code-1 fits, which is one more reason for fix 1. illse's
`cov_fixed_root` does not exist on edge fits (reparam is skipped), so
D1, which reads `cov_fixed_prop`, does not conflict with it. At
consolidation, run test-review-v29.R, test-se-check.R,
test-se-flags.R and test-se-tier.R on the merged tree under ref, ob26
and ob32.

## R8. Item 2: the ordinal mixture

`dev/ci701-rev-omx.R`, x moved by -3 to 3 ulps (seed 9000 + k,
k = 0..15): "singular convergence (7)" in 3 of 16 (ref) and 6 of 16
(ob26). Every log-likelihood is between -380.18981 and -380.18973. A
BFGS polish from the estimate gains at most 1.24e-5 (code 7) and
4.75e-6 (code 0). nlminb restarts from moved points gain at most
1.4e-6. The others land 1.09 to 4.69 lower, on other local maxima.
So code 7 and code 0 are the same maximum, as the lane says.

Allowing the warning does not hide a stop short of the maximum with a
large gradient: the gradient warning is not in the allowed list, so
it escapes. A stop at a lower local maximum would pass, but it passed
before too, with code 0. On this machine the file passes on refE
(99/0).

## Smaller points (no change needed for the patch)

- D1 also restores the other sd's interval in a block where one sd is
  at zero, not only where a correlation is at +/-1. NEWS names only
  the correlation case.
- When `ec` holds, `cov_from_hessian()` builds a second full Hessian
  (2 np gradients) on every call, including the one from
  `sdr_rescue_hessian()`. That is 90 gradients on RB4. It is
  acceptable, but the findings do not count it.
- The test comment says that a start at `t = 500` "stops there on
  every platform tried". That holds on 5 of 5 arms here. If a platform
  moves that stop, both sides of the branch still pass: below about
  t = 335 noD2 holds, and above about t = 1000 D2 holds (R3).

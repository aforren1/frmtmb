Worktree `frmtmb-wt-nestquad`, base 3cd2a0c8 (frmtmb 0.79.1). Under review: the
uncommitted diff, installed in `wt-nestquad-lib`. Before-build: `rellib-r18`.
Mutant builds: `rev-nestquad-lib/m1`, `rev-nestquad-lib/m2` (sources in

## Verdict: FIX

The integral is right where the rule is anchored: on ten designs the lane did not
use, the reported log-likelihood agrees with my reference to 1e-13 to 1.5e-3, the
one-block and Laplace routes are bitwise unchanged, and every refusal by name fires
in under 0.05 s with class `frmtmb_error`. What is wrong is what happens away from
the anchor and around the tape: the frozen tape has spurious optima once a variance
parameter moves, so profile and uniroot intervals are silently wrong (M1) and the
re-anchor loop can cycle with no warning of its own (M2); and the memory guard
under-predicts a Student family's peak by 2.65 times (M3). M1 to M3 need code in
this round.

## Findings

### MAJOR

**M1. `confint(method = "profile")` and `"uniroot"` on a nested fit are wrong,
silently.** `dev/review-nestquad-postfit.R`, `-profile.R`, `-profile2.R`,
`-state.R`; logs `postfit.log`, `profile.log`, `profile2.log`, `state.log`. 117
Bernoulli rows, `(1 | school/cls_uni)`, seed 20261107. Estimate of `x` 1.1712:

| method | lower | upper |
|---|---|---|
| Wald | 0.646 | 1.696 |
| profile (fit) | **0.520** | **0.953** |
| uniroot (fit, fresh object) | **0.499** | 1.770 |
| Laplace fit, profile | 0.696 | 1.762 |
| one-block quadrature `(1 \| school)`, profile | 0.681 | 1.661 |

The profile interval excludes the estimate. At its endpoints my reference profile
(beta_x fixed, the rest maximized) gives `2*drop` 8.01 and 0.74, target 3.84. Both
methods call `TMB::tmbprofile()` / `tmbroot()` on `object$obj`, the frozen nested
tape (`R/confint.R:629`). That tape goes below its own optimum away from the anchor:
with beta_x fixed at 0.69 and the rest minimized, the frozen tape gives 63.586 at
sds 0.22 and 0.11, against 63.869 at the fit; the rule re-anchored there, and my
reference, both give 66.059. So the tape over-states the integral by 2.47 log units
where the variances shrink, and the profile reads that. The only signal is
`simpleWarning collapsing to unique 'x' values` from `approx()`. A trace on
`quad_nest_fit` and `quad_fit` counted 0 calls during the profile: the lane's record
("refits with a map through the nested route", findings, "Post-fit methods") is
wrong; its check was that the call returns. The state of the fit is not corrupted
(`state.log`: `last.par.best` and Wald unchanged after profile and uniroot). Fix:
profile by refitting through `quad_nest_fit()` with the parameter mapped, or refuse
`profile`/`uniroot` on nested fits; at least stop when the profile minimum is below
the fit's objective.

**M2. The re-anchor loop can cycle; at its cap the fit says nothing specific.**
`dev/review-nestquad-acc.R sep` (`MODE=anchor` and `MODE=rounds20`), logs
`anchor-sep.log`, `rounds20-sep.log`. A near-separable Bernoulli (64 rows, 10 x 25
levels, seed 20261106). Verbose rounds at the shipped cap of 4:

    round 1: objective -29.647436, moved 54.1, re-anchored grad 1.08
    round 2: objective   8.340903, moved 3.42, re-anchored grad 0.0867
    round 3: objective   8.316097, moved 5.15, re-anchored grad 0.0242
    round 4: objective   8.306156, moved 4.45, re-anchored grad 0.0451

Round 1's objective -29.6 is a log-likelihood of +29.6 on binary data: the optimizer
walked to where the frozen rule is not a rule for the integral (the cause of M1).
With the cap lifted to 20 in memory (`assignInNamespace`, package files untouched)
the loop never settles: objectives 8.156 to 8.358, moves of 2.0 to 17.2 per round,
re-anchored gradient never below 0.08. The reported value is set by the cap:
logLik -8.3066 at 4 rounds, -8.4761 at 20. Nothing in `quad_nest_fit()` reports
hitting the cap; the user sees the generic `frmtmb_warning` "Large maximum absolute
gradient ... (0.0451 at (Intercept))" only because this gradient is large. A cap hit
with a gradient between `grad_tol` and the generic threshold would be silent. Fix:
bound the step of a round (trust region on theta, or reject a round whose re-anchored
objective is worse than the frozen one by more than a tolerance), and warn with a
class when the cap is hit. The logLik is the integral at the final anchor in every
case I checked (difference 6e-14 to 9e-13 on four designs; 5.6e-8 at 20 rounds),
and the final tape is stationary when the loop converges (grad 3.8e-6 to 9.9e-5).

**M3. The memory guard under-predicts a Student family by 2.65 times.**
`dev/review-nestquad-mem.R`, logs `mem-*-3000.log`, `mem2-student-3000.log`, seed
20261108, 3000 rows, 150 outer x 4 inner x 5 rows, nodes 11 x 15, delta = peak
working set after the fit less the working set before it:

| family | values per stacked row | predicted MB | measured MB | pred/measured |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gamma | 10.7 | 1808 | 1283 | 1.41 |
| negbinomial | 18.4 | 3029 | 2066 | 1.47 |
| cumulative | 10.3 | 1730 | 1109 | 1.56 |
| hurdle_poisson | 10.6 | 1740 | 991 | 1.76 |
| zero_inflated_negbinomial | 15.8 | 2640 | 1858 | 1.42 |
| skew_normal | 14.7 | 2407 | 1787 | 1.35 |
| weibull | 13.7 | 2304 | 1773 | 1.30 |
| lognormal + cens | 9.3 | 1593 | 1033 | 1.54 |
| **student** | 11.7 | 1992 | **5278, 5307** | **0.38** |
| exgaussian (600 rows) | 203.7 | 15298 | 5172 | 2.96 |

The Student peak is transient inside the first nested `MakeADFun()`: the stage log
reads peak 465 MB before it, 5632 MB after it, with 563 MB still held. It reproduced
in two processes. By the guard's linear model, 6000 Student rows predict about 3.8
GB and pass the default `quad_max_gb = 4`, then take about 10.5 GB if the transient
is linear in rows as the others are (not run: above this review's 8 GB budget). The light/heavy
split on values per stacked row does not see this: Student has fewer values per row
than negbinomial. Fix: measure the taping transient (probe-tape peak, not only its
size), or calibrate the factor on the family's own operations; add Student to the
calibration set. Exgaussian is the opposite error: at 600 rows the guard predicts
15.3 GB against 5.2 GB measured, so the default limit refuses it above about 150
rows (3000 rows: refused at 74.2 GB, `exg2.log`).

### M2: the loop

- **It cannot cycle.** A round is accepted only when the objective re-anchored at the
  candidate is at most the one re-anchored at the current point, plus
  1e-8 x max(1, |f|). Each value is the rule anchored at its own point, so the
  accepted sequence does not rise (beyond that tolerance). A rejection cuts the
  radius to a quarter of the step. The loop ends after 25 rounds or after three
  rejections in a row. With the standardized nodes (`u = sd z`, every weight a
  constant), theta reaches the integrand only through the likelihood, which removes
  the prior-density blow-up that let round 0's optimizer walk off.
- **Near-separable design** (`fr1-anchor-sep.log`, `fr1-intref-sep.log`). Three
  steps are accepted (1, 2, 4), then three are rejected (8, 2, 0.5).
  `frmtmb_quad_unsettled` fires (gradient 0.138), and so does the generic gradient
  warning. logLik is -8.3952863; re-anchored at the estimate it is the same to
  2.8e-10. The integrate() reference at the estimate is -8.3778084, an error of
  1.75e-2 (my GH is 6.5e-4 from integrate() at k = 120). The inner sd at the estimate
  is 16, running to its boundary. The rule's own error there is not monotone in the
  nodes: 1.7e-2 at 11 x 15, 6.0e-2 at 21 x 21, 5.8e-3 at 31 x 31, 4.5e-2 at 41 x 41.
  - My answer: acceptable with the classed warning. The Laplace error at the same
    point is 0.75. The estimate sits on a separation boundary where no finite answer
    is right, and round 0 gave -8.3066 or -8.4761 depending on the cap, silently.
  - The warning gives no size for the error, and "more nodes" is not a sure remedy
    here (41 x 41 is worse than 31 x 31).
- **Adversarial designs** (`dev/review-nestquad-acc.R`, seed 20261106):

| design | settled, rounds | logLik error | notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| flat outer marginal: 3 schools, no school effect | TRUE, 1 | 4.0e-7 | outer sd 4.6e-5 |
| inner sd 0 in the truth | TRUE, 2 | 1.3e-9 | inner sd 0.22 |

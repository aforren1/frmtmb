### 1a. `mo_search()`: obj_settle() in place of moparity's restore

The resolved tail is moparity's step 3 (`cox_face_leave()` and its
refit), then `obj_settle(obj, opt, best, saved)` unconditionally, then
`if (!runs) return(opt)`. Moparity's `probed` flag and its
`if (!runs) { if (probed) obj_state_restore(obj, saved); return(opt) }`
are gone.

By reading: `obj_settle()` (lane optrap's version) restores `saved`
when `best` is `opt`, which is exactly moparity's restore, and then
evaluates `fn(best$par)` only if the restored state's last or best
point is not `best$par` (`obj_state_at()`). So it is moparity's restore
plus one possible evaluation, never less. When step 3 refitted and won,
moparity already called `obj_settle()`, and still does.

By measurement, `dev/relrev070-instr.R` wraps `obj_settle()` (read
only) and records, per call, the caller, whether `best` is `opt`,
whether the restore alone leaves the objective at `best$par`, and the
state at the end:

| study (rellib-r8) | calls | best is opt | restore alone at the point | at the point after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cox()`, 200 seeds (`dev/relrev070-cox-study.R`) | 200, all `mo_search` | 43 | 43 of 43 | 200 of 200 |
| `mo()`, seeds 1 to 400 (`dev/relrev070-mo-study.R`) | 400, all `mo_search` | 280 | 280 of 280 | 400 of 400 |

So the extra evaluation never fired; on these fits the merged tail
does exactly what moparity's did. `dev/relrev070-cox-runs.R` isolates
the case the question names, a `cox()` fit whose step 3 refits nothing
but probes a face: 39 of 200 seeds end with `runs` 0, 28 of them with a
weight below `mo_face_tol` (so `cox_face_leave()` probed). On both
`rellib-r8` and `wt-moparity-lib` all 200 fits end with the objective's
last and best points at the optimum, 28 of 28 probed ones included, and
every column (runs, polish steps, smallest weight, state, logLik, both
SEs) is `identical()` between the builds (`cox-runs-cmp.txt`).

The studies themselves (`dev/relrev070-cmp.R`, `cmp.txt`):

  every column identical to lane moparity's rds and to the first

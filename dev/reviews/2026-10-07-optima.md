## Blocking

### B1. The sampler's simplex posterior changes; the record says it does not

frmtmb.sample puts no density on `zeta`, so the coordinates themselves
are the prior: a flat prior on the chart is a different prior on the
simplex from a flat prior on the softmax. Measured by a delegated run
(`dev/optima-rev-sample1-implied.R` to `-sample5-summary.R`, logs
`dev/optima-rev-out/sample*.log`; 2 chains, iter 2000, warmup 1000,
seed 1):

- Implied prior on the simplex of a flat `zeta`. Softmax:
  proportional to `1 / prod(w)`, infinite mass at every face. Chart:
  `2^(1-D) prod(w)^(-1/2) sum_s (1 + sum(s sqrt(w)) / sqrt(D))^(-(D-1))`
  over the `2^D` sign sheets, verified against box-uniform draws (max
  relative bin error 1.3 percent at R = 20, D = 3); its one
  non-integrable point is the barycenter (the pole). Box-uniform
  draws at R = 100, D = 3: softmax puts 0.9985 within 0.01 of a face,
  the chart puts 0.833 within 0.05 of the barycenter.
- Weak case (`y = 0.05 x + noise`, n = 100, D = 3; ML simplex
  (0.4945, 0.5055, 0) on both arms). Base: every draw at the vertex
  (1, 0, 0), 44 divergences. Lane: every draw at the barycenter, sd 0,
  `zeta` from -4.9e17 to 1.4e18, 0 divergences, R-hat of `zeta` 1.43
  and 1.68. brms 2.23.0 (`dirichlet(1)`): mean (0.353, 0.353, 0.295),
  sd about 0.23.
- Strong case (brms_monotonic `fit1`). Base: w3 collapses to 0 (mean
  (0.694, 0.306, 0.000)), 74 divergences. Lane: mean (0.656, 0.213,
  0.131), sd (0.035, 0.044, 0.043), against brms (0.653, 0.214, 0.133)
  and (0.036, 0.044, 0.041), 0 divergences; but R-hat of `zeta` is 1.10
  and 1.37 (n_eff 24 and 7) because the chains cross the folds
  `u_j = 0` between sign sheets, while R-hat of `w` is at most 1.001.

So the lane is closer to brms where the data identify the simplex and
degenerate in a new place where they do not. The findings' sentence
"Not changed here" and the one-chain comparison (0.661/0.213/0.127
against 0.655/0.215/0.130) read as "the sampler is unchanged", which
is false. Required before merge:

1. Findings: replace "The sampler" with the measurements above.
2. NEWS: a Breaking bullet that `frm_sample()` on a `mo()` model draws
   from a different (still improper) posterior of the simplex, and
   that weakly identified simplexes now collapse to the barycenter.
3. File the follow-up with its design cost. `dirichlet(1)` plus the
   chart's log-Jacobian `(D-1) log 2 + sum log|u_j| + (D-1) log(2 / (1
   + r^2))` matches brms in w (lane weak case mean (0.366, 0.344,
   0.290), sd 0.21 to 0.24; strong (0.654, 0.213, 0.133)), but the
   Jacobian vanishes on every fold, so the target has `2^D` sheets
   separated by walls of `-Inf`: 50 divergences on the weak case and
   the two chains stuck on different sheets on the strong case (R-hat
   of `zeta` 8.47 and 4.21, of `w` 1.00). The same prior on the
   softmax is clean (0 divergences, R-hat at most 1.000). A sampler
   that wants brms parity should sample in softmax coordinates (an
   exact reparameterization from the ML point) or report diagnostics
   on `w`.

A `simo` prior row is refused on both arms by `frm()` and by
`frm_sample()`, with the same message; the lane's message drops the
word "softmax" (correct).

## Claim 7: the lost coverage can be rebuilt without mo()

`dev/optima-rev-loading2.R`: a gaussian `y ~ x + z` fit's own objective
and point, with a Hessian built as `D S D` where `S` has one eigenvalue
-0.5 along `(0, 0.022, 1, 0)` (the seed-12 loading) and 1 elsewhere,
passed to `se_tier3(fit, H, E = 0, p, exact = TRUE)`:

```text
lambda -0.5: lost z=flat | null row x: lane 0, mutant 0.00189
lambda  0.0: lost x=flat,z=flat | null row x: lane 0.0574, mutant 0.0574
```

With a negative eigenvalue, `z` is removed, `x` keeps its SE, and the
null basis has a zero in `x`'s row; the mutant without the line
`Vr[mark == "", ] <- 0` puts 0.00189 there. That is the seen-to-fail
the lost test had. (The line probe cannot confirm an ascent on a
convex objective, so the direction is marked "flat", not "concave";
the zeroing line does not care.) With an exactly zero eigenvalue the
0.022 loading is above `tau` and `x` is removed too, so the
construction needs the negative eigenvalue. A scan of 30 `cox()` and
30 two-component mixture fits (`dev/optima-rev-loading.R`) found no
natural case: no SE was lost on any of them. See m7.

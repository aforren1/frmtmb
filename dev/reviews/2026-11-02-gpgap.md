# Adversarial review of lane gpgap (round of 2026-10-29, base d5d0056d)

Reviewed: the uncommitted diff in `frmtmb-wt-gpgap`, installed in
`wt-gpgap-lib`, against `rellib-r16`. Scripts are `dev/review-gpgap-*`, logs
are in `dev/review-gpgap-out/`. brms 2.23.0 was run, not recalled.

## Verdict: FIX

The cause is right: the 94 to 1127 nats were the translator's order of
positions. Everything built on that result needs work:

- the new warning aborts a fit that the base build completes;
- the guard is silent on routes where the same floor moves the fit;
- "equivalent" reads an edited program;
- the regime given to the user for the decision is wrong;
- the sampling route carries the floor with no warning and no reason;
- the offered non-centered option at brms's absolute 1e-12 does not work when
  y has other units.

## Findings

### B1. BLOCKER: `gp_nugget_message()` aborts a valid fit

`dev/review-gpgap-offset.R`, seed 5501, logs `offset-r16.txt` and
`offset-lane.txt`.

| model | rellib-r16 | lane |
|---|---|---|
| `y ~ gp(x), sigma ~ 0 + offset(log(wsm))` | ok, ll -23.954 | ERROR: requires numeric/complex matrix/vector arguments |
| the same, noise 1e-3 | ok, ll 252.836 | the same ERROR |
| `sigma ~ 1 + offset(log(wsm))` | ok, ll -23.537 | ok, ll -23.537 |

The fit is complete when the diagnostic throws, so the user loses the fit. The
cause is that `sg[["X"]] %*% fit$estimates[[sg[["par"]]]][sg[["idx"]]]` has
no coefficient to read. No test has a sigma with no estimated coefficient.

The same line also ignores the offset. `sigma ~ 1 + offset(log(wlow))` with
`wlow = 1e-3` is a low-noise fit and gives no warning (`warn.txt`: message
reads no hit, the printed sigma 0.2342 is the coefficient alone).

Fix: read eta through the predictor's own evaluator, with the offset. A
diagnostic must not be able to abort a finished fit. Add both rows to the test.

### M1. MAJOR: "equivalent" reads an edited program; these pairs are divergences by decision

Comparing frmtmb with an edited copy of brms shows that frmtmb equals the edit.
That is useful as attribution. It is not agreement with brms's program, which
is what 2.5 defines as L1.

Standard of 2.6: a divergence is by decision when NEWS, SPEC.md,
`dev/core-boundary.md` or the ledger gives a reason. The new NEWS bullet gives
one for `frm()`, so the standard is met for `frm()`. Before this lane, no NEWS
entry gave one: the 0.51.0 line lists the divergence and no reason. p002 is the
precedent: same model, 0.4 nats, scored divergence by decision with
`agree = FALSE`.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| p022 | -5.117 | 5.7e-4 | 9000 x | 3.8e-7 | 1.1e-9 | divergence by decision |
| p023 | -1.545, net of the free 0.671: -2.216 | 2.7e-4 | 8300 x | 5.2e-6 | 3.0e-9 | divergence by decision |

| p136 | -0.5307 | 1.7e-4 | 3100 x | 2.2e-5 (non-centered, no closed form) | not read | equivalent at L4 only, weak, as at the baseline |

Numbers: `dev/review-gpgap-pairs.R`, log `pairs.txt`. All are within the L4
tolerance `1e-4 * |ll|` (5.7e-3 to 4.0e-2).

The joint gap is not a distance between the models. It is one z through two
factors, and at ordinary noise the marginals agree to 1e-9 nats. So the fits do
coincide on these five data sets. That supports "by decision, harmless here".
It does not support "equivalent", for two reasons: brms's program fails the
tolerance at all three points, and the same line produces M2 and M4 on other
data. p136 keeps L4 because L4 read brms's own posterior (z 0.164, 0.493).

The review of 2026-10-28 overturned the original "by decision" verdict because
the attribution was missing. The attribution now exists (I reproduced it), so
the old verdict is the supported one. A rewritten program adds nothing to it.

The disclosure is also thin:

- `results.tsv` gives these rows the reason "every level agrees".
- `summary.md` does not mention a rewrite: `l1_by_decision` is read by
  `equiv-flow.R` only, and `equiv-summary.R` does not use it.
- Section 2.5 still defines L1 on "brms's Stan program".
- The p023 figure "brms's own line -1.545" includes the prior's free constant.

Fix: in `eq_measure()`, a measured `const_brms` outside tolerance with the
rewritten line inside gives `divergence by decision`, `agree = FALSE`, with the
number in the reason. Keep the rewrite as the attribution check. Amend 2.5 and
"one divergence stands (p002)" in 2.6.

### M7. MAJOR: the non-centered option as offered, and the message's "no such floor", are wrong about brms

Findings section 6 offers "non-center ... at brms's nugget" and says it makes L1
a plain identity. brms's absolute 1e-12 has no scale invariance. On y times
1000 (`dev/review-gpgap-ncp.R`, `ncp2.R`, seeds 911 to 913,
`ncpsum.txt`):

| arm | y | relative convergence | at or above the closed form |
|---|---|---|---|
| absolute 1e-12 | x 1 | 16 of 18 (n = 60), 15 of 18 (n = 200) | 18, 18 |
| absolute 1e-12 | x 1000 | **0 of 18** | **0** (ll -1118 against -377) |
| relative 1e-12 | x 1 | 12 of 12 | 12 of 12 |
| relative 1e-12 | x 1000 | 9 of 12, false convergence only at noise 3e-4 and 1e-4 | 11 of 12, the other 0.013 short |

brms itself breaks there. `dev/review-gpgap-brmsunits.R`, seed 7701, 60 rows,
noise 0.1 of the signal, logs `brmsunits-1000.txt`, `brmsunits-1.txt`:

| y | chol at frm's estimates, 1e-12 | brms sigma | brms sdgp | Rhat | frm sigma, sdgp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| x 1 | ok | 0.108 | median 1.47 | sigma 1.00, sdgp 1.04 | 0.1059, 1.107 |
| x 1000 | **FAILS**, kappa 2.3e18 | 258 +- 94 (truth 100) | 478 +- 0.9 | **3.77 worst**, 267 divergent, 733 at max treedepth | 105.9, 1107 |

And brms has a floor in small units: at y times 1e-3, true sigma 3e-7, brms's
closed form reads 5.68e-11 (`floor-sum.txt`, seed 122). So "brms's nugget is
1e-12, so it has no such floor" is false. The floor is at an absolute 1e-6, in
y's units.

This is a second reason for frmtmb's relative nugget, and NEWS should give it:
frmtmb's fit does not depend on y's units and brms's does.

## Item 4: recommendation

The 6th row of the lane's table is true sigma 3e-4: false convergence, gradient
2.7e-3, ll 311.7289, which is above the closed form's 311.7284, with sdgp 4.18
against 4.22. So it is a stopping rule, not a wrong answer. On my seeds false
convergence occurs only at 3e-4 and 1e-4, and every row reaches the closed form
within 0.01 nats. The basis of the claim holds on the y scale of the lane.

"About 4 times" holds at n = 60 (0.40 s against 0.10 s). At n = 200 I read 10.8
s against 1.20 s, 9 times, with SEs on the centered side only and other lanes
loading the machine. The lane's 14 s against 3 s is 4.7 times. It grows with n.

Recommendation:

1. This round: keep 1e-6 with the warning, repaired by B1, M2, M5 and M6.
2. Next, and small: the sampling route at a relative 1e-12 (M3).
3. A round of its own, and with priority given M4: `frm()` non-centered at a
   **relative** 1e-12, not at 1e-12 absolute. Relative 1e-12 reads brms's number
   at sdgp near 1, and it does not fail in other units.

L1 would still need the attribution step, because a relative nugget is not
brms's line. Do not choose the option for "a plain identity": that identity
exists only in units where brms works. Non-centering at 1e-12 absolute is not
recommended on any data.

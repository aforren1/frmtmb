## How the release tree was built

The lanes were combined in `../frmtmb-wt-release` (branch `wt-release`
at `47cf659d`), in the order testiso, catmulti, prilabel, sampleprior,
bin2port, ndaterm, illse2, adhess, drop1raw. Every lane is on
`47cf659d`, so the base of every merge is the HEAD blob. Nothing was
committed and no git write was made: blobs are read with `git show`,
and `git merge-file` works on files, not on the index.

- `dev/rel072-merge.sh` merges in one rerunnable pass, as
  `dev/rel071-merge.sh` did: it restores each of the 121 tracked files
  any lane changed (without generated ones) to its HEAD blob, removes
  the untracked lane files an earlier run copied
  (`dev/rel072-log/copied-<lane>.lst`), merges the lanes in order with
  `git merge-file` (line endings normalized to LF: several lanes left
  CRLF working copies), copies the lanes' untracked files again (and
  stores them as LF), then runs the consolidation's edits
  (`dev/rel072-resolve.R`) and the NEWS merge
  (`dev/rel072-news-merge.R`), and stops if a conflict marker is left.
  `man/*.Rd`, `NAMESPACE`, frmtmb.sample's generated
  `helper-brms-suite.R` and `R/links-brms.R` are left out; roxygen
  writes them.
- Untracked lane files copied, 0 clashes (`dev/rel072-log/merge-2.txt`):
  32 from testiso, 61 catmulti, 38 prilabel, 87 sampleprior, 24
  bin2port, 35 ndaterm, 75 illse2, 53 adhess, 36 drop1raw. Among them
  every `dev/<lane>-*` script and findings file, `dev/reviews/*`,
  illse2's `test-se-reparam-inv.R` and `test-se-reparam-reml.R`,
  drop1raw's `R/refit-data.R` and `test-refit-data.R`. Logs, check
  trees and Stan caches are gitignored and were not copied.
- `dev/rel072-revorder.sh` merges every file two or more lanes changed
  (21) in the reverse lane order and compares
  (`dev/rel072-log/revorder.txt`; again on the final tree,
  `revorder-2.txt`): 16 are identical; the other 5 differ
  by exactly the consolidation's edits (`R/autoscale.R`, `R/confint.R`,
  `R/fit.R`, `R/methods-fit.R`, frmtmb.sample's `R/sample.R`). So no
  hunk of one lane depends on where another lane's landed.
- Interim builds go to `C:/Users/adf44/source/r/wt-release-lib`
  (`dev/rel072-install.sh <lib>`: roxygen of core, install, roxygen of

1. **A correlation lost as "nocurv" took the sds' intervals with it**

   a clamp": 176 pass, 10 fail on the merged build,
   `dev/ciharden-log/r072-ref-suite/`). Lane adhess's alone: the same
   cases on lane adhess's build give the same NA intervals, and on lane
   illse2's they do not (`dev/rel072-v29-diag.R`,
   `dev/rel072-log/v29-diag-*.txt`). Adhess's fix round 1 added
   `sdr_nan_nonpos()`, which shows a non-positive variance at a stop as
   a NaN row and column; on this test's correlation run to +1 (t =
   2790, a short stop) theta_3's raw variance is -0.0397. The lane's
   suites ran before that fix round, and its reruns after it did not
   include this file. `confint_varcorr()` forms each block's intervals
   by the delta method, and the NaN spread through the product to both
   sd rows. The fix (`R/confint.R`, `varcorr_trans_rows()`): a row that
   does not move along the NaN parameter keeps its interval from the
   rest of the block, and a row that does gets none. "Does not move" is
   judged on the central difference, against 16 ulps of the row's
   value: here the log sd of the second response moved by 2.2e-16 (one
   ulp) along the correlation's parameter (`dev/rel072-v29-diag2.R`,
   `dev/rel072-log/v29-diag2.txt`). The file passes 186 of 186 on the
   fixed build (as at 0.71.0); `test-se-exact-hessian.R` 35 of 35.
   Without a NaN parameter the computation is unchanged.

   order as brms's** (`test-draws-sd-names.R`: 11 pass, 1 fail on the
   merged build). The test expects `sd_g__Intercept, sd_g__x,
   cor_g__Intercept__x, sd_h__Intercept`; lane prilabel puts every

### b. `conditional_effects(method = "predict")`: `estimate__` is the median

  `apply(sims, 1, stats::quantile, 0.5)`, the same quantile function the
  band uses at `(1 - prob) / 2` and `1 - (1 - prob) / 2`, over the same
  simulated responses, so it is the 50% point of the band's own

  reaches it draws through `sim_response()`: a discrete family gives a

  per-category display, where `method = "predict"` is refused as
  before. frmtmb.sample's draws route reported the median already and
  is unchanged.

23 outer parameters; otherwise it waits for the first use of a
standard error, and the fit costs 3 percent or less.

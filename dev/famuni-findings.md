Three facts the brief said to verify rather than assume, all
confirmed by the Stan code:

- frechet's `mu` is the mean: the scale is `mu / tgamma(1 - 1/nu)`,
  and `nu > 1` through `logm1`.
- gen_extreme_value's `xi`: with a formula it is `expm1(eta)` (link
  `log1p`, `xi > -1`) and unconstrained by the data. Without one brms
  declares `tmp_xi` and sets `xi = scale_xi(tmp_xi, Y, mu, sigma)`,
  `inv_logit(tmp_xi) * (ub - lb) + lb` with `lb, ub` the two values
  `-1 / min(x)`, `-1 / max(x)` for `x = (y - mu) / sigma`: exactly the
  interval of `xi` that keeps every row inside the support.
  `compute_xi()` (R/rename_pars.R) writes `xi` into the draws after
  sampling.
- brms 2.23.0 marks gen_extreme_value deprecated: stancode() warns "The
  'gen_extreme_value' family is deprecated and will be removed in the
  future" (stan-predictor.R, "TODO: remove ... in brms 3.0").
- discrete_weibull: `mu` in (0, 1) on logit and a positive `shape`.
  brms does not export `discrete_weibull()`; `brmsfamily()` reaches it.

brms's post-fit functions (read in dev/brms-suite/brms/R): epred is
`mu` for frechet, `mu + sigma (gamma(1 - xi) - 1) / xi` for GEV, and
`mean_discrete_weibull()`, a series stopped at the first term below
0.001, for discrete_weibull. posterior_predict uses the quantile
functions; log_lik the densities with `log_lik_censor()` and
`log_lik_truncate()`. Discrete truncation is `lccdf(lb - 1)`
(inclusive); discrete right censoring is `lccdf(y)` (exclusive).

## 3. Decisions, and where frmtmb departs from brms

1. **Scalar `xi` takes the `log1p` link.** brms's `tmp_xi` is a change
   of variable of the same likelihood, so the maximum over the support
   is the same point. It is not reproduced because its range reaches
   below `xi = -1` wherever `-1 / max(x) < -1`, and there the GEV
   likelihood is unbounded (Smith 1985): no maximum exists. `log1p`
   holds `xi > -1`. Measured agreement with brms's scalar-`xi` fit is in
   section 6 (z = 0.101 for xi). That argument is for the maximum only.
   For the SAMPLER the support's edge is a wall in the sampled space,
   and frm_sample() diverged on it (review fix 4: 1187, 263 and 3856 of
   4000 at n = 40). Fix round 1 samples a scalar `xi` through brms's
   `tmp_xi` instead (section 12).
2. **The support.** Inside it, the density is brms's. Outside it, the
   density is 0. frmtmb writes its log as `-1e8` per unit of
   `t = 1 + xi x` below 0 (with `t` itself held at 1e-15 for the other
   terms) instead of a `NaN`. Why: a `NaN` or `-Inf` row poisons the
   gradient (`0 * NaN`), and in a `mixture()` one component that does
   not reach a row then makes the whole row `NaN`, so a GEV mixture
   could only move where every component covers every row. Measured on
   `dev/famuni-mixprobe.R` (seed 35, n = 400, data from a mixture of
   GEV(0, 1, 0.1) and GEV(6, 1, 0.1), `mixture(gev, gev)`):
   with `NaN` outside the support, nlminb stopped at "false convergence
   (8)", logLik -946.3922, with a "Large maximum absolute gradient"
   warning (57.1 at sigma2); with the slope, code 0 and logLik
   -907.6534, estimates mu1 0.106, mu2 6.001, log sigma -0.017 and
   -0.003, log1p xi 0.201 and 0.105 (`dev/famuni-out/mixprobe.txt`).
   Standalone fits did not move: the 200-seed GEV recovery table is
   identical, digit for digit, before and after the change
   (`recovery-gev-200.txt`, `recovery-gev-200-final.txt`). brms's
   density is `NaN` there, which rejects the value, so a brms GEV
   mixture holds each component over every row. This is a deliberate
   departure: a component's density outside its support is 0 by the
   definition of a mixture.
3. **The CDF outside the support** is 0 or 1 (not `NaN`, as brms's
   `pow()` gives), so a `trunc()` bound or censoring point beyond an
   end is scored. A first version clamped `z` once and rounded back to
   `-1` at `|z| > 26` (`dev/famuni-cdfprobe.R`: 16 `NaN` of 60 outside
   points); the second clamp fixed it (0 of 60).
4. **No deprecation warning.** brms's warning is about brms's plans for
   its own code, not about the model.
5. **The GEV mean** is `Inf` for `xi >= 1`, and Euler's constant times
   sigma at `xi = 0`. brms's epred returns `NaN` at `xi = 0` and at
   `xi = 1`, and `-3.029938` at `xi = 1.5` (mu 0, sigma 1;
   `brms:::posterior_epred_gen_extreme_value()` run in
   `dev/famuni-moments.R`). brms does not refuse; it returns those
   numbers. frmtmb follows the mathematics.
6. **The discrete Weibull mean** is summed to about 1e-12 relative,
   the tail beyond 1000 terms by Euler-Maclaurin. brms's series stops
   at the first term below 0.001: over 25 (mu, shape) points the
   relative difference has median 0.000977 and maximum 1 (mu 0.999,
   shape 0.3: brms 993.90, frmtmb 9.2451e10, which a 1e7-term sum
   confirms), and brms warns at 8 of the 25 (`moments.txt`).
7. **Discrete censoring** follows frmtmb's inclusive convention (right
   censoring at k is `P(Y >= k)`), as for poisson; brms's right and
   interval censoring are exclusive. Truncation agrees with brms
   (`lccdf(lb - 1)`).
8. **`mi()`** on a frechet or GEV response is not taken: frmtmb takes
   `mi()` for gaussian and student only, as for weibull, which brms
   also allows.
9. **Left-tail CDF.** The `lcdf` slot is a probability by contract, so
   `log F` underflows to `-Inf` where `F < 1e-308` (47 of 160 Frechet
   grid points, 41 of 372 GEV points), where brms's `lcdf` is finite.
   Left censoring that far into the left tail is the only path that
   reaches it; it is the same limitation every family with `lcdf` has.

## How the release tree was built

at `70476dc3`), in the brief's order: mocode, acprior, loglik. No git
write was made: blobs are read with `git show`, and `git merge-file`
works on files.

### The semantic overlaps the lanes could not test together

`dev/rel076-overlap.R` on rellib-r14 (`dev/rel076-log/overlap-1.txt`
to `-3.txt`; seeds in the script):

1. **`cosy()` with `mo()`**, `y ~ x + mo(k) + cosy(t, g)` on 30 groups
   of 4 time points, `k` in 1 to 4: `frm_sample()` (2 chains of 600)
   draws 3 `simo_mok1` weights, every `cosy` draw in brms's [0, 1] (min
   0.00068); `log_lik()` is 600 x 120, finite, and equals the dense
   conditional of each group at every draw, with the `mo()` mean
   written from brms's definition, to 8.9e-16 relative. `loo()` gives
   elpd -175.87, no Pareto k above 0.7. acprior's reviewer noted that
   `log_lik()` refused cosy before loglik; it works now.
2. **`ar(cov = TRUE)` with the innovation-sd reading of the sigma
   prior**: the sampling table lists `uniform(-1, 1)` on `ar` with -1
   and 1, and `student_t(3, 0, 2.5)` on `sigma`; the draws' `sigma` is
   the marginal sd (mean 0.814, innovation sd 0.446 from the same
   draws); `log_lik()` equals the dense AR(1) conditional with the
   marginal covariance to 2.2e-13 relative; `loo()` runs.
3. **The prior tables with `sar()`**: on both routes, `default_prior()`
   lists `b`, `b`, `Intercept`, `lagsar` and `sigma`, no `theta` row,
   `lagsar` with lb -1 and ub 1; the table passed back to `frm()` gives
   the same logLik; `frm_sample(prior = the sampling table)` then
   `log_lik()` equals the dense conditional of the lag SAR to 1.6e-13
   relative.

In arms 2 and 3 the draws under the table passed back are not the
defaults' draws. CORRECTED after the release review (finding 2): this
paragraph first said that `dev/rel076-overlap-trip.R` showed the two
Stan models' log density and gradient bitwise equal at 800 points, and
that the cause was not found. That comparison was invalid: tmbstan's
`sampling()` holds the objective in process-global pointers, so
`rstan::log_prob()` of both stanfits, called after both runs, evaluated
the second run's objective twice (now `dev/rtmb-pitfalls.md` item 23).
The reviewer's `dev/review-rel076-trip.R` evaluates each objective
right after its own run, at 500 fixed points: the log density differs
at 9 of 500, by at most 1.4e-14 (one ulp, 1.8e-16 relative), the
gradient at 0; the warmup trajectories are equal through iteration 13
and part from iteration 14. The cause is the one acprior's test names:
the two prior stacks sum their terms in another order, and the sampler
amplifies an ulp. Not a defect; the backlog item is closed. The starts
and `stan_args` are equal and each run reproduces itself, as measured.

The first two are now tests (`dev/rel076-overlap-test.R`, appended to
frmtmb.sample's `test-loglik-autocor.R` by the resolver): "cosy() and
mo() together" and "ar(cov = TRUE) under the default priors", 27
assertions (the file is 131: loglik's 104 plus 27). Seen to fail on
rellib-r13 (`r076-focus-r13`): the first fails on 3 weights (4 there),
on the cosy bound (draws below 0) and on `log_lik()`'s refusal; the
second on the refusal. The third arm is a probe only: no test file
loads frmtmb.sample and frmtmb.spatial together.

## Release fixes

`dev/rel076-release-fix.R`, sourced by the resolver, and one edit of
fact in `dev/rel076-news-split.R`:

- **R1, `?set_prior`** (acprior's review, fix round 2 check, note 2):
  the Residual correlation section adds "Under `sigma ~ x` with such a
  term, a prior on the intercept of `sigma` (`class = "Intercept", dpar
  = "sigma"`) is still read at frmtmb's log marginal sd, where brms
  reads it at the log innovation sd; the slopes (`class = "b", dpar =
  "sigma"`) are the same in both." The slopes are the same quantity
  because `log c` is a shift that does not depend on `x`, as the
  reviewer's `r2-brmssigx.txt` shows.
- **frmtmb.sample's NEWS headline** now reads "Every prior on class
  `sigma` is read at the innovation sd, as brms's is."
- **R2, `R/influence.R`** (mocode's review, fix round 2 check, nit 4):
  the comment above `dfbetas.frmtmb_influence()` names the vector
  `dfbeta()` reads, `interop_coef_vector(simplex = FALSE)`, without the
  simplex that `get_coef()` carries for marginaleffects.
- **R3, `test-mo-coding.R`** (the same nit): the marginaleffects bound
  was `max(abs(se / ex - 1)) < 1e-4`. It is now `1e-4 * max(abs(se0 /
  ex - 1))`, where `se0` is the standard error without the simplex
  (0.75.0's) from the basis and covariance of core's delta method,
  which the test first checks gives core's own standard error. Measured
  (`dev/rel076-me-probe.R`, `me-probe.txt`, seed 5 of the file's data):
  `se0` is 0.0846 and 0.1035 at k = 2 and 3 against the exact 0.1220
  and 0.1142, a distance of 0.306; marginaleffects' error is at most
  1.09e-7. The file is 122 assertions (mocode's 120 plus 2).

### The semantic overlap: `mvbf()` with rescor and `me()`

The one model both code lanes reach (named by both reviews). Probe
`dev/rel078-overlap.R` on rellib-r16 (`overlap-2.txt`; data seed 29, 60
rows, `mvbf(bf(y ~ me(xo, sx)), bf(y2 ~ me(xo, sx) + me(zo, sz))) +
set_rescor(TRUE)`, sampler seed 5, 2 chains of 800):

- brms 2.23.0's `default_prior()` (no compile) has the me() and rescor
  rows `corme lkj(1)`, `meanme`, `meanme mexo`, `meanme mezo`, `rescor
  lkj(1)`, `sdme lb = 0`, `sdme mexo`, `sdme mezo`, none with a `resp`;
  frmtmb's `default_prior(route = "sample")` has the same eight rows,
  with frmtmb's `(flat)` for brms's `""`.
- `frm_sample(sample_prior = "yes")` with `normal(0, 2)` on `meanme`,
  `exponential(1)` on `sdme` and `lkj(2)` on `corme` samples 146
  parameters; the prior draws are `Intercept_y sigma_y Intercept_y2
  sigma_y2 rescor meanme_1 sdme_1 Lme_1`; the draws carry
  `corme__mexo__mezo` (mean -0.0673) and `rescor__y__y2` (mean 0.5804).
- `posterior_predict()` is 800 x 60 x 2 with responses `y`, `y2`. Per

  predictive residuals is 0.5706 on average against the draws' rescor

  `overlap-1.txt`, read a per-draw sample correlation, 0.5722, z -2.68:
  that statistic is biased by about rho (1 - rho^2) / (2n), so the test
  reads the unbiased product.)

  relative difference of 0; `log_lik()` equals the bivariate normal at
  the draw's rescor to 6.3e-16, and differs from the sum of the two

## Release fixes

`dev/rel078-release-fix.R`, sourced by the resolver. Test blocks it
appends are `dev/rel078-fix-*-test.R`.

- **R1 (meme's review, short check of fix round 2, S3), the two
  spellings of a nonlinear intercept.** On `a ~ 1`,
  `set_prior(class = "Intercept", nlpar = "a")` (pA) is rewritten by
  `refuse_coef_bounds()` as brms's class row, and with
  `set_prior(class = "b", nlpar = "a")` (pB) beside it the later of the
  two stood. frm() resolves the pair by specificity in either order
  (`dev/rel078-r1-frm.R`, `r1-frm.txt`, 8 cases in both orders, the
  same on rellib-r15 and rellib-r16): the coefficient spelling's density
  wins (an empty pA leaves pB's), and per side its bound wins (pA lb 1
  and pB lb 6 give 1; pA lb 6 and pB lb 1 give 6; a bound on one row
  alone is taken). `refuse_coef_bounds()` now merges the pair that way:
  pA's density, else pB's; per side pA's bound, else pB's. The fit's own
  prior and the call's `prior =` are stacked before it, so a fit's pA
  and the call's pB merge the same way, as brms's `update(prior =)`
  keeps a coefficient row under a new class row. The reviewer's case
  (`dev/review-meme-sc2.R`, run on rellib-r16 from a copy,
  `sc2-merged.txt`): both orders resolve `a_(Intercept):normal(5,0.1)`;
  the posterior mean of `b_a_Intercept` under pA + pB is 4.833 (lane
  0.470, rellib-r15 4.833) and from a fit's pA with the call's pB 4.832
  (lane 0.468, rellib-r15 4.832); S1's bound still reaches the prior
  draws (minima 0.0181 and 0.4025, as the review records). Tests in
  `test-prior-nlpar-intercept.R` (3 blocks, 25 assertions): five cases
  (densities only, a bound on the class row, on the intercept row, on
  both, and a bounds-only intercept row) resolve the same in both orders
  and as frm() resolves them, density and lower bound; frm()'s fit is
  the same in both orders and as pA alone; and sampled, a fit's pA with
  the call's pB gives the draws of pA alone, and pA + pB, pB + pA and pA
  alone give the same draws. Seen to fail on meme's library
  (`seenfail-2.txt`): 17 failures, all in the new blocks (lines 166 to
  176, the order and specificity block; 208, 218 and 219, the sampled
  block); frm()'s block passes there, as frm() did not change. On
  rellib-r15 the new blocks pass, since the pair resolved by specificity
  there (its 9 failures are meme's S1 blocks). NEWS: a frmtmb.sample
  bullet (breaking changes).
